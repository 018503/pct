## [2026-08-15] analysis | Farigiraf matchup calcs

## [2026-08-14] analysis | Meowscarada Protean coverage calcs

Ran Protean Meowscarada damage calcs against Reg M-B Charizard Y and Mega Staraptor wiki builds using Showdown Champions calculator JS API. Tested Power Gem (special, Rock), Triple Axel (physical, Ice), and Thunder Punch (physical, Electric) across multiple natures, items, and stat point spreads.

- **Optimal set**: Naive Life Orb, 32 Atk / 0 SpA / 32 Spe — guaranteed OHKOs every Zard Y build with Power Gem (uninvested SpA 101 is enough at 4x SE) and every Mega Staraptor build at +0 Def with Triple Axel
- **Key finding**: Charizard Y's 4x Rock weakness makes SpA investment unnecessary, freeing all offensive points for Atk to handle Staraptor's 2x Ice weakness
- Power Gem and Thunder Punch are ineffective vs Mega Staraptor; Triple Axel is the only viable OHKO option
- At +1 Def (Contrary Close Combat), Triple Axel still rolls for the OHKO on most builds
- Created [meowscarada-protean-calcs.md](teambuilds/meowscarada-protean-calcs.md) with full calc tables
