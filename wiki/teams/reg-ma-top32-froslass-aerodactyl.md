## Strategy

This dual mega team pairs Froslass and Aerodactyl, with Aurora Veil on Mega Froslass as the centerpiece. Aerodactyl complements Froslass nicely by covering for Charizard, which would otherwise give Froslass a lot of trouble. A trend across this whole tournament is players putting a lot of bulk on Froslass and Sneasler, and this team is no different — arguably the biggest takeaway from the top teams here is that super-defensive Sneasler is rapidly rising in popularity across many compositions.

This particular Froslass, though, still leans more offensive than most: rather than the near-zero Special Attack, minimal Speed spreads seen elsewhere, this one runs close to max Special Attack and max Speed with just a little HP investment. Aurora Veil is what ties it together, because the rest of the team — Aerodactyl, Sneasler, Garchomp — is already very bulky by default, and Aurora Veil on top makes all of them even harder to break through. As with several other teams from this event, Basculegion runs Focus Sash rather than Choice Scarf, giving it room to operate against Kingambit rather than getting locked into a losing Sucker Punch endgame.

Overall this is a team of format staples executed well — a Sitrus Berry Garchomp with Aurora Veil support in particular can feel extremely annoying to face, since it sustains for a long time and opponents struggle to knock it out quickly enough.
