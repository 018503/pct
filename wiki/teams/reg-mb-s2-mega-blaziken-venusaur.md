## Strategy

Dual mega starters that aren't Charizard — Mega Blaziken for fast-paced aggression, Mega Venusaur for Aurora Veil-amplified bulk. Blaziken has Speed Boost even without mega evolving, making it fundamentally strong in non-mega mode.

Alolan Ninetales sets Aurora Veil to make the already bulky Venusaur-Mega a nightmare. Farigiraf is notably more physically defensive (Bold, 134 Def) unlike most SpD-focused variants — Helping Hand from Farigiraf amplifies damage for key OHKOs.

Basculegion has zero speed investment (98 Spe) — doesn't need it since Aurora Veil provides bulk and Trick Room lets it outpace threats. Under Trick Room, this Basculegion outspeeds even more targets. Kingambit's speed is set exactly 1 point faster than Farigiraf, so without Trick Room Kingambit moves first for KOs, and in Trick Room Farigiraf moves first to provide Helping Hand.
