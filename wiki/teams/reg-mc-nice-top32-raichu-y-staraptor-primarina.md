## Strategy

The **Mega Raichu Y + Mega Staraptor** pairing was popular heading into Worlds. This is a newer version, built by Michael Kelsch and taken to top 32 at Nice by schianchi_luca. Either can be the mega, and bringing both still has value. Regular Raichu redirects with **Lightning Rod** (against Raichu and Archaludon), and regular Staraptor still gives **Intimidate + Tailwind**. **Leftovers Calm Mind Primarina** and **Life Orb Nasty Plot Gholdengo** take over games once boosted. Rillaboom and Focus Sash Hisuian Arcanine add raw damage, and Arcanine matches up well into much of the format.

You can play **setup** (Rillaboom + Gholdengo, Rillaboom + Primarina, or Raichu with either) or **fast**: Staraptor Tailwind, under which most of the team outpaces everything except ultra-fast Pokemon. Primarina is slower. Under Tailwind it beats Mega Delphox, but not the Pokemon faster than that. Watch for opposing Tailwind and Choice Scarf Trick Room.
