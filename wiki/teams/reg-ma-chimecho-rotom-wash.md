## Strategy

Mega Chimecho is one of the lowest usage rate Pokemon right now, but it still has a lot of potential! It's a great Trick Room setter with access to moves like Flash Cannon, Helping Hand, and Boomburst. Boomburst is one of the strongest attacks in the game, and when paired with Kommo-O and Chandelure, you can quickly knock out your opponents. This team also features fun sets like Soft Sand Mudsdale!
