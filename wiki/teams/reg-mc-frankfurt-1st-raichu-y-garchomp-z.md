## Strategy

Eric Rios won the Frankfurt Regional Championships undefeated (17-0) with this dual-mega team. Mega Raichu Y and Mega Garchomp Z are two of the fastest special attackers in the game, backed by three Fake Out users (Raichu, Rillaboom, Incineroar), a Tailwind and Rage Powder Volcarona, and a Nasty Plot Gholdengo. Cybertron calls it one of the most flexible teams in the format.

Volcarona is the unsung MVP: Timid with enough Speed to outpace max Speed Hisuian Arcanine, bulky enough to take Rock Slides, and Rocky Helmet breaks Sashes. Overheat hits Kingambit and Rillaboom while Struggle Bug blunts special attackers. Garchomp Z runs Dragon Pulse over Draco Meteor so it can stay in, and its Earth Power / Power Gem coverage hits Charizard and Volcarona even without mega evolving. Raichu is a Timid set with Speed investment that outspeeds max Speed Mega Froslass and Sneasler with the rest dumped into bulk.

Leads and modes: Rillaboom + Garchomp with Incineroar and Volcarona behind for Mega Garchomp mirrors; Volcarona + Garchomp (Eric's lead in the finals) for slower Trick Room teams; Incineroar + Gholdengo for Fake Out into Nasty Plot; or Raichu + Gholdengo. Watch for Farigiraf's Armor Tail blocking Fake Out. Weak to fairy types, so beat those early with Gholdengo before bringing Garchomp.
