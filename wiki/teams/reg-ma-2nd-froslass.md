## Strategy

This is the team that finished in 2nd place, built around Mega Froslass and a lot of surprisingly bulky Pokemon. Milotic and Kingambit are already hard to knock out, and Aurora Veil makes them tougher still. Froslass itself carries almost no Special Attack investment, running heavy HP and Defense instead, while Sneasler (White Herb, Unburden) skips Attack and Speed investment entirely — Unburden triggers so reliably that it still outpaces the format afterward, and the extra bulk lets it tank things opponents never expect, like Dual Wingbeat from Aerodactyl or Earthquake from Garchomp.

Kingambit runs Speed investment here to capitalize on Tailwind from Dragonite and Icy Wind from Milotic. This Dragon Fang / Dragon Claw / Extreme Speed / Low Kick Dragonite set picked up in popularity out of this tournament — bulky enough to survive hits while still hitting hard, with Tailwind (sometimes swapped for Protect) helping Basculegion and Kingambit outspeed opponents. Milotic's Life Dew keeps the team topped up alongside Aurora Veil.

The Basculegion here runs Focus Sash rather than the more common Choice Scarf, specifically to beat opposing Kingambit — it survives a Sucker Punch and retaliates with Liquidation (chosen over Wave Crash to avoid recoil) into Last Respects. Overall the team leans on unexpected bulk across Sneasler, Froslass, Dragonite, and Kingambit, backed by Aurora Veil and Life Dew healing, to outlast opponents before overwhelming them with offense.
