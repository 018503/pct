## Strategy

The team that made sand super meta in MB. Initially covered as an anti-meta pick but became one of the standard meta teams as players realized its strength. Multiple players reached Champion tier with this composition.

Tyranitar-Mega provides sand for Excadrill's Sand Rush while being a powerful mega evolution itself. Milotic with Competitive punishes Intimidate users. Sinistcha is a defensive Bold variant with Colbur Berry (survives dark attacks) providing redirection and Hospitality healing.

Staraptor-Mega here is built almost purely for speed and bulk (189 HP / only 161 Atk) — it functions more as a Tailwind setter and Intimidate provider than a primary attacker. Gholdengo with Nasty Plot can sweep behind setup.

The metagame is still evolving around this team — Ice Beam over Haze on Milotic, Low Kick on Tyranitar, High Horsepower on Excadrill, Jolly vs Adamant Excadrill, and Choice Scarf Tyranitar (with solo Mega Staraptor) are all adaptations being explored.
