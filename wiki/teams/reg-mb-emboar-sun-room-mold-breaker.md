## Strategy

James Baek's attempt to give Mega Emboar's otherwise pointless Mold Breaker a job: Mold Breaker Helping Hand bypasses Good as Gold, so Emboar can boost a Choice Scarf Gholdengo for surprise nukes with Steel Beam and Make It Rain. It also doubles as a bulky attacker with Flare Blitz and Close Combat under the sun.

The team is a sun-room hybrid: Torkoal sets Drought, Vileplume's Chlorophyll plus After You lets slow Emboar act first, and Farigiraf sets Trick Room with Armor Tail protection. Mamoswine (Oblivious, so it ignores Intimidate) with Ice Shard one-shots Garchomp, which the Gholdengo + Emboar core struggled with.

Min-Speed Emboar and Farigiraf take advantage of Trick Room, and Torkoal's Stone Edge exists purely for Charizard Y. A flexible team with a lot of damage output; Timid Gholdengo outspeeds Mega Aerodactyl and Scarf Basculegion.
