## Strategy

James Baek's own Mega Altaria team: Cloud Nine before mega keeps sand from chipping or boosting the wrong Pokemon, then Pixelate turns Double-Edge into a very strong Fairy STAB. It sets up with Dragon Dance and recovers with Roost, with Coaching from Sneasler and Sinistcha's redirection and Hospitality healing to stay alive through Double-Edge recoil.

The rest of the team covers Altaria's weaknesses (Ice, Steel, Poison, Fairy): Excadrill's Ground and Steel STAB hits all of them, and Tyranitar's Sand Stream with Rock Slide handles Charizard Y, which otherwise is awkward when Altaria loses its Flying type. Sneasler's Focus Sash is fine here because Cloud Nine Altaria prevents sand chip when it is on the field. Milotic's Competitive punishes Intimidate and other stat drops aimed at the sweepers, which scares away Intimidate leads.

Typical modes: Sneasler + Altaria or Sneasler + Excadrill, with Coaching and Fake Out to set up. Tyranitar is the second mega option, mainly to answer Charizard and Kingambit (Low Kick). The team is built to be good into Charizard teams.
