## Strategy

Mega Delphox won a ~300 person tournament the night after Pokemon Champions released! As one of the fastest attackers in the game, it has the ability to take quick knock-outs. Surrounded by other strong options like Swords Dance Kingambit, Scarf Basculegion, and Lum Berry Dragonite, the team is incredibly well balanced.
