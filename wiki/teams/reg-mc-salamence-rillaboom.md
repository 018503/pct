## Strategy

This team won a 150+ player online tournament in the first week of Regulation MC, piloted by SableyeVGC (a four-time Worlds qualifier). Mega Salamence serves as both the primary damage dealer and speed control setter, running a mixed set with Hyper Voice and Double-Edge — the same archetype that won the 2019 World Championships. The core idea is that Salamence pressures with massive flying STAB from both the physical and special side while Aerilate amplifies Hyper Voice into a devastating spread move, and as Salamence is about to faint, it clicks Tailwind to set up the endgame sweepers. Intimidate before mega evolving also helps neutralize opposing physical threats on turn one.

The supporting cast is loaded with raw offensive power. Hisuian Arcanine provides recoilless coverage via Rock Head (Flare Blitz and Head Smash hit steel and flying types that wall Salamence), while Focus Sash ensures it survives to fire off at least one attack. Basculegion closes games under Tailwind with Adaptability-boosted Wave Crash and Last Respects, the latter scaling to absurd damage as teammates fall. Kingambit brings Defiant to punish opposing Intimidate users and four attacks with Chople Berry for fighting-type coverage survivability. Sneasler offers fast Fake Out support with Unburden activation off Rillaboom's Grassy Seed, and Dire Claw threatens poison/paralysis/sleep against bulky targets. Rillaboom sets Grassy Terrain for chip healing and Grassy Glide priority while providing a key terrain war tool against Psychic Terrain Indeedee teams.

The team's main weakness is heavy physical skew — bulky physical walls like Archaludon (Stamina) and Mega Golisopod can be difficult to break through without precise positioning of Sneasler's Close Combat or Basculegion's Last Respects in the endgame. Faster opposing Mega Salamence with Draco Meteor can be problematic, and the team has no Trick Room answer outside of raw aggression. Archaludon specifically requires careful Sneasler conservation — if Sneeasler goes down before getting Close Combat onto Archaludon, the matchup becomes nearly unwinnable. The Salamence speed benchmark of 185 outspeeds max-speed Whimsicott, which is critical for maintaining offensive tempo.

| SpD | 90 | 0 | 99 |
| Spe | 120 | 29 | 185 |

**Moves:** Hyper Voice · Double-Edge · Tailwind · Protect

**Role:** Mixed lead sweeper + Tailwind support

Salamence is the engine of the team, threatening knockouts from both attack stats simultaneously. Hyper Voice hits both opponents as an Aerilate-boosted spread move, punishing teams without strong flying resistances — and many teams in the early MC metagame lack them. Double-Edge provides single-target burst that specifically OHKOs Grassy Seed Sneasler after its Defense boost (the reason for 21 Attack investment). The 29 Speed points reach 185, outspeeding max-speed Whimsicott to prevent it from getting free Tailwinds or Encores. Naive nature over Jolly sacrifices SpD to boost both Attack and SpA output. Tailwind is what makes this set uniquely dangerous — even when Salamence is about to faint, it converts its last turn into speed control that enables Arcanine and Basculegion to sweep in the endgame. The pre-mega Intimidate is enormous for softening physical attackers on turn one before Salamence mega-evolves.

**Moves:** Wood Hammer · Grassy Glide · U-turn · Fake Out

**Role:** Terrain setter + pivot + priority attacker

Rillaboom brings several critical roles to this team. Grassy Surge activates Sneasler's Grassy Seed for Unburden, provides passive healing for the entire team, and wins terrain wars against Psychic Terrain Indeedee (which would otherwise block Fake Out and enable Expanding Force spam). The 113 Speed outspeeds the entire format under Tailwind. This Rillaboom is more offensively invested than typical builds — Wood Hammer chunks even resisted targets, and with Miracle Seed (noted as an upgrade option) it OHKOs Mega Gardevoir without Grassy Terrain. U-turn provides safe pivoting to preserve momentum when the matchup calls for repositioning. Fake Out gives turn-one disruption. Sitrus Berry offers longevity, though Miracle Seed for extra damage is a viable alternative.

**Moves:** Dire Claw · Close Combat · Fake Out · Protect

**Role:** Fast Fake Out + Archaludon counter

Sneasler is the team's primary answer to Archaludon, which otherwise walls most of the team. Close Combat two-shots Archaludon even through a Stamina boost, and the team's win condition in the Archaludon matchup revolves around preserving Sneasler for a clean endgame angle. With Rillaboom's Grassy Seed consumed, Unburden doubles Sneasler's speed to 340, outspeeding the entire metagame. Even without Unburden active, 170 Speed is fast enough to outpace most unboosted threats, which is important since Cybertron often led Sneasler without Rillaboom. Dire Claw threatens 30% status affliction on contact, creating variance that punishes bulky switch-ins. The 16 HP and 20 Atk hit optimal stat thresholds. Against rain teams, Sneasler leads with Salamence for Fake Out + Tailwind, with Basculegion sweeping from the back.

**Moves:** Flare Blitz · Head Smash · Extreme Speed · Protect

**Role:** Anti-steel coverage + priority closer

Hisuian Arcanine paired with Salamence is the default lead — Salamence's Intimidate softens opposing physical attackers while Arcanine threatens steel types that wall Salamence's flying STAB. Rock Head eliminates recoil from both Flare Blitz and Head Smash, making it functionally indestructible offensively. Head Smash at 150 base power hits everything that doesn't resist it enormously — including Mega Charizard Y switch-ins and opposing flying types. Focus Sash guarantees at least one attack gets through even against super-effective hits. Extreme Speed provides +2 priority for picking off weakened threats, bypassing Tailwind and Trick Room entirely. Max Attack and max Speed with Jolly nature ensures it hits hard and fast. The team's key late-game pattern is Salamence faint → Tailwind → Arcanine or Basculegion sweep.

**Moves:** Kowtow Cleave · Iron Head · Low Kick · Sucker Punch

**Role:** Anti-Intimidate punisher + four-attack wallbreaker

Kingambit runs four attacks with no Protect, trading defensive utility for maximum coverage. Defiant punishes opposing Intimidate users — if they lead Salamence or Incineroar into Kingambit, the +2 Attack boost often results in an immediate KO. Kowtow Cleave is 100% accurate Dark STAB that bypasses Protect, Iron Head provides Steel STAB with flinch chance, Low Kick hits opposing Kingambit and heavy steel types, and Sucker Punch gives priority for closing games. Chople Berry survives one fighting-type attack, buying a crucial turn. The main downside is only 71 Speed — even under Tailwind, Kingambit still gets outsped by a fair portion of the format. The 1 Speed point is a micro-optimization for same-speed tiebreakers. Max HP at 207 combined with 142 Defense and 111 SpD makes Kingambit surprisingly tanky.

**Moves:** Wave Crash · Last Respects · Aqua Jet · Protect

**Role:** Endgame closer + Tailwind sweeper

Basculegion is the team's primary late-game win condition. Adaptability doubles STAB moves to 2x instead of 1.5x, making Wave Crash hit like a truck. Last Respects scales with each fainted teammate, becoming devastating in the endgame — after two or three teammates fall, it one-shots nearly anything. Aqua Jet provides +1 priority for finishing weakened threats and sniping around Sucker Punch mindgames. Mystic Water boosts water moves without recoil (the original team had Life Orb, which is higher damage but costs HP alongside Wave Crash recoil). The 13 Defense investment specifically helps survive non-Life Orb Kingambit's Sucker Punch. The 124 Speed is awkward against opposing Basculegion mirrors — the creator noted you often just have to assume you're slower. Under Tailwind from Salamence, 124 Speed outspeeds the entire unboosted metagame.
