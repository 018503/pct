## Strategy

James faced this team on the ladder with very little information (Metagross Explosion, Medicham Trick Room and Feint, Golurk Earthquake) and rebuilt it. The plan is to lead Medicham with Choice Scarf Metagross: Metagross Explosion gets rid of an attacker while Medicham sets Trick Room (Feint breaks Protect), and the free switch brings in Mega Golurk. The original team's Medicham had Telepathy so Golurk's Earthquake would not hit it; this paste uses Pure Power Medicham, so Golurk's Earthquake is not used next to it.

Closed team sheet bluffs are the point: Floette (Mega-capable) and a Tailwind-looking Whimsicott make opponents expect Tailwind, while in reality Floette is a Light of Ruin Mega bluff and Whimsicott is a Trick Room/Encore/Memento backup. Choice Scarf Metagross carries Stone Edge so it can delete Charizard Y on the lead, Iron Head for Fairies and Ice Punch for Garchomp.

In the ladder test the Metagross + Medicham lead set Trick Room against Staraptor/Maushold and Golurk Poltergeist and Feint cleaned up. James notes Golurk should really be Brave (the build is Adamant by mistake) and has no Protect because it is only meant to clean up during Trick Room turns.
