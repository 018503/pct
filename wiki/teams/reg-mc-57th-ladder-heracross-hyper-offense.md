## Strategy

Same team as the CybertronVGC Mega Heracross video (same rental code, P9S5X48KEN). James's paste lists **Protect** over Detect on Heracross and a **Timid** Glimmora (Cybertron's is Modest). Jhin0213 finished **57th** last ladder season. Mega Heracross loses a little speed when it mega evolves but becomes one of the strongest physical attackers in the game. James's calcs: Close Combat OHKOs **Chople Berry Kingambit**, Bullet Seed breaks Sash Basculegion in rain, and Rock Blast handles Charizard and Chople Incineroar. If it stays un-mega'd, a KO gives a Moxie +1.

It is a hyper offence team with two speed modes. **Tailwind**: Whimsicott sets it, and Incineroar, Glimmora and Heracross deal the damage. Glimmora's Toxic Debris spikes chip toward a Heracross / Incineroar endgame. Leading Incineroar + Whimsicott or Heracross + Whimsicott both work, and the offensive Incineroar catches opponents off guard. **Trick Room**: Farigiraf + Incineroar is the lead, with Drampa as the slow sweeper. Farigiraf's **Imprison** blocks the opponent's Trick Room once your Tailwind is up, and it blanks Gardevoir psyspam.
