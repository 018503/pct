## Strategy

Joseph Ugarte won the Baltimore Regional Championships, the first official Regulation MC event (nearly 1,100 players), with this sand dual-mega team and it also made Top 16 at Frankfurt. Mega Salamence and Mega Tyranitar can each be the mega of the day, and the team frequently brings both. Cybertron stresses the stats are marcofiero's recreation, so they are very close to but not identical to Joe's.

Everything is fast and offensive: Salamence and Tyranitar are both max Speed, so Tyranitar can Knock Off before many base-100ish Pokemon move and Salamence at worst ties other Salamence. Salamence runs Flamethrower over Tailwind to beat steel walls (Gholdengo, Kingambit, Mega Golisopod, the last relevant in the Baltimore finals). Sneasler carries Coaching instead of Fake Out to boost Tyranitar, Corviknight and Excadrill, and Choice Scarf Indeedee uses Protect or Trick to buy a turn or cripple Trick Room setters.

Common modes: Sneasler + Corviknight (Coaching into double Bulk Up); Sneasler + Tyranitar or Excadrill with Salamence in the back (as Joe did in the Baltimore finals, holding the mega for a few turns); or Salamence + Indeedee for fast Hyper Voice + Expanding Force spread damage. Weaknesses: very little bulk, so a single well-placed super effective hit KOs most of the team (Indeedee, Salamence, Sneasler), and priority attackers are a problem. Rillaboom (which overwrites Indeedee's terrain) and Competitive/Defiant Pokemon (which punish Intimidate) are awkward matchups.
