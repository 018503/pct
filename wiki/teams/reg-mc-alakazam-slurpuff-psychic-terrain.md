## Strategy

James's recreation of a ladder team built around Mega Alakazam, Slurpuff and Sirfetch'd. He replaced the original Kingambit with Life Orb Garchomp for Volcarona, Incineroar and opposing dragons. Mega Alakazam is one of the strongest special attackers in Champions but very frail. Psychic Surge Indeedee returning in Reg MC gives it spread Expanding Force. Magic Guard before mega means only direct attacks hurt it; after mega, Trace can copy something great (Intimidate, Adaptability) or something bad (Drizzle, Grassy Surge).

The gimmick is Slurpuff. Its Psychic Seed is consumed in Psychic Terrain (+1 SpD), which triggers Unburden and doubles its Speed. It then uses After You to make a slow Torkoal move next for a sun Eruption, or Fake Tears so Alakazam's Expanding Force or Focus Blast nukes the target. Indeedee-F's Eject Button lets it pivot out after taking a hit, so it can come back later to reset Psychic Terrain.

How to use it: Alakazam + Indeedee are brought most games. The aim is to keep terrain up and use Follow Me, Helping Hand and Fake Tears so Alakazam lands KOs. Garchomp and Torkoal are the other main damage; Sirfetch'd handles Incineroar and Kingambit and helps Torkoal into Rillaboom. Trick Room (on Indeedee) is a possible late-game mode but hard to set up because Eject Button pulls Indeedee out. James called it not the most serious team.
