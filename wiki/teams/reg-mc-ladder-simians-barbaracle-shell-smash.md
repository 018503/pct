## Strategy

SwapDogVGC took six of the least-used Pokemon in Champions (the three Unova monkeys, Gourgeist-Large, Samurott and Mega Barbaracle) into the top 100 of Champion tier. The win condition is a Shell Smash Mega Barbaracle. The rest of the team gives it a safe turn to set up. Simisear's Fake Out and Yawn, Samurott's Encore, and Gourgeist's Ally Switch (used as pseudo-redirection) all serve that goal.

Each monkey covers a hole. Choice Scarf Simipour outspeeds and pressures Garchomp and Mega Salamence, which would otherwise be very hard for the team. Coba Berry Simisage brings Sunny Day, because without it rain teams (Raichu or Golisopod rain) would crush the team. Sash Simisear provides Fake Out, Yawn, Blaze Kick and Rock Slide. Samurott's Upper Hand stops Rillaboom's Grassy Glide. Gourgeist-Large walls some end games with Leech Seed and Will-O-Wisp.

On stream, game 1 against Maushold/Whimsicott/Gholdengo was won with Ally Switch dodging a double-up on Barbaracle and a Rock Slide KO on Whimsicott. Gholdengo is a huge problem, since the team has almost nothing for it besides Blaze Kick. James's verdict: "it's actually very usable". You have to make a lot of hard reads and go for risky, inaccurate moves, but the team has real tools to win.
