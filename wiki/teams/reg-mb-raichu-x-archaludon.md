## Strategy

The Pokemon Company reached out during the beginning of Regulation MB and asked if I could build a team around one of the new Mega Evolutions! I decided to pick Raichu X and see how I could make it work best in the format... it turns out that physical Mega Raichu X is an amazing partner for Archaludon, threatening its biggest threats with OHKOes and setting up Electric Terrain for stronger Electro Shots!
