## Strategy

A themed team from a Reddit post of slow, shell-wearing Pokemon that reached Champions tier on the Reg MB ladder. James Baek does not give a normal breakdown because it is a theme team, but calls it more viable than it looks. The main mode is Trick Room with Galarian Slowking, Fake Out Mega Blastoise and the slow bruisers Torkoal, Appletun and Avalugg.

Appletun's Apple Acid lowers Special Defense before Eruption or Blastoise's attacks. Avalugg is one of the best defensive Pokemon around, with Wide Guard support, and Body Press hits Kingambit hard thanks to its Defense stat. The backup mode is Torterra: Blastoise Fake Out into Torterra Shell Smash, then Rock Slide threatens Charizard, which is how the team beats it.

Typical leads: Blastoise + Slowking for Trick Room, or Blastoise + Torterra for the Shell Smash mode. The team is weak into Trick Room denial (Whimsicott) and fast priority, but its bulk lets it take hits and fire back.
