## Strategy

The highest-placing Mega Absol Z team from the Global Challenge, 65th worldwide. Mega Absol Z has one of the lowest win rates of any mega because it is paper thin, but it is one of the fastest Pokemon in the format. This team uses that Speed for Icy Wind, which slows Mega Salamence and Garchomp Z so Timid Life Orb Kommo-o can Clanging Scales them. Opponents often assume they survive an Absol hit and outspeed Kommo-o, and Icy Wind catches them out.

Absol, Kommo-o, Armarouge and Metagross are all max Speed / max offence. Mega Metagross is the second mega because Absol and Kommo-o have almost nothing into fairy types. The creator's report says that even though the team was built around Absol + Kommo-o, they used Metagross more, and it is the more consistent of the two. Absol is great in the right matchup and dead weight in the wrong one. Indeedee-F (max HP / Defense) and Drampa (max HP / SpA, Quiet) are the slow pieces: Drampa's Cloud Nine shuts off weather and it works under Trick Room.

Main modes: Absol + Kommo-o into fast dragons, with Indeedee and Armarouge in the back; Indeedee + Metagross into fairies or weather (Drampa in the back); Absol + regular Metagross; and Indeedee + Armarouge Expanding Force spam into teams that can't stop it. Avoid that last lead into dark types, Rillaboom or Wide Guard. Weaknesses: it is hyper offence, so a wrong lead is hard to recover from, with little bulk to switch around (example: Absol + Kommo-o into a Grimmsnarl + Garchomp lead). It also struggles against Trick Room when Indeedee or Drampa isn't brought.
