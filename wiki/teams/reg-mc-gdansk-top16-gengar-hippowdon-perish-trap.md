## Strategy

Marco Fiero placed top 16 at Gdansk (and 14th in Global Challenge II) with this exact paste. Cybertron only made a rental code for it. It is a solo-mega **Shadow Tag Mega Gengar** team with two win routes. **Perish Song** is the first, with Fake Out cover from Rillaboom and Incineroar (the "Sinistcha" in the transcript is Incineroar). **Raw damage** is the second: Grassy Seed Unburden Sneasler moves before almost anything, and Swords Dance Life Orb Kingambit adds offence. The new piece is **Hippowdon**. It turns opposing weather into sand, and Yawn + Shadow Tag traps a target and puts it to sleep. Stone Edge is for Mega Charizard Y, Volcarona and Mega Salamence.

Each game asks one question: open with Perish because the opponent has no ghosts or pivots, or lean on offence and close with Perish later? Rillaboom (Occa) and Incineroar (Chople) both run Protect, so they drop Wood Hammer and a Dark move. Cybertron rates it one of the hardest teams in the video to pilot. One mistake with Perish loses, and this Gengar is far slower than opponents (and you) expect.
