## Strategy

I've been on a mission to feature a strong team with every brand new Mega Evolution introduced in Regulation MB. I only have two remaining, and in this video, I check another one off the list... Mega Dragalge is an interesting Mega Evolution because it has two fantastic abilities before and after Mega Evolution, can soak up damage against the format's most popular Mega Evolution, and can threaten lots of common Pokemon in the format with super effective damage.
