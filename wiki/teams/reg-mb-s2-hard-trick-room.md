## Strategy

Built by Johnny, one of the best players from Taiwan, who brought hard Trick Room to Worlds last year (Day 2, 35th place). Dual mega pairing of Kangaskhan and Charizard-Y.

Kangaskhan + Farigiraf or Hatterene gives immediate Fake Out into Trick Room. Charizard provides a secondary mega that functions outside of Trick Room with its speed investment (137 Spe). Double weather control with Torkoal + Charizard makes it very hard for opponents to deny sun.

Incineroar runs White Herb to ignore Intimidate and reset Close Combat drops — max Atk makes it a serious offensive threat rather than pure support. Hatterene with Life Orb and Magic Bounce shuts down opposing Trick Room while dealing massive damage (206 SpA). Charizard uses Overheat over Weather Ball for guaranteed OHKOs — then switches out after Trick Room is set.
