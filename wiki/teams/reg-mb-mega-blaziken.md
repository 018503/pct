## Strategy

This team revives a classic interaction from 2018, when players paired Blaziken with Bisharp — now Kingambit — to great effect. Blaziken doesn't want to be Intimidated, so bringing along an Intimidate user like Incineroar simultaneously hands Kingambit a free Attack boost via Defiant, turning Life Orb Sucker Punch and Blaziken's own offense into a one-two punch that threatens most of the format. Speed Boost also gives Blaziken a choice: stay in base form a turn to bank a Speed boost first, then mega evolve once it's already fast.

Torkoal sets up sun to power up Flare Blitz, and doubles as a disruptor with Yawn and Burning Jealousy. Credit for this build goes to Metafy creator Alex, who had a strong run on ranked ladder with it and wrote up a free, in-depth team report on Metafy alongside sharing the paste. Sylveon runs Moonblast instead of the more common Hyper Beam or Giga Drain, specifically for reliable coverage against Soundproof Kommo-o, which can otherwise be very annoying for both Kingambit and Blaziken. Mega Aerodactyl serves as a secondary mega (this particular set skips Protect and Wide Guard), and Adaptability Basculegion rounds the team out.

Kingambit carries a bit of Speed investment to win the mirror matchup and to take advantage of Tailwind when it's up. Sylveon's HP/Defense mix is less physically-defensive than the Sylveon spreads seen elsewhere in this format. Torkoal runs a plus-Defense nature with heavy Special Defense — it isn't built to deal damage, just to enable whichever attacker follows it in as effectively as possible.
