## Strategy

Built by Singaporean player Jude Lee, who also reached #1 at the end of a ranked season in Pokemon Sword and Shield. This is a dual mega team centered on Calm Mind Mega Floette supported by Coaching Sneasler. The goal is to set up multiple Calm Mind boosts on Floette, which becomes nearly impossible to KO thanks to Draining Kiss healing back massive HP. Double fake out support from Incineroar and Sneasler creates openings for safe setup turns, while Sinistcha offers Rage Powder redirection and Hospitality healing. The Coaching defense boost from Sneasler is the key innovation — it lets Floette survive physical attacks like Dire Claw and Iron Head that would otherwise threaten it, buying extra turns to stack Calm Minds.

Mega Garchomp serves as the secondary mega for matchups where Floette struggles, particularly against fire types (Mega Delphox, Charizard Y + Venusaur) and steel types. Even without mega evolving, Garchomp provides strong offensive pressure alongside Floette. The very specially defensive Adamant spread with minimal speed survives most special attacks, and Coaching makes it incredibly difficult to deal with. Under Trick Room from Sinistcha, Mega Garchomp actually gains a speed advantage since it loses base speed when mega evolving. Incineroar's Leftovers and Helping Hand are notable — Leftovers provides sustain for cycling fake outs, while Helping Hand + Draining Kiss at +2 SpA secures OHKOs that Floette otherwise misses.

Floette is timid max speed because its speed tier is heavily contested — outspeeding opposing Charizard and Garchomp after setup means getting a Draining Kiss off before they can attack, which is often the difference between winning and losing. The team excels at long games where Floette can accumulate boosts, but requires mental resilience since a single critical hit or status can undo multiple turns of setup.
