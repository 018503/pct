## Strategy

One of the most unique high elo teams from the previous ranked season was built around Mega Blaziken, supported by really unique sets like Curse Mudsdale, Life Orb Meowscarada, Scale Shot Dragonite and Focus Sash Sylveon. The team aims to take care of big threats to Mega Blaziken early, opening the path for Mega Blaziken to sweep in the end-game.
