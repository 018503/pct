## Strategy

This is James Baek's recreation of a dual-mega team (Mega Meowstic-F and Mega Charizard Y) that finished Top 4 at Frankfurt Regionals in Regulation MC. James considered Mega Meowstic one of the weakest megas because Trace is unreliable and can overwrite your own terrain (tracing Rillaboom's Grassy Surge over Indeedee's Psychic Surge stops Expanding Force from being a spread move), but the team shows it can work with the right support.

The team's central plan is Psychic Terrain: male Indeedee sets it with Psychic Surge, which blocks priority moves against grounded Pokemon, and Meowstic plus Indeedee can both click Expanding Force as spread moves. Fake Out from Meowstic, Trick Room and Imprison on Indeedee (Imprison shuts off the opponent's Expanding Force or Trick Room), and Psychic Seed Sneasler give the team ways to dictate the pace. Mega Charizard Y is the coverage insurance: Drought sun and Heat Wave punish Rillaboom and terrain teams, and the team has little speed control otherwise, so Kingambit is built to simply survive and take hits.

Speed benchmarks: Meowstic and Indeedee are max Speed, outspeeding some non-max Raichu and Hisuian Arcanine respectively. Charizard outspeeds Timid max-Speed Gholdengo while surviving an Adamant Sneasler Rock Slide. Kommo-o is a fast Modest set that beats a +1 Delphox. Sneasler is max-Attack Adamant to KO the less bulky Charizards with Rock Slide. Kingambit is a very bulky set (max HP, SpD) meant to live Eternatus's Light of Ruin. Leads suggested: Meowstic + Indeedee (double Expanding Force), Kingambit early with faster cleaners behind it, or Meowstic Fake Out into Kommo-o's Clangorous Soul.

**Moves:** Expanding Force · Thunderbolt · Fake Out · Protect

**Role:** Fake Out + Expanding Force spread damage

Competitive before mega evolving, Trace after. Trace can copy Intimidate or a weather-speed ability, but tracing a rival terrain setter is a real downside for Expanding Force. Max SpA and Speed, with Expanding Force as the main damage, Thunderbolt for Basculegion, Charizard and Kingambit, and Fake Out for support. Frail, so it is invested purely in offence, and its Speed outruns some non-max Raichu.
