## Strategy

Built around Mega Aerodactyl + Scizor as a leading duo — innovative pairing that complements each other well. Scizor's Life Orb Close Combat and Knock Off gives coverage into steel types (which wall Aerodactyl) and ghosts. This is the most difficult team to pilot in the video but also one of the most creative.

Delphox-Mega is a Champions-exclusive mega that hits extremely hard (198 SpA) at high speed (179 Spe). Incineroar runs Taunt to shut down Trick Room — lead Incineroar + Delphox for Fake Out into Nasty Plot. Sinistcha is an offensive variant with Miracle Seed (187 SpA) specifically to handle Milotic, which is rising in usage and threatens the rest of the team.

The creator is described as the most creative high-ladder team builder from Japan — previously brought Golisopod/Raichu and Groudon/Kyogre to Worlds top cut.
