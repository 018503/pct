## Strategy

Mega Gengar is one of the most unique Mega Evolutions thanks to its ability, Shadow Tag. This team combines the classic Perish Song strategy, paired with an Alolan Ninetales to further disrupt opponents with Encore/Disable, and a ton of offense to make sure that you don't have to solely rely on Perish Song to win games! It's one of the best Mega Gengar teams right now, having finished 5th and 15th out of 6,000+ players in the Grand Champions Festival, the largest VGC tournament to date.
