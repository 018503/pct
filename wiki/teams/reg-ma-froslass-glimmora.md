## Strategy

Evan Scott's Indianapolis Regionals top-8 run showed how to fix Mega Froslass's worst matchup — Mega Charizard. The best Froslass teams stack multiple Charizard answers, and this one has two: Focus Sash Glimmora with Power Gem, and Sitrus Berry Garchomp with Rock Slide. Both are exceptionally hard for an opposing Charizard to deal with, which covers off the single biggest threat to Froslass in the format.

Bulk Up Corviknight adds another layer — Brave Bird and Body Press let it set up while also deterring Incineroar leads, since its Mirror Armor bounces Intimidate straight back at the sender (though that also means Froslass itself doesn't love seeing an opposing Incineroar). Between Glimmora, Garchomp, and Corviknight, this team applies real pressure into compositions that would otherwise give Froslass trouble. Froslass itself is heavily physically defensive with minimal speed investment — the plan is to click Aurora Veil early and let the boosted defenses carry the rest of the game.

Garchomp isn't run max Atk / max Spe either — Sitrus Berry plus real bulk means it survives big hits even without Aurora Veil, and becomes very difficult to knock out once the Veil is up. That combination of offense and bulk across the roster lets this team trade positively into opponents who lean hard into just offense or just defense. Tyranitar is another problem matchup for Froslass, but Corviknight, Garchomp, and Incineroar all help cover it — and since this is a single-mega team, it's sometimes correct to simply not bring Froslass in matchups where it isn't adding much.
