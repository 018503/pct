## Strategy

The team is built around a single Trick Room setter (Farigiraf with Armor Tail for priority protection) and two offensive Megas for the reverse-speed turns. Mega Camerupt (Solid Rock → Sheer Force) hits extremely hard with Earth Power, Heat Wave and Ancient Power, and is one of the few Pokemon that can remove Archaludon. Mega Abomasnow keeps Snow Warning, so it resets weather against other Megas and gets a +50% Defence boost in Snow, with Blizzard, Energy Ball and Earth Power plus an Ice Shard for outside of Trick Room.

Maushold is a Technician Population Bomb attacker with Wide Lens (99% per hit), plus Follow Me and After You. After You on a fast Maushold lets Farigiraf set Trick Room immediately (before negative priority) or lets a slow attacker move right after it. Incineroar with White Herb provides Intimidate, Fake Out and Darkest Lariat/Flare Blitz to support Farigiraf, and Life Orb Sylveon (Hyper Voice/Hyper Beam/Quick Attack) is a bulky special attacker one point faster than Farigiraf.

James notes there are several ways to pilot it: Farigiraf + Incineroar, Farigiraf + Maushold (Follow Me Trick Room or Helping Hand Population Bomb surprise KOs) or Maushold + an attacker with After You and no Trick Room. In the games the lead and back selection depended on whether the opponent had Tailwind offense or Rain.
