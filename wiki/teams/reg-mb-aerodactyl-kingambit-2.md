## Strategy

In this video, I try out the team that 2023 World Champion Shohei Kimura used to finish Rank #1 in the world in the 3rd season of ranked battles. Shohei adapted the common core of Aerodcatyl/Charizard and replaced Farigiraf, which is often seen on these compositions, with a VGC classic in Incineroar. The team has incredible offense and defense, optimizing the strongest Pokemon in the format.
