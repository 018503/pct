## Strategy

A Japanese ladder player won 16 games in a row and nearly reached 1800 Elo on the Champions ladder with this Mega Scrafty team. Mega Scrafty is unusual in that it keeps Intimidate both before and after mega evolving, letting it shut down physical attackers twice over across a single switch-in. This set sets up with Swords Dance, sustains with Drain Punch, and carries Knock Off — one of the best offensive moves in the game — to strip items along the way.

Scrafty pairs with Alolan Ninetales, which sets up Aurora Veil; combined with the newly added Light Clay item extending screen duration, and double Intimidate, Scrafty becomes extremely hard to break through. Its clear weak point is Fairy-types, so the team stacks answers: Choice Scarf Gholdengo (Make It Rain, Steel Beam — strong enough to threaten one-hit knockouts even through Wide Guard), and Roseli Berry Garchomp with Poison Jab, a set that was one of the most popular late in Regulation MA specifically for trading positively into Sylveon, Whimsicott, and Mega Floette. Life Orb Sylveon and a Tailwind Aerodactyl (which also checks Mega Charizard) fill out the rest of the team.

Unlike a lot of Scrafty sets, this one carries real Speed investment rather than pure bulk. Gholdengo and Sylveon are both close to max/max, Ninetales-Alola prioritizes Speed while keeping some bulk, and Garchomp keeps a little HP to last longer in exchanges.
