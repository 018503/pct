## Strategy

UB_SLOW, one of the best Bo1 closed-sheet players ever, finished **#1** last ranked season with this. Mega Garchomp Z usage has dropped, which Cybertron thinks could let it surprise people again. The team pairs Charizard Y and Garchomp Z with a classic **Fire / Water / Grass** core (Charizard, Milotic, Rillaboom), plus Incineroar and Sneasler.

The surprises: **max-speed Charizard** with **Ancient Power** over Solar Beam, which nearly always outpaces and threatens opposing Charizards, since most run slow and bulky now. **Garchomp Z** has enough bulk to survive Adamant Choice Scarf Dragon Claw from the Scarf Chomps built to check it. **Rillaboom has Protect**, and Cybertron lost to this team because of it. Opponents assume a safe double into Rillaboom, and Protect also helps stall Tailwind. Expect special Garchomp Z, fast Ancient Power Charizard and Protect Rillaboom when you see these six.
