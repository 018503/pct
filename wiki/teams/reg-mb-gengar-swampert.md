## Strategy

Mega Gengar is one of the hardest high-level mega evolutions to use, and this team pairs it with regular Swampert — a Pokemon that saw almost no usage before this archetype emerged. Michael Kelch (multiple World Championship top finishes) built and piloted this team to win a 200+ player Victory Road tournament, and a near-identical variant later finished top four in the biggest online tournament before the 2026 World Championships. The core concept is using Swampert's Yawn alongside Mega Gengar's Shadow Tag to guarantee sleep — the opponent simply cannot switch out to avoid falling asleep on the following turn. Swampert also provides bulk, ground-type offense against threats like Tyranitar and Excadrill, and Flip Turn pivoting.

The team runs Substitute over the more common Perish Song on Gengar, using it more for Shadow Tag trapping and offensive pressure than perish trapping. Gengar is modest max speed with some special attack investment (10 points), allowing potential OHKOs on super-effective targets with Shadow Ball that zero-SpA spreads would miss. Dragonite carries Inner Focus instead of Multiscale, making it immune to Fake Out — a massive advantage against the format's many Fake Out leads. Sneasler runs Rock Slide over Fake Out, giving it crucial coverage against Mega Charizard and flinch potential. Choice Scarf Ninetales-Alola provides Encore to lock opponents after Gengar protects, plus Icy Wind speed control for Dragonite follow-ups.

Key leads include Gengar + Sneasler (offensive pressure with speed), Gengar + Swampert (Yawn + Shadow Tag trapping), Gengar + Dragonite (double Fake Out immunity), and Dragonite + Swampert (anti-Trick Room and anti-setup). The team's main weaknesses are ground-type attacks hitting most of the team super-effectively, Choice Scarf Pokemon outspeeding and KOing Gengar in best-of-one, and the general difficulty of piloting Mega Gengar precisely in closed team sheet formats where surprise items can be punishing.

Modest max speed Mega Gengar with 10 SpA points and 20 HP / 4 Def for bulk. The SpA investment can secure OHKOs on super-effective targets that zero-investment spreads would miss. The defensive investment lets it survive Life Orb Earthquake from -1 Jolly Garchomp (after Intimidate).
