## Strategy

This Mega Gardevoir team was built by TollerWeb, one of the few players in Pokemon VGC to have won a regional, national, and world championship title, and one of the format's premier Mega Gardevoir pilots since its early days. It's a newer version of a Gardevoir team they built previously featured on the channel. The core idea is that Mega Gardevoir can function under either Tailwind or Trick Room. Talonflame provides the Tailwind route — this is the no-item Talonflame, which gets an immediate damage boost to Acrobatics and hits hard as a result.

Alongside Tailwind, there's a Trick Room component: leading Maushold plus Gardevoir gives Friend Guard damage reduction for Gardevoir plus redirection support from Maushold, and Super Fang immediately lowers a target's HP so you can secure a follow-up knockout. Maushold itself is faster than Gardevoir, Basculegion, and Kingambit, so it can angle for a Super Fang into an immediate knockout from a teammate. The Choice Scarf Garchomp doesn't require committing the mega evolution slot — you can just lead with Earthquake and regular Hyper Voice or Psyshock from Gardevoir instead.

What makes this team hard to deal with is the flex between Trick Room and Tailwind, with Maushold as a strong supportive option and powerful closers in Garchomp, Basculegion, and Kingambit able to finish games out either way.
