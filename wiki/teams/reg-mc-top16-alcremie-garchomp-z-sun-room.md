## Strategy

London Faust took this to Top 16 of over 1,000 players at the Louisville Regional Championships, the second North American Reg MC Major. Mega Garchomp Z is the only mega. Behind it is a slow Trick Room core of Farigiraf, Torkoal and Kingambit, plus a support pick that sits outside the top 100 in usage: Alcremie. Decorate gives a partner +2 Attack and +2 Special Attack and goes through Protect. Sweet Veil stops Alcremie and its partner from being put to sleep, which matters with Venusaur so common (Venusaur had just won another regional).

Speeds are spread on purpose. Garchomp is Timid with 17 Speed points (206), so it outspeeds Modest max-Speed Mega Garchomp Z and anything a point or two faster. Venusaur is max Speed for sun. Torkoal, Farigiraf and Kingambit all have minus-Speed natures for Trick Room. A +2 Draco Meteor from Garchomp OHKOs almost anything that doesn't resist it, which answers the usual complaint that Garchomp Z doesn't hit hard enough.

Cybertron's leads: Alcremie + Garchomp (Protect / Decorate turn one, or Helping Hand for an immediate KO), then Farigiraf + Torkoal Trick Room to clean up; Torkoal + Venusaur into teams with no weather or no answer to Sleep Powder / Eruption; Kingambit + Farigiraf into Trick Room and fairy teams (Mega Gardevoir, Sylveon), forcing awkward targeting; and Alcremie + Farigiraf for immediate +2 Hyper Voices. Many teams have only one real check to Torkoal, so removing it often lets Torkoal sweep under Trick Room.
