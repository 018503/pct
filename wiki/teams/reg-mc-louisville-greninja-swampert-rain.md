## Strategy

Andrew Cavanah went 9-3 at Louisville Regionals and just missed top cut with this dual-mega rain team. James recreated the spreads. Mega Greninja (base 142 Speed) is one of the fastest megas but has low offences for a mega, so it relies on Protean: its type changes to match whichever move it uses first, both before and after mega. Mega Swampert runs special despite its 150 base Attack. Ice Beam OHKOs Mega Salamence and Intimidate doesn't matter, and Earth Power has about an 87.5% chance to OHKO max-HP Mega Raichu and hits Archaludon before Stamina stacks up.

The other trick is Choice Scarf Tsareena with Aromatic Mist, which gives an ally +1 Special Defense. On top of Archaludon's Stamina Defense boosts, that makes it very hard to remove, and in good matchups it can win 4v1. Queenly Majesty also blocks Grassy Glide aimed at Swampert and priority aimed at the frail Greninja. Speed control is double: Pelipper Icy Wind and Life Orb Gale Wings Talonflame Tailwind.

How to use it: either all-out offence or slow the game down to set up Archaludon. Leads: Pelipper + Swampert (Ice Beam Salamence before Tailwind, Earth Power Raichu, Muddy Water spread); Pelipper + Archaludon for Electro Shot, with Tsareena coming in to Aromatic Mist; or fast offence with Greninja + Tsareena or Greninja + Talonflame.
