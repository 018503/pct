## Strategy

Mega Golisopod has been one of the most hyped new mega evolutions in Regulation MC thanks to incredible base stats, Tough Claws as its mega ability, and one of the deepest movepools of any viable mega. This team is built around maximizing Golisopod's offensive output with four attacks (no Protect, no Swords Dance) while covering its two main weaknesses: low base speed and a 4x fire weakness. Farigiraf provides Trick Room to turn Golisopod's slow speed into an advantage, while Life Orb Politoed sets rain to neutralize fire-type threats and acts as a devastating offensive option in its own right — one-shotting Incineroar with Weather Ball and threatening Salamence and Garchomp with Ice Beam.

The team operates with several distinct modes. The primary lead is Staraptor plus Farigiraf, where Armor Tail blocks priority on Staraptor, allowing a safe Final Gambit for an immediate knockout followed by Trick Room setup — then Politoed or Golisopod sweep from the back. Against physically oriented teams without clear fire damage, Pawmot leads alongside Golisopod with Coaching, stacking Attack and Defense boosts that make Golisopod extremely difficult to remove while it sustains with Leech Life. The Choice Scarf Staraptor also provides mindgame value in team preview, as opponents frequently assume it's Mega Staraptor and play accordingly. Liquidation over the more common Rock Slide gives Golisopod a much better matchup into fire types under rain — hitting Mega Camerupt, Hisui Arcanine, Mega Delphox, and Incineroar for massive damage.

Key weaknesses include Mega Charizard Y (which wins the weather war and threatens a 4x super-effective STAB), well-played defensive Charizard positioning that stalls Trick Room turns, coil Mylotic with Lightning Rod Raichu support, and taunt users on Indeedee-F or Gardevoir that shut down Farigiraf's Trick Room setup. The team's biggest vulnerability in best-of-one is tech fire coverage on unexpected Pokemon — Mystical Fire Gardevoir, Flamethrower Salamence — so assuming fire coverage until proven otherwise is essential.

**Moves:** First Impression · Leech Life · Iron Head · Liquidation

**Role:** Four-attack Trick Room sweeper

Golisopod is the centerpiece of this team, running a pure offensive set with four coverage moves to maximize damage output against any opposing Pokemon. First Impression provides massive priority damage on switch-ins and turn-one pressure. Leech Life sustains Golisopod through extended sequences, especially devastating after Coaching boosts where the defense buff lets it tank hits and heal back with Leech Life. Iron Head covers Fairy types and provides general neutral coverage. Liquidation is the key tech over the more common Rock Slide — under rain from Politoed, it hits fire types incredibly hard, giving Golisopod actual counterplay against its biggest weakness. The Brave nature with max Attack and HP investment makes it a raw damage machine under Trick Room, and 195 base Defense on the mega form means physical attackers struggle to break through it, especially after a Coaching boost.

**Role:** Coaching support + fast physical attacker

Pawmot provides the team's non-Trick Room offensive mode by leading alongside Golisopod with Coaching, giving immediate +1 Attack and Defense boosts. This is especially powerful because the Defense boost compounds with Golisopod's already massive 195 base Defense and Leech Life sustain — making it nearly impossible to KO through physical damage. Double Shock and Close Combat deal massive damage into common threats, and Iron Fist boosts Double Shock's already high base power. Revival Blessing gives a second chance to Golisopod or Politoed if they fall early. The trade-off with Double Shock is that using it removes Pawmot's Electric typing, so positioning matters — ideally you get one big Double Shock KO and pivot to Coaching or Close Combat.

Farigiraf is the team's primary support Pokemon with a mixed defensive spread covering both physical and special attacks. Armor Tail is critical — it blocks priority moves like Fake Out from Incineroar and opposing First Impression, protecting Staraptor's Final Gambit on turn one and Golisopod's setup turns. Helping Hand amplifies the already massive damage output from Golisopod and Politoed. Psychic provides STAB coverage, and Thunderbolt gives supplementary damage against Water types and Charizard. This Farigiraf runs no Protect, so opponents can exploit predictable turns, but the trade-off is having four useful moves for different game states.
