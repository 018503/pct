## Strategy

The team is built around Glaceon as a consistent damage dealer across multiple seasons, reaching Champion Tier in both Regulation MA and MB. The core pairing is Mega Froslass + Glaceon — Froslass mega-evolves for Snow Warning, setting up snow that activates Glaceon's Snow Cloak and compounds with Bright Powder to make Glaceon extremely difficult to hit. Even without evasion procs, Glaceon hits extraordinarily hard with Modest nature and 30 SpA investment, and the snow grants it a 50% Defense boost in its Ice typing. Blizzard spam is the primary gameplan, with Freeze-Dry providing crucial coverage into bulky waters like Milotic and Basculegion that resist nothing else on the team. Ice Shard gives Glaceon a priority option for finishing off weakened threats.

The secondary lead is Talonflame + Mega Glimmora, which covers Charizard-heavy matchups where the ice duo struggles. Life Orb Talonflame hits far harder than the standard Sharp Beak or Acrobatics variants — Brave Bird + Flare Blitz provide meaningful damage into megas and can set up KO combos with Sneezler's Close Combat or Kingambit's Sucker Punch. Glimmora offers Adaptability-boosted Power Gem and Earth Power with wide coverage, and Spiky Shield breaks Focus Sashes on contact. The team's flexibility comes from mixing and matching the two megas — regular Glimmora still hits hard even without mega-evolving, and regular Froslass's Taunt shuts down Trick Room, Coil, Rage Powder, and stat-boosting setups.

Key weaknesses include teams with multiple ice resistances (steel, fire types), opposing weather control (especially Tyranitar), and Life Orb Swords Dance Kingambit which can snowball if given a free boost. Wide Guard also hard-walls the Blizzard spam. The team trades bulk for speed and offense — Talonflame, Glimmora, and Sneezler are all quite frail and need careful positioning to extract value before going down.

**Moves:** Blizzard · Freeze-Dry · Ice Shard · Protect

**Role:** Primary special attacker; Blizzard spam + Freeze-Dry coverage for water types; evasion tank in snow.

Glaceon's offensive and defensive identity both revolve around snow. 30 SpA with Modest pushes raw damage to 198 — enough to OHKO Garchomp with Blizzard and 2HKO most neutral targets. Bright Powder compounds with Snow Cloak under snow to make hitting Glaceon genuinely unreliable (Rock Slide at 90% accuracy becomes ~72% effective). Freeze-Dry is critical for OHKOing Basculegion and hitting bulky waters that wall the rest of the team. Ice Shard provides priority to finish off weakened threats and break Sashes. 10 Speed points reach 95, enough to outpace Sneezler and Mega Froslass under Tailwind. The 24 HP / 2 Def spread leverages Glaceon's high base 110 Defense alongside the snow Defense boost to survive physical attacks that would KO frailer Ice types. Can be led without Froslass — against Garchomp + Charizard teams, Glaceon with Sneezler (Fake Out) or Talonflame (Tailwind) forces Garchomp out immediately via Freeze-Dry threat, creating space for Glimmora.

This Kingambit drops Iron Head for Low Kick, specifically to beat opposing Kingambit — at 91 Speed it outspeeds the vast majority of Kingambit variants under Tailwind and threatens a clean 2HKO or OHKO with Low Kick depending on their bulk.
