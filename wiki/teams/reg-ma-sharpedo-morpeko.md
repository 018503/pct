## Strategy

A really unique team featuring Vanilluxe, Morpeko, and Mega Sharpedo placed in 102nd place out of over 6,000 players in the Grand Champions Festival, a huge Pokemon Champions VGC tournament. None of these are popular picks in the metagame at all, so I wanted to see just how good they could be and when to use them!
