## Strategy

Mega Ampharos has seen very little usage in Pokemon Champions, but it's surprisingly good into many of the top Pokemon in the format. It can wall Mega Charizard Y and quickly boost up its defense with Cotton Guard, becoming a physical tank. As we wrap up Regulation MA, I thought it would be fun to try out one of the least popular Mega Evolutions in the format and see just waht it can do!
