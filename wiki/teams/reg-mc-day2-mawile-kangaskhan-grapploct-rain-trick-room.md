## Strategy

A hard Trick Room team that put Grapploct into Day 2 of the Frankfurt Regionals, the first time the Pokemon had done so. Technician Storm Throw always crits, ignoring Intimidate drops and defensive boosts, and Mach Punch gives it priority (boosted by Technician and Life Orb), and Stomping Tantrum is for Gholdengo.

Mega Mawile is the main sweeper under Trick Room (Hyper Cutter before mega so Intimidate cannot touch it; Huge Power after), with Mega Kangaskhan as a second mega option (Scrappy blocks Intimidate and hits Ghosts). Farigiraf sets Trick Room with Armor Tail, Pelipper sets Damp Rock rain for Archaludon's Electro Shot and Tailwind as an alternate speed mode.

Plan: set Trick Room, then sweep with Mawile, Grapploct, Kangaskhan and Archaludon. Rain Dance + Electro Shot beats opposing weather. Archaludon's Stamina plus Sitrus Farigiraf makes the team bulky.
