## Strategy

This team won the 2027 Taiwan Premier Ball League, an invite-only event of about 100 top Taiwanese players with Worlds invites on the line. It was Mega Camerupt's first Masters win ever. It builds on Ricardo Stellini's Top 8 Nice Regionals team. Camerupt has only about 3% usage, but Sheer Force lets it one-hit KO Incineroar, Rillaboom, Kingambit, Sneasler and Mega Charizard Y. It is also bulky enough that only Water attacks really scare it. The team pairs **Mega Camerupt** with **Mega Raichu Y** for two speed modes, and the two megas are rarely brought together. The **Trick Room mode** (the default) is Farigiraf next to Incineroar or Rillaboom. The partner can Fake Out, or just Parting Shot / U-turn while Trick Room goes up, so Camerupt comes in for free. Incineroar is the safer lead because of Intimidate. Rillaboom gives faster pressure with Helping Hand Life Orb Wood Hammer, and it can KO a Farigiraf trying to reverse Trick Room. Helping Hand Heat Wave / Earth Power / Ancient Power from Camerupt wins most endgames. It's especially strong against the Protect people use on the last turn of Trick Room, and the damage is huge in the opponent's sun.

Other modes: **Raichu Y + Sylveon** with triple Fake Out is the fast mode. Zap Cannon paralysis lets Sylveon outspeed, and Sylveon's 7 Speed points let it outspeed paralysed base 120s (Adamant Sneasler, Modest Froslass). **Sylveon + Fake Out user** leads delay the mega: Fake Out, Calm Mind, pivot Farigiraf in for a mid-game Trick Room, then bring Camerupt late. **Lead Camerupt** (usually with Farigiraf) into teams with no Water answer, or Kingambit + redirection (Sinistcha). It threatens a KO on Kingambit before it can Swords Dance. **Calm Mind Moonblast Sylveon** is the second win condition. Opponents Protect to stall Trick Room, which gives it free Calm Minds, and Grassy Terrain heals it.

**Camerupt vs Raichu.** Cybertron brought Camerupt in all 8 ladder games. On closed team sheet best-of-one it's more consistent: it's bulky, Rillaboom covers its Water weakness, and it OHKOs bulky threats like Archaludon, Chandelure, Mega Golisopod and Gholdengo. Raichu Y is fragile and loses to random Choice Scarf users (Garchomp, Baxcalibur) and opposing Lightning Rod. It matters more in open team sheet best-of-three. Bring it into bulky Waters (Milotic) when they lack an answer to it, into a known Life Orb Kingambit, or in a later game once the opponent has over-adjusted to Camerupt.

**Weaknesses:** well-played bulky Waters (Milotic, Pelipper rain), especially once Rillaboom is gone. Sash Baxcalibur takes Grassy Glide and KOs Camerupt. Kingambit is a threat whether it holds Sash, Chople or Life Orb / Black Glasses (which OHKOs Farigiraf), and it punishes Incineroar leads through Defiant, as Milotic does through Competitive. Anything that stops Trick Room is a problem: non-Prankster Taunt, Imprison, a reverse Trick Room, Roar, or slow leads that force Fake Out on turn 1 so the pivot isn't free. Opponents can also Protect where Camerupt will target. Golisopod and Steel types out-trade Sylveon, and if you bring only one of Camerupt or Incineroar and lose it, Golisopod can beat everything else. Rillaboom has no Protect, and under your own Trick Room it is sometimes slower than you want. Raichu's speed control is awkward because most of the team is slow even when the target is paralysed.

**Moves:** Zap Cannon · Focus Blast · Fake Out · Protect

**Role:** Zap Cannon fast mode

Timid 26 HP / 14 Def / 26 Spe. As a mega it has 193 Spe, creeping the common 190–192 (max-speed Sneasler / Mega Froslass and those that creep them). Survives Jolly Sneasler Close Combat 15/16. Bring it into bulky Waters without a Raichu answer, or as a best-of-three switch-up once opponents build their games around stopping Camerupt. Avoid it into an opposing Raichu (Lightning Rod), a possible Choice Scarf user, or Gardevoir.

**Moves:** Heat Wave · Earth Power · Ancient Power · Protect

**Role:** Sheer Force Trick Room nuke

Quiet max HP / max SpA. Ancient Power OHKOs Mega Charizard, Heat Wave handles Rillaboom / Kingambit, Earth Power handles Archaludon. Helping Hand from Farigiraf amplifies it further. One of the slowest Pokemon in the format, which is ideal under Trick Room. Default mega on ladder. It was one of the best answers to Mega Meganium and Archaludon in Cybertron's games, and Helping Hand Heat Wave in the opponent's sun swept a Charizard team. While Trick Room is up, use it to KO the fast threats first (Ancient Power on Mega Aerodactyl). Heat Wave can miss, so in tight endgames prefer Earth Power. It can also lead into teams with no Water attacks.

**Moves:** Flare Blitz · Darkest Lariat · Parting Shot · Fake Out

**Role:** White Herb offensive Fake Out

Brave 32 HP / 25 Atk. White Herb cancels an opposing Intimidate, so it can lead into Incineroar and still Flare Blitz bulky Gholdengo. Parting Shot pivots Camerupt in on the Trick Room turn. Its heavy Attack investment makes it less bulky than usual, so the plan is Fake Out or pivot, not tanking. Helping Hand Flare Blitz / Darkest Lariat secure KOs. Avoid leading it into Kingambit (Defiant) or Milotic (Competitive), and remember that priority-blocking abilities stop Fake Out.

**Role:** Life Orb Fake Out attacker

Adamant 32 HP / 25 Atk / 5 Def / 3 SpD / 1 Spe. It's the team's main answer to Water types, so keep it alive for Milotic. Helping Hand Wood Hammer KO'd a Farigiraf before it could reverse Trick Room. Grassy Glide removes Focus Sashes (Kingambit) and picks off weakened Pokemon. It has no Protect. Fainting early can even help, because Camerupt then comes in for free.
