## Strategy

This team won the Taiwan Premier Ball League Championships and made top 8 at Nice Regionals. It pairs **Mega Camerupt** with **Mega Raichu Y** for two different speed modes. The **Trick Room mode** has double Fake Out with Farigiraf. Incineroar (Parting Shot) and Rillaboom (U-turn) can pivot straight into Camerupt as Trick Room goes up. Farigiraf's Helping Hand makes Camerupt's Ancient Power (Charizard), Heat Wave (Rillaboom, Kingambit) and Earth Power (Archaludon) one-hit KOs.

The **fast mode** is **Raichu Y + Sylveon + Rillaboom + one**. Raichu Zap Cannons, and paralysed targets then sit below the mid-speed Sylveon and Rillaboom. Cybertron's mode picks: commit to Trick Room with Fake Out user + Farigiraf + Camerupt + the other Fake Out user / Sylveon. Play fast with Raichu + Rillaboom + Sylveon + 1. **Calm Mind Moonblast Sylveon** is the unusual set. Opponents tend to Protect to stall your Trick Room, which hands Sylveon free Calm Minds, and Grassy Terrain heals it.
