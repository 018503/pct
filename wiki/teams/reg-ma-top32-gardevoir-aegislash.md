## Strategy

If you want a different take on Mega Gardevoir, this team pairs it with Aegislash and again runs the Tailwind/Trick Room flex. There's a fun Discharge Rotom-Wash plus Garchomp duo — Discharge/Earthquake was one of the most popular early-VGC strategies (think Zapdos and Garchomp), built around the fact that both Pokemon are immune to their own spread damage, letting you target both opponents freely. Discharge also carries a paralysis chance, which paired with Rock Slide from elsewhere on the team can be genuinely tricky to play around.

Spell Tag Aegislash is a highlight — one of the strongest Ghost-type attackers in the game, and flexible enough to function under either Trick Room or Tailwind, with a little Speed investment here to support both modes. Rounding things out are Soft Sand Garchomp, Rotom-Wash, and a Tailwind/offense-focused Aerodactyl that can also protect the team with Rock Slide and Dual Wingbeat pressure, alongside Incineroar. On the Tailwind route, lead Aerodactyl with Garchomp or Gardevoir and start dealing damage with Hyper Voice or Earthquake; on the Trick Room route, lead Incineroar plus Gardevoir, Fake Out into Trick Room, then bring in Aegislash to hit hard while the field is slowed.

The Discharge combination and the Spell Tag Aegislash are the standout fun elements that separate this from the other Mega Gardevoir builds in this list.
