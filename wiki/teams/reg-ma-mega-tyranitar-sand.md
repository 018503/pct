## Strategy

One of the most consistent sand teams since the format began — an adapted version of a build by JoeUX9, one of North America's premier players. It leans on a lot of bulk and excellent typing coverage across the board, and Mega Tyranitar in particular is fantastic into Mega Charizard-Y, still one of the most dominant mega evolutions in the game.

Tyranitar hits hard but also carries real speed investment, which pays off especially under Tailwind from Corviknight — Corviknight itself runs a speedy spread so the whole team can outrun the format once Tailwind is up. With only Brave Bird as an attacking move, Corviknight mostly uses Bulk Up in front of things that can't threaten it, then swings back with big numbers; Roost plus Leftovers makes it exceptionally hard to knock out without super-effective damage.

Hydreigon's Haban Berry Snarl set is disruptive into special attackers, chipping away Special Attack stat-by-stat, while Draco Meteor threatens immediate burst KOs — Garchomp in particular. Excadrill threatens OHKOs with Iron Head into Floette and Sylveon, and pressures Mega Charizard with Rock Slide under sand. Between the offensive and defensive synergy across the roster, this has proven to be one of the most reliable ways to run both sand and Mega Tyranitar all format long.
