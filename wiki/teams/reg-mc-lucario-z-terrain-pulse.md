## Strategy

This team from shaikhvgc786 reached 2000+ ELO in the opening days of Regulation MC off a 10-game win streak, before Master Ball and Champion tiers even unlocked. It pairs Mega Lucario Z, a fast special attacker, with both terrain setters, Indeedee-F (Psychic Surge) and Rillaboom (Grassy Surge), so Lucario's Terrain Pulse becomes a surprise Psychic or Grass hit that one-shots Sneasler, Milotic, Basculegion and Politoed. Cybertron notes Calm Mind and Nasty Plot are the more tournament-proven Lucario Z sets; Terrain Pulse is best in closed team sheet Bo1 where opponents cannot play around it (on ladder Dark Pulse is another option).

The team has no Tailwind or Trick Room, so every Pokemon except Indeedee-F invests heavily in Speed. Modest Lucario outspeeds max-speed base-120s after mega evolving; Armarouge is near max SpA/Spe and Weak Armor pushes it past nearly everything; Rillaboom outspeeds max-speed Adamant Basculegion; Sneasler outspeeds max-speed Adamant Palafin even without Grassy Seed, and with it outspeeds the whole metagame. The default lead is Lucario + Hisuian Arcanine, with a terrain user (Indeedee-F or Rillaboom) in the back to be switched in for a super-effective Terrain Pulse. Other leads: Rillaboom + Sneasler into Garchomp (Fake Out, Swords Dance, Close Combat) and Indeedee-F + Armarouge for an immediate Psychic Terrain Expanding Force.

Weaknesses: well-positioned Dragons (Mega Salamence, Mega Garchomp Z, Garchomp), Tailwind, Choice Scarf and Focus Sash users, and Ghost types walling Sneasler (Gholdengo). Lucario often fails to get one-hit KOs and remains frail to super-effective hits despite Aura Guard. Cybertron's verdict: a fun, decent mega, best paired with a second mega in tournaments to cover its problem matchups.

**Moves:** Flash Cannon · Terrain Pulse · Aura Sphere · Protect

**Role:** Fast special sweeper + terrain-pulse tech

Mega Lucario Z is the team's main damage dealer and usual lead. With 20 Spe points and Modest it outspeeds max-speed base-120s such as Mega Froslass and Sneasler once mega evolved, though it stays slower than base-130s (a Timid nature is the suggested tweak). Terrain Pulse becomes a Psychic or Grass move under Indeedee's or Rillaboom's terrain, giving one-hit KO threats on Sneasler, Milotic, Basculegion and Politoed. Double Aura Sphere with Armarouge answers Archaludon. Aura Guard cuts contact damage but Cybertron stresses it is still frail to super-effective hits, so expect to take ~80% rather than tank.
