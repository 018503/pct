## Strategy

Winner of the Indianapolis Regional Championships (Arsal went 15-1 with it). Unlike most Charizard/Florges teams, this one runs Calm Mind Floette instead of max offense Floette, and drops Tailwind entirely in favor of raw natural speed and a Trick Room backup plan. Two distinct modes: a sun mode (Charizard, Venusaur, Garchomp, Incineroar) and a setup mode (Incineroar, Floette, Sinistcha, plus one of Garchomp/Venusaur) — the dual identity makes it difficult for opponents to prepare for both Mega Charizard and Calm Mind Floette at once.

The offensive core hits hard and fast rather than tanking hits — Choice Scarf Garchomp and Focus Sash Venusaur both run minimal defensive investment, prioritizing speed and immediate pressure. Floette is built more offensively than the typical bulky Calm Mind set: a single Calm Mind boost threatens an immediate OHKO on even max HP / SpD Kingambit. Mega Charizard-Y trades some speed for HP and Defense to survive Rock Slide, but still outpaces most of the format at 152 Speed — a real edge against the many opposing Charizards that have been slowed down defensively. Incineroar's speed investment (96) lets it win the Fake Out war or click Throat Chop before the opponent can Parting Shot away.

Trick Room is a clever counter-tech against the Tailwind-heavy, Whimsicott-based strategies that dominate the format — since Charizard, Garchomp, and Floette already have good natural speed, Trick Room flips the matchup against faster Tailwind teams rather than being the team's primary plan. Venusaur is crucial for leading into Whimsicott and Glimmora with super-effective damage (Sleep Powder to remove a threat, Earth Power for coverage into Incineroar/Arcanine) now that dedicated Venusaur answers have fallen out of the meta.
