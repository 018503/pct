## Strategy

This team revolves around a Psych Up Metagross paired with Psychic Seed Coil Milotic — Milotic sets up multiple Coil boosts alongside its Psychic Seed special defense buff, then Metagross copies everything with Psych Up and sweeps with Psychic Fangs (boosted by Psychic Terrain) and Body Press (scaling off the copied defense boosts). The Milotic concept was built by Dorian King (SlycedBread), who also finished top 16 at Baltimore, and the overall team was created by Daniel Yu (ArKaneVGC), a former regional champion.

The team features strong anti-meta options beyond the core combo. Life Orb Infiltrator Dragapult threatens common meta threats like Gholdengo, Basculegion, and Mega Salamence with immediate one-hit knockouts via Shadow Ball and Draco Meteor, while Will-O-Wisp neuters physical attackers. Cloud Nine Altaria shuts down opposing weather teams entirely — no Chlorophyll for Venusaur, no Electro Shot for Archaludon, no sand Rush for Excadrill — and carries Tailwind, Ice Beam, and Will-O-Wisp for disruption. Hisuian Arcanine rounds out the offense with Rock Head Head Smash and Flare Blitz for massive recoil-free damage. The team pilot, Dylan Matthews (dylanis_chilln), piloted it to top 16 at Baltimore Regionals.

A key tension in playing the team is knowing when to commit to the Coil/Psych Up strategy versus when to lead aggressively with Dragapult and Arcanine. Into Rillaboom, Gholdengo, or Mega Raichu Y compositions, the setup plan struggles — Milotic takes too much burst damage or Metagross gets walled. In those games, Dragapult + Arcanine as a fast offensive lead is far stronger, with Metagross just providing raw Psychic Fangs damage in the back without ever needing to Psych Up.

**Moves:** Muddy Water · Coil · Hypnosis · Recover

**Role:** Setup wall + Psych Up enabler

The engine of the team's setup strategy. Psychic Seed grants an immediate SpD boost in terrain, then Coil raises Attack, Defense, and accuracy each turn. After two Coils, Hypnosis becomes 100% accurate, putting threats to sleep. Recover keeps it alive through prolonged setup. The speed investment lets it outpace uninvested Rillaboom and threaten Hypnosis before it moves. Every decision with Milotic is critical — choosing between a second Coil, Hypnosis, Recover, or Muddy Water chip damage requires reading the opponent's positioning carefully. The lack of Protect means opponents can punish predictable turns.

**Role:** Psych Up sweeper + late-game closer

Copies Milotic's Coil boosts with Psych Up, then sweeps with Psychic Fangs (boosted by Psychic Terrain and Tough Claws) and Body Press (scaling off the massive copied defense stat). No Steel STAB or Ground coverage — the moveset is entirely built around the Psych Up combo. Body Press specifically covers Incineroar and Kingambit, which resist Psychic Fangs. The 173 speed outspeeds neutral max speed base 120 Pokemon like Mega Froslass and uninvested Adamant Sneasler. Even without Psych Up, raw Psychic Fangs in terrain often two-shots most neutral targets. Can also copy opposing stat boosts — notably Milotic Coils or Kommo-o Clangorous Soul.

A super low usage rate pick specifically designed to counter weather teams. Limited offensive output means its partner must do the heavy lifting.
