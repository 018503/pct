## Strategy

This is the Regulation MA team that qualified the eventual 2026 World Champion by getting Top 64 at Japan Nationals. The plan is to set Trick Room, give Snorlax Simple (Audino Simple Beam) so each Stockpile counts for two stages, and then Psych Up on Mega Slowbro and Farigiraf to copy the boosts into Stored Power, a 20 BP move that gains 20 BP per stat boost. Shell Armor on Mega Slowbro removes crit variance, which is the usual weakness of this kind of setup team.

Sableye (Reflect, Light Screen, Taunt, Fake Out) and Maushold (Follow Me, Taunt, Sunny Day) buy the turns needed. Several Taunts stop Whimsicott's Encore and Sinistcha's Rage Powder, and Sunny Day helps against Rain with Archaludon. Farigiraf adds priority protection and a Psych Up backup.

The speed numbers are exact: under Trick Room Audino moves before Snorlax (so Snorlax gets Simple before Stockpile), and Slowbro is one point faster than Snorlax so it moves after Snorlax's second Stockpile and copies it with Psych Up. James tried it in Regulation MB expecting little from an MA team; in the test game +4 Snorlax body-pressed through Incineroar and Sylveon and Slowbro outlasted Life Orb Hyper Beam.
