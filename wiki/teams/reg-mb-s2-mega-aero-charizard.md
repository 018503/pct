## Strategy

One of the most popular compositions in both Regulation MA and MB. 2019 senior world champion Kosukadeay used this team to finish 3rd. Aerodactyl flexes as either a mega evolution or regular non-mega for speed control via Tailwind. Life Orb Garchomp is now available in MB (was soft sand only in MA).

What makes this version stand out are the surprise spreads. Kingambit is max HP / max SpD rather than max Atk — survives special attacks people expect to OHKO it (Light of Ruin, Hyper Beam). Charizard is Bold with heavy physical defense rather than Modest — lives physical hits that normally KO. Floette is Timid max speed / max SpA rather than Modest. These spreads exploit opponents' internal damage calcs.
