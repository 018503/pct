## Strategy

Created by Tatsuya Watanuki, who also built the Feraligatr/Sceptile/Houndoom team earlier in the season and finished 17th at the 2022 World Championships with an off-meta Groudon/Kyogre/Raichu/Golisopod team. This is easily the most unique team in the Season 4 top 100, using several Pokemon that see virtually no usage in competitive VGC. The core idea is stacking residual damage through sand (Hippowdon) and poison (Glimmora's Toxic Debris + Mortal Spin) to chip opponents down, then closing out games with Mega Staraptor or burst from Mimikyu/Tyrantrum.

Hippowdon replaces the typical Tyranitar as sand setter because it's far bulkier and can sustain itself with Slack Off + Sitrus Berry, while Tyranitar's fighting weakness is heavily exploited by Sneasler and Mega Staraptor. Hippowdon's Brave nature lets it operate under opposing Trick Room. Gholdengo runs a unique Substitute + Leftovers + Focus Blast set instead of the standard Make It Rain — Substitute stalls out turns while poison and sand tick, and Focus Blast handles Kingambit which would otherwise wall it behind Sucker Punch. Good as Gold blocks status moves, making it exceptionally hard for Trick Room teams to deal with.

Mega Staraptor is a mid-to-late game closer, not a lead. After residual damage chips opponents to ~60-70%, Staraptor comes in, gets a Close Combat KO with a defense boost, and becomes increasingly difficult to remove with Roost. Tyrantrum with Choice Scarf and Strong Jaw provides surprise OHKOs on Garchomp (Ice Fang) and breaks screens with Psychic Fangs. Mimikyu's Phantom Force bypasses Protect and punishes slow setup-oriented teams. This is an advanced team that rewards knowledge of all its unique components — not recommended for beginners.
