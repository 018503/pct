## Strategy

Built by Fede Campo, this team makes the case for Mega Pyroar over the far more popular Mega Charizard-Y: not being 4x weak to Rock is a real advantage in a format where Rock-type Pokemon (like Lycanroc, which rose in popularity specifically to punish Charizard) have become common tech picks. Pyroar also pairs naturally with Heat Rock Torkoal — a new weather item introduced this regulation that can't be held by Charizard — giving extended sun uptime. Combining sun, Helping Hand, and Unnerve lets Heat Wave and Overheat stack up damage extremely quickly.

Torkoal here is built purely to support Pyroar rather than attack: it runs max HP and max Special Defense instead of the more common max Special Attack set, and doesn't carry Eruption or Weather Ball at all — just Heat Wave for chip, Helping Hand to enable Pyroar, and Yawn for disruption. Torkoal paired with Venusaur also makes for a disruptive lead, chaining Sleep Powder and Yawn to put multiple opposing Pokemon to sleep early.

It's a dual-mega team, with Mega Aerodactyl running a little HP investment for extra survivability alongside its usual speed-control role. Garchomp and Basculegion round out the back line as straightforward attackers.
