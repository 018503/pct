## Strategy

A dual-mega team pairing Mega Charizard-Y with Mega Glimmora, plus Weavile as a unique support pick. Created by J Modri, who finished 34th in the ranked season, this build was one of the earliest examples of the Whimsicott + Glimmora core, with Weavile added specifically to cover matchups Glimmora struggles with — ground types like Garchomp are the obvious issue for Glimmora, and Weavile handles them so Glimmora can sweep afterward.

Basculegion runs a Colbur Berry here to improve the Kingambit matchup, which otherwise can trap and Sucker Punch it in the endgame — the Citrus (Sitrus) Berry Garchomp continues a season-long trend of bulkier Garchomp builds. Glimmora itself is bulkier than players might expect, largely because the team runs Tailwind support from Whimsicott: Charizard, Basculegion, and Garchomp all carry noticeable bulk investment rather than pure Speed, since Tailwind is expected to be up most games. Charizard specifically has physical investment to better tank Rock-type attacks.

Notably, Mega Glimmora remains a fully functional Pokemon even without mega evolving, so games exist where Glimmora is played alongside an already-mega-evolved Charizard. Between the Weavile tech and the dual-mega Charizard/Glimmora core, this build showcases why regular (non-mega) Glimmora usage has also been trending upward across the format.
