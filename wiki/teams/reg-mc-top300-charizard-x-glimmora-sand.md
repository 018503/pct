## Strategy

This sand team reached the top 300 of the Season M-6 ladder. It bluffs dual weather: opponents see Charizard and expect Y, but it is Mega Charizard X. Charizard X's Fire/Dragon typing removes a fourth Water weakness from a team that already has Tyranitar, Excadrill and Glimmora. Its surprise Dragon Claw can OHKO the Garchomp that threatens the team's three Ground-weak members. The Passho Berry Tyranitar is non-mega and bulky (32 HP, 8 SpD, plus the sand SpD boost). It can stay in on Muddy Water and threaten Swift Swim users.

Modes: **sand offence** with Tyranitar + Excadrill, with Glimmora's spread Sludge Wave safe next to the Steel types. **Corviknight Tailwind / Bulk Up**: the old OG Champions set (Brave Bird, Bulk Up, Tailwind, Roost). Corviknight is hard to remove now that Gholdengo usage is falling, and Excadrill removes the Raichu and Archaludon that threaten it. **Kommo-o Clangorous Soul**: Fairies are hard to bring into Excadrill, Corviknight, Glimmora and Charizard, which opens the door for a Kommo-o sweep. Charizard X is the answer to Garchomp + Charizard leads, since opponents usually lock into Rock Slide on Charizard.

James's verdict: "overall team was really strong". He missed Flare Blitz's power and doubted Flame Charge, because +1 Charizard X still doesn't outspeed base 120s, and being slower than Basculegion outside sand hurt.
