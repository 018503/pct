## Strategy

Built around Perish Song Mega Gengar, complemented by an offensive Calm Mind / Draining Kiss Floette so the team has two distinct game plans. The defensive plan leans on Incineroar, Gengar, Maushold, and Sinistcha to stall out a Perish Song and win from there — Soundproof Kommo-o pairs especially well with Gengar since it's immune to its own team's Perish Song, letting it set up Clangorous Soul while the Perish Song timer runs. The offensive plan instead centers Floette, with Incineroar / Floette / Sinistcha forming a hard-to-break defensive-into-offensive core: Fake Out and an early Calm Mind, then Sinistcha comes in for healing, while Incineroar slows the opponent down with Will-O-Wisp, Intimidate, and Parting Shot.

Maushold is a strong partner for Floette, providing Follow Me redirection so Floette can safely set up Calm Mind, then Super Fang to chip an opponent's HP down before finishing with Draining Kiss or Dazzling Gleam. The team is naturally very bulky as a result — Gengar, Sinistcha, Maushold, Floette, and Incineroar all run minimal offensive investment and lean fully into survivability. Kommo-o is the one exception, built for a faster, more explosive game plan around Clangorous Soul into Clanging Scales.

The key selling point of the team is that it doesn't hard-commit to either plan — it can lean on the Incineroar / Maushold / Sinistcha Perish trap support core, or lead with Floette as the primary attacker, with Kommo-o flexible enough to support either approach.
