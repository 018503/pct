## Strategy

James's recreation of a 55th-place Global Challenge team. Mega Raichu X became a sleeper pick in the best-of-one, closed-team-sheet Global Challenge. It was weak in Reg MB, but its Electric Surge pairs with Electric Seed Espathra: the seed gives +1 Defense in Electric Terrain, and Speed Boost adds +1 Speed every turn. Raichu X provides what Espathra teams were missing, a mega that supports with Fake Out, Reflect and Light Screen. Before mega, Lightning Rod redirects Electric moves.

Main mode: Raichu X + Espathra lead. Fake Out and screens let Espathra Calm Mind safely, then Baton Pass the boosts into Archaludon. Politoed sets rain for Electro Shot and can Psych Up to copy Espathra's or Archaludon's boosts. The second mode is a surprise sun burst: Charizard Y with Helping Hand plus Life Orb Chlorophyll Whimsicott (Moonblast / Solar Beam / Psychic). Helping Hand Solar Beam OHKOs Mega Raichu Y, and Helping Hand Moonblast OHKOs Mega Salamence.

Speed notes: Espathra outspeeds Hisuian Arcanine before any boost, and after one boost it outspeeds the Z megas, Mega Aerodactyl and Choice Scarf base 90s. Politoed speed-creeps Focus Sash Kingambit and Incineroar so it can attack before Parting Shot. James mixed up the Charizard partway through recording; the paste spread is the accurate one.
