## Strategy

The wildest team in the video and arguably the coolest of the rank season. The creator is known as the most creative high-ladder builder from Japan — previously brought Golisopod/Raichu and Groudon + Kyogre to Worlds top cut.

Core concept: stack poison (Glimmora's Mortal Spin + Toxic Debris) and sand (Hippowdon) for massive residual damage. Opponents lose chunks of HP every turn from sand + poison combined. Gholdengo runs Substitute + Focus Blast (no Make It Rain) — subs up as Kingambit tries Sucker Punch, Focus Blast OHKOs Kingambit and Incineroar.

Mega Staraptor comes out late game once opponents are at 50-60% from residual damage — Close Combat gets KOs and stacks Contrary defense boosts for a sweep. Tyrantrum (a Pokemon most people don't even know is in Champions) runs Choice Scarf with Strong Jaw-boosted Ice Fang to OHKO Garchomp. Life Orb Mimikyu with Phantom Force provides burst damage behind Disguise.

Not recommended for beginners — requires precise positioning. But completely unlike anything else in high ladder.
