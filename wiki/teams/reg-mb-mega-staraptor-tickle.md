## Strategy

CybertronVGC's own day-one build, kept deliberately simple and easy to pilot. Contrary Mega Staraptor turns normally-negative moves into self-boosts: Tickle drops the target's Attack and Defense but boosts Staraptor's own Attack and Defense, and Fake Tears gives an immediate +2 Special Defense. With a great base 110 Speed and huge Attack, Staraptor can sustain fights and heal back up with Roost even without any bulk investment of its own, since it can boost its own defenses on demand.

The preferred lead is Whimsicott and Staraptor. Gholdengo covers the Fairy-types that otherwise give Staraptor trouble, and Choice Scarf Typhlosion-Hisui is the team's dedicated answer to the two Pokemon Staraptor genuinely struggles to threaten — Froslass and Mega Metagross, both of which hit back hard without fearing an immediate knockout. Typhlosion is also immune to Fake Out and, with Frisk, scouts the opponent's held item in Best-of-One.

Spreads elsewhere are mostly max/max for damage. Basculegion carries a little HP and Defense specifically to survive Sucker Punch from a non-Black Glasses Kingambit, and Garchomp's small Defense investment helps against rival Dragon Claw users.
