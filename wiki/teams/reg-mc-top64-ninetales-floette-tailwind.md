## Strategy

Ruuto's Global Challenge Top 64 team, which also reached the top 100 on the ranked ladder. It is a Tailwind hyper offence built from underused picks: Kanto Ninetales, Choice Scarf Basculegion-F and Glimmora alongside Mega Floette-Eternal. Prankster Whimsicott sets Tailwind or Encores, and Fairy Aura Floette's Moonblast / Dazzling Gleam / Light of Ruin destroys anything without a fairy resist.

Ninetales does three jobs. Drought helps a lot against rain. It is a Fire type faster than Gholdengo, so Floette + Ninetales Heat Wave covers Gholdengo and Rillaboom. Its Fire coverage handles the steels that wall Floette. Scorching Sands covers Incineroar. Glimmora's Toxic Debris lays Toxic Spikes when hit physically, and that chip changes calcs. Female Basculegion is popular among top Japanese players: Adaptability Shadow Ball for Gholdengo, Ice Beam for dragons (especially Salamence) and water STAB that ignores Intimidate.

Leads: Whimsicott + Floette is the strongest (double fairy damage KOs without a resist). Whimsicott + Glimmora is a classic from Reg MA. Whimsicott + Ninetales gets Moonblast + Heat Wave immediately. Basculegion leads into Mega Salamence + Fake Out to Ice Beam it before Tailwind. Indeedee leads into psychic spam and Trick Room, where Imprison blocks Expanding Force, Trick Room and Hyper Voice. Against slow teams, lead two attackers. The plan is to deny opposing speed control and get fast KOs.
