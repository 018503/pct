## Strategy

A Sejun Park creation (the 2014 World Champion) built entirely around protecting whichever mega evolution you pick — Mega Delphox or Mega Floette-Eternal, chosen based on the opponent's team. Get boosts off with Nasty Plot or Calm Mind and let the chosen sweeper close the game out. Because there's no offense outside of the mega, the whole game hinges on how well that mega evolution is protected.

Delphox matches up especially well into opposing Mega Charizard, which generally struggles to deal much damage to it while Delphox Nasty Plots in front and threatens huge damage in return. The support cast enables whichever mega is chosen from every angle: Fake Out from Incineroar, Friend Guard from Maushold, Feint on Maushold to break through Protect (useful for landing Delphox's Heat Wave), Sleep Powder from Vivillon, and Sinistcha for pivot-healing.

Both sweepers are built to actually outrun the format — Mega Delphox is one of the fastest Pokemon around, and a +2 Psychic from it is enough to knock out a standard Garchomp. Floette-Eternal here is instead super physically defensive to maximize its Draining Kiss healing, while still running modest max speed like most Calm Mind Floette in the format. Vivillon can take a Dual Wingbeat from Talonflame and is otherwise max speed — enough to outrun even max-speed Mega Aerodactyl, a useful benchmark since the rest of the team is fairly slow and defensive by design.
