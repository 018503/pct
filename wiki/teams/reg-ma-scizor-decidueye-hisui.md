## Strategy

A really interesting team featuring four different non-meta picks like Aurorus, Hisuian Decidueye, Hisuian Zoroark, and Liepard recently reached Champions tier and I had to try it out! This is the most unique team I've seen so far in Champions tier, and you can use uncommon picks to really surprise opponents and get knockouts.
