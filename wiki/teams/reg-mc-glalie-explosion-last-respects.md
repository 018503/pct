## Strategy

This team gives Mega Glalie a job: blow up. Refrigerate turns Explosion into a huge spread Ice move. **Glalie + Medicham** is the main lead. Medicham's Telepathy makes it immune to its ally's Explosion. It adds Fake Out, Feint (to break Protect) or Helping Hand so Explosion lands on the right target. The alternative is Whimsicott's Tailwind followed by Explosion.

The back line turns the early faints into power. Basculegion's Last Respects gains 50 BP for every fainted ally, and with Adaptability it can reach 250 BP. Pawmot has Fake Out support, and its Revival Blessing can bring Glalie back for a second Explosion. Archaludon (Sturdy, White Herb Draco Meteor, Aura Sphere for Kingambit) is the flex slot. Spreads are simple max/max. Whimsicott carries Mental Herb against opposing Encore. Medicham is max Speed so it can Close Combat first.

Game 1 (into a Charizard / Garchomp / Incineroar team) showed the plan. Glalie dodged a Heat Wave, Feint + Explosion removed Charizard, then Fake Out + Last Respects and Close Combat closed it. Sash on Basculegion, not Pawmot, fooled the opponent's Fake Out. James's verdict: "not the best team in the world, but definitely a fun one". Fast Tailwind mirrors are a problem, and Unburden Sneasler next to Rillaboom's Grassy Glide double-ups is the hardest matchup.
