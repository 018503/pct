## Strategy

Andreas Wilkerson finished 13th (Top 16 cut) of 1,000+ players at the Baltimore Regional Championships, the first official Regulation MC major, with this dual mega team. Mega Lopunny is its best-ever Champions tournament result. Scrappy Close Combat threatens one-hit KOs on Incineroar, Gholdengo, Mega Raichu Y and Mega Froslass, and Triple Axel hits Dragons like Salamence and Garchomp. Fake Out from Lopunny or Sneasler enables the setup sweepers: Swords Dance Ceruledge and Dragon Dance Life Orb Inner Focus Dragonite.

The team has two modes. The fast mode leads Lopunny + Dragonite (Fake Out into Dragon Dance), or Lopunny + Indeedee-F (Psychic Terrain blocks opposing Fake Out/priority so Lopunny gets two Close Combats in), with Ceruledge as the flexible third and Sneasler or Dragonite as the fourth. The slow mode is Indeedee-F + Mega Gardevoir into Trick Room and slow special-heavy teams: Follow Me into Calm Mind, then boosted spread damage. Sneasler + Indeedee-F leads trigger Psychic Seed for instant Unburden speed, and Dire Claw punishes teams that lead fairies to counter Lopunny.

Watchouts: only Gardevoir has Protect, and Triple Axel, Poltergeist, Scale Shot and Rock Slide can all miss, so games swing on hit/miss and lead positioning. Tough matchups: Hisuian Arcanine (Sash), Mega Metagross (Steel Roller), Mega Swampert in rain, plus Choice Scarf and Focus Sash users. Intimidate on a not-yet-mega Lopunny and Fairy/Psychic types are its worst enemies; Mega Gardevoir dislikes Steel types and Wide Guard.
