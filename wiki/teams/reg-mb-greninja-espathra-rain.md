## Strategy

Mega Greninja has 142 base Speed (outspeeding most Megas) but weak special attack and Protean, which can backfire in doubles. The team keeps the pre-mega Torrent and Protect so it keeps both types and STABs, then uses Hydro Pump, Dark Pulse and Water Shuriken (multi-hit, ideal with Archaludon's Stamina) in rain.

Greninja plus Focus Sash Speed Boost Espathra is the main lead: Lumina Crash drops Special Defence by two stages so Greninja can KO with a double, and Rain Dance on Espathra helps win the weather war against Charizard's Mega-triggered sun. Pelipper (Drizzle, Tailwind, Wide Guard) and Leftovers Archaludon (Electro Shot in rain) provide the core; Scizor Technician Bug Bite/Bullet Punch and Choice Scarf Annihilape (Rage Fist after Water Shuriken, U-turn to Pelipper) cover Fairies, Farigiraf and Incineroar.

The team usually double protects on the first turn in Greninja's base form and wants to avoid the Protean type change from clicking Protect or non-Water moves first.
