## Strategy

The team centres around a Swagger Mega Banette + Lum Berry Sneasler lead that can be brought in roughly 90% of games. Banette mega-evolves for Prankster, clicks Swagger onto its own Sneasler to grant +2 Attack, and the Lum Berry clears confusion while Unburden doubles Sneasler's Speed. From there, Sneasler threatens OHKOs on most of the format with Close Combat, Throat Chop (for ghost types like Sinistcha, Basculegion, and Gholdengo), and Rock Slide (for Charizard). Banette contributes heavy damage with uninvested Poltergeist and can trade itself via Destiny Bond.

The back half of the team revolves around Politoed's Perish Song &mdash; after Sneasler and Banette pick up quick KOs, Politoed enters and forces the endgame clock. Hypnosis provides a bail-out in tricky perish situations despite 60% accuracy. Corviknight walls physical megas like Staraptor and Floette in the late game and becomes a win condition with Iron Defense + Body Press. Archaludon runs Snarl over Dragon Pulse for survivability and rain-synergy with Politoed, while Sylveon provides a slow, bulky fairy option with Fairy Feather Hyper Voice for Trick Room matchups.

The main variance comes from Swagger's 85% accuracy &mdash; a miss often costs Sneasler since it lacks bulk. Redirection (Sinistcha), Focus Sash Pokemon, and double-targeting into Sneasler are the primary counterplay. Pre-mega Frisk provides valuable item scouting in best-of-one ladder.
