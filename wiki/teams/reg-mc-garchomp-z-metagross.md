## Strategy

A dual-mega team pairing Mega Metagross with Mega Garchomp Z as a secondary mega that patches specific matchups. Built by 2024 World's finalist Yuta Ishigaki, the core idea is that Garchomp Z, Metagross, and Volcarona each cover different threats — you identify which one is strongest into the opposing team and build your game plan around it. Garchomp Z's incredible base 151 speed and special coverage let it outpace and one-shot threats that trouble Metagross and Volcarona: Draco Meteor demolishes Mega Salamence before it moves, Power Gem snipes Mega Charizard Y, and Flamethrower melts Steel types like Gholdengo and Kingambit.

Volcarona acts as a hidden third mega evolution, sweeping with Quiver Dance when opponents lack Rock or Flying coverage. Grassy Seed from Rillaboom's terrain gives it an immediate Defense boost, and Giga Drain heals in conjunction with Grassy Terrain recovery. Triple Fake Out support from Incineroar, Rillaboom, and Sneasler provides incredible positioning flexibility — you can cycle fake outs across turns to deny attacks while your megas set up or attack. Coaching on Sneasler immediately boosts Metagross's Attack and Defense for games where you need immediate power without setup time.

The team requires patience and careful positioning. Games often run 10-15 minutes as you battle for position with triple Fake Out cycling. Garchomp Z excels when threatening super effective one-hit knockouts but feels underwhelming when opponents resist its coverage. Fairy types are Garchomp's biggest weakness, which is exactly why Metagross is on the team. The key skill is identifying in team preview which of the three offensive threats to prioritize and how to best enable them.
