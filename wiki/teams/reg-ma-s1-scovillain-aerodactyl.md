## Strategy

Finished 4th in the ranked season, pairing Mega Aerodactyl with Mega Scovillain — a dual-mega combination that was already trending upward in the metagame, so its strong finish wasn't a huge surprise. The build's standout techs sit on three of the most common Pokemon in the format: Garchomp, Sylveon, and Kingambit. Garchomp skips Protect entirely, running Rock Tomb and Stomping Tantrum alongside Poison Jab for extra coverage into Fairy types like Floette and Sylveon that would otherwise trouble it, while carrying enough HP/Def investment plus Sitrus Berry to stay bulky despite still being fairly fast.

Sylveon runs Calm Mind and a heavily physically-defensive spread to tank Sneasler's Dire Claw, with Mega Scovillain's Rage Powder providing the redirection needed to set up safely before spamming Hyper Voice. Kingambit is unusually fast here, built to take advantage of Tailwind and running four attacking moves rather than Protect — and it carries Supreme Overlord instead of Defiant, a shift some players are making as Incineroar usage (which punishes Defiant) declines, in favor of Supreme Overlord's more consistent value.

Rounding out the team is the classic Focus Sash Basculegion, which the video notes is showing up on a huge share of top teams this season. Overall the build leans on double-mega pressure from Aerodactyl and Scovillain plus some of the strongest non-megas in the game, run with unconventional tweaks.
