## Strategy

Finished top 5 in both this and the previous rank season, yet has flown under the radar. The key is the flexibility between two powerful special mega evolutions that cover each other's weaknesses.

Froslass-Mega excels against rain teams with weather control (Snow Warning) and Aurora Veil. Dragonite-Mega is incredibly hard to KO with Multiscale (both before and after mega) — Dragon Pulse + Heat Wave covers dragons and steels. Fairy types threaten Dragonite but are handled by Sneasler (Dire Claw), Garchomp (Poison Jab), and Kingambit (Iron Head).

Feint on Sneasler is an amazing best-of-one tech — breaks Protect, enabling Dragonite to OHKO Whimsicott with Heat Wave on turn one, putting you up 4-3 before Tailwind is even set.

Kingambit has speed investment to outpace opposing Kingambits and leverage Tailwind. Four-attack Chople Berry set thrives in best-of-one ladder where versatility matters.
