## Strategy

The highest-placing Mega Lopunny team in the video, featuring a mixed Dragonite running Dragon Pulse and Air Slash instead of the more common rain-oriented set, plus a Choice Scarf Sturdy Archaludon — a build that inspired a separate meta-trends video on Choice Scarf Archaludon. The team leans on double-mega pressure from Lopunny and Dragonite: Lopunny can one-shot targets with Giga Impact or Close Combat while disrupting with Encore, and Dragonite runs max Special Attack and max Speed with Extreme Speed as a priority finisher — for example Air Slash into a Whimsicott, then Extreme Speed to clean up, despite the minus-Attack nature.

Dragonite's set is a deliberate departure from the usual rain-team Dragonite (Hurricane / Draco Meteor / Tailwind): Inner Focus grants immunity to Fake Out before mega evolving, and Multiscale kicks in once it mega evolves, giving good coverage into Grass types like Snorlax and Mega Venusaur via Air Slash. Sylveon runs its usual defensive spread to survive Sneasler's Dire Claw while still threatening a one-hit knockout with Hyper Beam, and Talonflame provides additional speed control.

As a hyper-offensive build, nearly every Pokemon besides Sylveon maximizes both Speed and Attack (or Special Attack), reflecting a team designed to move first and end games quickly. The Mega Lopunny, the unconventional Dragonite set, and the Choice Scarf Archaludon are the standout elements that set this team apart from other Dragonite builds seen this season.
