## Strategy

Hippowdon has been rising in Reg MC alongside **Mega Raichu Y**, because the format has very few Ground types. This team took it to top 16 at Nice Regionals, its first regional top cut. The core is **Shadow Tag Mega Gengar + Yawn Hippowdon**. Gengar traps the opponent, Yawn makes them drowsy, and since they can't switch they fall asleep. Gengar uses Substitute / Protect to stall that turn safely. Sand also gives Gengar a safer matchup into Charizard sun and Basculegion rain.

The main mode slows the game down. Fake Out from Incineroar or Rillaboom, Parting Shot and **Snarl** from Incineroar, Yawn from Hippowdon, and Shadow Ball / Sludge Bomb chip from Gengar. Then Life Orb Rillaboom and Grassy Terrain finish while healing Hippowdon. There is also a straight **sand mode** with Hippowdon + Sand Rush Excadrill, and a **Kommo-o setup mode**: double Fake Out cover for Clangorous Soul, with Grassy Terrain recovery.

**Role:** Snarl / Parting Shot pivot
