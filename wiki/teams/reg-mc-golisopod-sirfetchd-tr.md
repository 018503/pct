## Strategy

This team pairs Sirfetch'd with Mega Golisopod as a complementary offensive core under Trick Room. Sirfetch'd's Scrappy ability lets it ignore Intimidate from Incineroar and hit Ghost-types like Gholdengo with Close Combat for OHKOs, covering the steel and fire types that wall Golisopod. With the Leek boosting crit rate, random critical hits on Close Combat give massive extra damage even into neutral bulky targets. Meteor Assault provides a nuclear option to close out games. Farigiraf sets Trick Room with Armor Tail blocking priority, and Incineroar leads alongside it to neutralize physical attackers with Intimidate and Parting Shot before pivoting out.

The default lead is Incineroar + Farigiraf with Golisopod and Sirfetch'd in the back, used in 80-90% of games. Turn one is typically Parting Shot + Trick Room, then the sweepers enter under Trick Room to deal massive single-target damage. Feint on Sirfetch'd breaks through Protect to let Golisopod secure KOs. Sucker Punch on Golisopod is a key tech — it provides dark-type coverage against Gholdengo and Mega Metagross, and allows Golisopod to threaten KOs even after Trick Room ends. The Adamant (not Brave) nature on Golisopod means it can sometimes capitalize on Tailwind, though 60 base speed limits this.

The secondary mode is Whimsicott + Glimmora under Tailwind, primarily for fire-type-heavy matchups like Mega Charizard teams. Sirfetch'd at 97 speed reaches 194 under Tailwind, outspeeding Sneasler, Mega Froslass, and common speed creeps at the 95 benchmark. Mega Glimmora's Adaptability-boosted Power Gem 4x OHKOs Charizard. However, the Tailwind mode is more situational — Golisopod barely benefits from it, and Glimmora's single-target damage can feel underwhelming without super-effective hits.

**Moves:** Meteor Assault · Close Combat · Feint · Detect

**Role:** Primary sweeper and Golisopod's perfect partner

Sirfetch'd covers the two biggest problems for Mega Golisopod: fire types and steel types. Scrappy Close Combat OHKOs Incineroar and Gholdengo while ignoring Intimidate, and Meteor Assault nukes anything that doesn't resist Fighting. The Leek gives boosted crit chance, and with crits Close Combat nearly OHKOs even neutral bulky targets. Feint breaks Protect under Trick Room to let Golisopod secure KOs. The 12 speed points hit 97, reaching 194 under Tailwind to outspeed Sneasler and Mega Froslass plus common speed creeps at 95. Under Trick Room it's fast enough to sometimes be outsped by very slow Pokemon, so watch the speed tier against opposing TR teams.

**Role:** Mega sweeper — Trick Room win condition

Mega Golisopod is the main win condition, sweeping under Trick Room with Tough Claws-boosted Leech Life and Iron Head. Sucker Punch is the standout tech — it provides dark-type coverage against Gholdengo and Mega Metagross that otherwise wall the Bug/Steel typing, and lets Golisopod threaten KOs even outside Trick Room. Max HP investment gives 182 HP for Leech Life recovery. The 4 SpD points shore up special defense slightly. Adamant over Brave means it can technically use Tailwind, though 60 base speed is still very slow. Be careful switching in pre-mega — without the Steel typing, it's vulnerable to fire and flying attacks.

**Moves:** Twin Beam · Thunderbolt · Trick Room · Protect

**Role:** Trick Room setter + pivot

Farigiraf is the primary Trick Room setter with Armor Tail blocking priority moves like Fake Out. The Bold spread with 31 HP / 24 Def is very physically defensive, helping survive hits from Basculegion and Kingambit. Twin Beam over Psychic lets it break Focus Sash on Sneasler with a single attack. Thunderbolt provides coverage against Basculegion switching in and hits water types. Sitrus Berry helps conserve HP so Farigiraf can set a second Trick Room later — getting two Trick Rooms is often necessary since the team relies on single-target damage and may not close out games in one set of Trick Room turns.
