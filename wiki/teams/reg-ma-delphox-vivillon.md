## Strategy

2014 World Champion Sejun Park is back with another brilliant team! The Korea National Championships recently took place, and Sejun went 8-1 with this "protect the king" style team, designed around enabling Nasty Plot Delphox and Calm Mind Floette to their full potential. With 3 redirection users in Maushold, Sinistcha, and Vivillion as well as an Incineroar, it can be incredibly difficult for teams to break through and do enough damage while your Mega carries you to victory!
