## Strategy

Empoleon is James's favourite Pokemon, but Charizard, Sneasler, Kingambit and Garchomp make it hard to use. He rebuilt this from a team he faced on stream. The plan: boost **Delphox** (Nasty Plot) or **Mega Staraptor** (Whimsicott **Charm** on its own Contrary Staraptor = +2 Atk), then bring in **Empoleon** to **Psych Up** the boosts. Competitive also punishes Intimidate.

The main leads are **Whimsicott + Staraptor** (Charm, then +2 Close Combat / Brave Bird) and **Indeedee + Delphox**. Regular Delphox stays grounded (no Levitate), so it gets the Psychic Seed SpD boost and the terrain-boosted Psychic. The fallback is Trick Room. Lead Indeedee with Staraptor or Delphox, sacrifice the partner, set Trick Room, and sweep with Life Orb Drampa.
