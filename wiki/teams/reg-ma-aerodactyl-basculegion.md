## Strategy

A team with Ariados just won the 1st Global Challenge in Pokemon Champions! Ariados is a niche pick that can be used to support Sylveon specifically, protecting it from powerful single target attacks like Kingambit's Iron Head & Sneasler's Dire Claw with Rage Powder. To see Ariados in action, check out games 3, 4, and 5! This team also features some of the strongest meta shifts right now, including Mega Aerodactyl, Focus Sash Basculegion, and Fairy Feather Sylveon.
