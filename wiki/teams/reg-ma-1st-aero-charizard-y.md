## Strategy

This is the team that won the entire tournament, taking 1st place out of over 6,000 entrants with a 19-1 record (a 95% win rate), piloted by Ragoff. The core is built around Aerodactyl and Mega Charizard-Y, with Charizard as the primary mega and star attacker. Wide Guard on Aerodactyl protects Charizard from the prevalence of Rock Slide and other spread moves in the format, while Aerodactyl's own Tailwind and Rock Slide let it set up and chip away at the same time. This Charizard is bulkier than average, carrying real HP and Defense investment so it can survive things like Rock Slide from an Adamant Garchomp.

The standout tech is Dragon Fang Scale Shot Garchomp — a set that wasn't widely used before this run and is expected to rise in popularity after this result. After a Scale Shot speed boost, this Garchomp outpaces virtually everything in the format short of Mega Aerodactyl; investing a bit more into Speed (28-29 points) even lets it edge out mirror Adamant Garchomp. Choice Scarf Basculegion and a Chople Berry Kingambit round out the back half as clean-up options, while Floette-Eternal is a flexible mega that can be brought in unmegaevolved against Dragon-types that resist Charizard, or mega evolved for an immediate Light of Ruin one-shot on targets that aren't maximally defensive.

Nothing here looks unusual on paper — it's mostly format staples — but the execution (Wide Guard support, the bulky Charizard spread, and the Scale Shot Garchomp tech) is what elevated it to a championship-winning team.
