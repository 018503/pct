## Strategy

Mega Golisopod gets huge stats (175 Defence, 120 Special Defence, 150 Attack), Tough Claws on its contact moves and a Bug/Steel typing that is only weak to Fire. The Swords Dance set uses Leech Life with grassy terrain recovery and Iron Head for Fairies, making it very hard to remove if the opponent has no Fire type.

Pelipper (Drizzle, Wide Guard to stop Heat Wave) and Leftovers Archaludon (Stamina, Electro Shot with no charge turn in rain) form the weather core. Rillaboom and Pawmot give Fake Out to set up Golisopod and Grassy Seed Farigiraf, whose Armor Tail and +1 Defence let it Nasty Plot and Hyper Voice through bulky teams or Trick Room.

James expected it to be hard against Charizard but found it strong into most of the format. It struggles against Gholdengo (hence High Horsepower on Rillaboom) and loses on a Sucker Punch Golisopod mirror because Sucker Punch ignores Golisopod's setup.
