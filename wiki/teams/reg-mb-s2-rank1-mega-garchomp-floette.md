## Strategy

Classic Incineroar / Floette / Sinistcha trio from Regulation MA, paired with a flexible Mega Garchomp. The mega stone makes Garchomp harder to KO and hit harder than Life Orb variants. Coaching Sneasler can boost Garchomp offensively and defensively, or give Floette a defense boost. Double Fake Out (Incineroar + Sneasler) protects Floette's setup turns while Sinistcha offers Rage Powder redirection.

Leftovers on Incineroar (Sitrus is taken by Sinistcha) gives it long-term sustain. Helping Hand amplifies Floette's damage — a Helping Hand + 2x Calm Mind Draining Kiss can nuke targets while healing back. Kingambit runs Chople Berry without Low Kick; opposing Kingambit is handled by Garchomp, Sneasler, and Floette instead.
