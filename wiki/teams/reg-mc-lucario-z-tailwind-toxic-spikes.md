## Strategy

This is a team James Baek built himself around Mega Lucario Z, which has base 151 Speed and 164 Special Attack and gains Aura Guard, halving damage from contact moves. Talonflame provides Tailwind, Glimmora sets toxic spikes when hit by a physical attack, and Rillaboom (Grassy Terrain) adds Fake Out for a Nasty Plot Lucario. The idea is to chip opponents down with toxic spikes and Tailwind damage so they fall into range of Scarf Basculegion and Lucario.

Lucario is max HP and max Speed: the Speed outruns Mega Aerodactyl (and threatens it with Flash Cannon) and most other Mega Z forms, which usually do not run max Speed, and the HP lets it live Close Combat from Sneasler and Flare Blitz from Incineroar. Hydreigon is a Chople Berry Taunt set with Levitate that exists for Gholdengo, Basculegion and Trick Room matchups: Talonflame's Quick Guard blocks Fake Out and Taunt stops Trick Room from Farigiraf, after which Dark Pulse can double up on it. Glimmora also resists Fairy and can switch into Fairy attacks.

Leads: Lucario + Basculegion for immediate fast pressure; Lucario + Rillaboom for Fake Out into Nasty Plot then Talonflame Tailwind; Glimmora for toxic spikes; and Talonflame + Hydreigon for Tailwind, Draco Meteor pressure and Quick Guard + Taunt against Trick Room leads. James notes the Talonflame Brave Bird set gives up dual Wing Beats for power, especially against Staraptor.
