## Strategy

The highest-ranking public Mega Mawile team from the previous ranked season (Season 4), and the only Mega Mawile in the top 100. This dual mega pairing exploits both Tailwind and Trick Room — most Mega Mawile teams force it into Trick Room only, but this Mawile has enough speed investment (15 points, 85 Spe) to outspeed max speed Jolly Garchomp and everything slower under Tailwind. This catches opponents off guard since they associate Mawile with Trick Room exclusively.

The team flexes between modes: Aerodactyl + Mawile or Sylveon leads pressure with Tailwind, while Farigiraf + Blastoise leads fake out into Trick Room. Mawile comes out in roughly 70% of games, Blastoise in 30%. Key leads include Aerodactyl + Sylveon (flexible — scout opponent, then bring the right mega from the back), Aerodactyl + Mawile (punishes teams without speed control — Mawile OHKOs Floette, Whimsicott, Sylveon, Mega Aerodactyl), and Blastoise + Farigiraf (fake out + Trick Room, then clean with Garchomp or Aerodactyl). Garchomp + Mawile is also strong — Garchomp scares away fire types, clearing the path for Mawile's sucker punch pressure.

Farigiraf's offensive Energy Ball is the surprise tech — opponents don't respect its damage output, but it OHKOs Mega Swampert and threatens bulky Water types that otherwise wall the team. The tradeoff is significantly less HP/SpD than standard Farigiraf, so it cannot survive Hyper Beam from Sylveon or Weather Ball from Charizard. Sylveon at max SpA/max Spe outspeeds Hippowdon without Tailwind and basically everything with it. Blastoise runs Water Pulse over Water Spout for consistent damage — opponents panic expecting Shell Smash + Water Spout but get punished by triple coverage (Dark Pulse into Gholdengo/Basculegion, Aura Sphere into Kingambit/Archaludon). Biggest weaknesses: bulky Water types (Milotic Coil especially), fire/ground types pressuring Mawile, Play Rough/Rock Slide/Hyper Beam miss RNG, and Choice Scarf Pokemon that outspeed Mawile even under Tailwind.
