## Strategy

Same creator as the [Rank #2 Mega Staraptor/Scizor](reg-mb-s2-rank2-mega-staraptor-scizor.md) team. Yanagi reached Rank #1 during the season with this team before finishing Rank #2 with the Staraptor build. This team put Special Mega Garchomp on the radar — previously not considered viable.

Special Mega Garchomp with sand (Tyranitar) buffs Earth Power and Power Gem. Threatens OHKOs on Kingambit, Gholdengo, Mega Charizard, and opposing dragons. Garchomp's 138 Spe outspeeds Modest max speed Kommo-o. Scizor, Sneasler, and Basculegion carry over as aggressive offensive cores from Yanagi's other builds.

Corviknight is a key anti-meta pick — extremely difficult for Mega Staraptor teams to deal with. Super specially defensive with a plus-defense nature, it walls Staraptor's Close Combat while resisting Brave Bird. Choice Scarf Tyranitar provides immediate sand + max speed max attack for burst damage.
