## Strategy

This is the third WhimsiZard variant in the video. Top player Michael Kelsch swaps Garchomp for **Mega Salamence**, a fast, powerful secondary mega that handles Rillaboom and Sneasler. Salamence fell off once Garchomp Z rose, but with Garchomp Z quieter it is coming back. The spreads differ from the Carson St. Denis version almost everywhere. **Charizard** is slower and even more physically bulky, and survives Life Orb Garchomp Rock Slide. **Basculegion** is Jolly max speed rather than Adamant. **Sneasler** is max HP with Gunk Shot + Rock Slide, so Sneasler + Salamence can double into one target for a KO. **Kingambit** has more Def and one speed point.

Michael's combo: Salamence Hyper Voice + Whimsicott Moonblast KOs Indeedee-F at very favourable odds. Cybertron says all three WhimsiZard variants are popular right now.
