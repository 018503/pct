## Strategy

This hard Trick Room team finished 252nd in the last ranked ladder season (Season M-6, Champion tier). Its main idea is regular (Kanto) Zoroark. Illusion disguises it as your last chosen Pokemon, usually Farigiraf. Opponents spend their first turn attacking "Farigiraf" to stop Trick Room, which gives Zoroark free turns. Then the real Farigiraf sets Trick Room later, often after Zoroark goes down. Zoroark is Dark type, so it is immune to Expanding Force. Snarl weakens the strong special attackers (Charizard, Gardevoir), and Throat Chop stops Hyper Voice, Perish Song and Parting Shot.

James's favourite lead is **Zoroark + Camerupt**. Sunny Day plus an attack turns opposing rain Weather Balls off, and Camerupt's Heat Wave / Earth Power does huge damage. The straightforward lead is **Farigiraf + Rillaboom**: Fake Out + Trick Room, then U-turn into Camerupt or Sylveon. Basculegion can also Flip Turn while Farigiraf sets Trick Room. Farigiraf or Zoroark leads almost every game. In game 1, a Zoroark + Sylveon lead into Farigiraf + Charizard Y won thanks to Snarl + Protect, and the opponent's Psychic went into the disguised Zoroark.

James's verdict: Zoroark "was actually doing a ton of work". The Sunny Day and Snarl tech stood out, and the team plays well into psyspam.

**Moves:** Heat Wave · Earth Power · Ancient Power · Protect

**Role:** Sheer Force Trick Room sweeper

Before mega evolving, Solid Rock reduces super effective damage. Mega Camerupt's Sheer Force boosts Heat Wave, Earth Power and Ancient Power (for Pelipper and Charizard). It is max HP / 30 SpA Quiet, and the 4 Def points let it live Mega Golisopod's Liquidation in sun. It is one of the slowest Pokemon in the game, and the Fire/Ground plus Sylveon's Fairy coverage is very hard to switch into.
