## Strategy

James built this for the start of the new ladder season around **Mega Dragalge**. Mega Dragalge has base 163 SpD and gains **Regenerator**, which gives it a Toxapex-like role with useful Dragon typing. It takes on Floette-Eternal and Charizard and threatens OHKOs on Garchomp and Mega Salamence with Draco Meteor. The partner is **Araquanid**. **Infestation** traps targets, and **Soak** turns Steel and Poison types (Kingambit, opposing Toxapex) into pure Water so **Toxic** works on them. Soak can also go on your own Dragalge to give it Water typing defensively.

**Hippowdon** is the weather control against rain. James first considered Ninetales or Vanilluxe, but they added another Rock weakness and a bad Gholdengo matchup. Sand chip plus Toxic plus Infestation adds up. Yawn sends one target to sleep while the other gets Toxic-stalled. Rillaboom, Incineroar and Mega Delphox round out the team.
