## Strategy

Mega Gyarados has seen very little play so far in Regulation MA in Pokemon Champions, but it recently just got Top 16 at Indianpolis Regionals. While it struggles as a solo Mega in the format, it's a fantastic secondary option next to Calm Mind Mega Floette. It can sweep teams by setting up Dragon Dance, especially when combined with Coaching Sneasler, and can function as a non-Mega in battles as well.
