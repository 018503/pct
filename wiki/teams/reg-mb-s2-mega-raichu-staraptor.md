## Strategy

One of the most popular teams in Champions recently, surging with multiple top players adopting it. Two distinct MB-exclusive mega evolutions — Raichu-Y (No Guard) and Staraptor (Contrary) — with incredible speed control: Tailwind from Staraptor, Trick Room from Farigiraf, and Zap Cannon paralysis from Raichu.

Hisuian Arcanine with Rock Head threatens massive damage with Flare Blitz and Head Smash (no recoil). Head Smash is one of the best answers to Mega Charizard. Life Orb Swords Dance Kingambit can sweep if given one wrong turn — +2 Life Orb Sucker Punch with Helping Hand is devastating.

Sylveon is the flex pick — works under both Tailwind and Trick Room. This version invests more in SpA (168) for harder hits, trading some physical bulk. Iron Head from max Atk Kingambit has 70%+ chance to OHKO this spread, so bulkier variants exist.
