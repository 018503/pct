## Strategy

Rain arguably got the biggest buff of any archetype in Regulation MB thanks to strong new megas like Swampert and Metagross. This build gives Mega Swampert three different lead options depending on matchup: pair it with Sableye for an immediate Rain Dance into Wave Crash, lead with Pelipper if the opponent lacks their own weather control, or lead with Archaludon — mega evolving Swampert for the Speed boost, Flip Turning out to trigger Archaludon's Stamina, then bringing in Pelipper to unload with Electro Shot. High Horsepower gives Swampert a guaranteed one-hit knockout on Electric-types like Mega Raichu-Y regardless of which form it's in.

It's a dual-mega team with Mega Venusaur, whose typing complements Swampert's weaknesses closely. Unusually, both Venusaur and Archaludon run Bold natures with heavy Defense investment rather than the more typical Calm Mind or Modest attacking spreads — trading some damage output for surviving physical hits.

Basculegion carries Defense investment specifically to survive Sucker Punch from Kingambit, and Swampert's own spread mixes HP with enough Speed to outrun max-Speed Mega Aerodactyl once rain is active.
