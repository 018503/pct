## Strategy

A new team featuring Mega Raichu Y and Mega Staraptor has been dominating tournament play in Pokemon Champions recently. The team utilizes tons of offense from other picks like Hisuian Arcanine, Sylveon, and Life Orb Swords Dance Kingambit, and has tons of speed control to pick from - Tailwind from Staraptor, Trick Room from Farigiraf, and paralysis from Raichu. It's one of the strongest teams in the format right now, so we take it for a spin on the last day of the ranked season!
