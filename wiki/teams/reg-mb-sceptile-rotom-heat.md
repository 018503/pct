## Strategy

Mega Sceptile was introduced to Pokemon over a decade ago, but it has had very limited tournament results compared to its counterparts in Mega Swampert and Blaziken. However, it's finally the format for it to shine thanks to its incredibly high base speed stat, a new move in Earth Power, and the ability to counter common picks like Mega Raichu, Garchomp, and Basculegion!
