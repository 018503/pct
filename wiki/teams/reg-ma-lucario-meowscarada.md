Mega Lucario is a really slept on Mega Evolution at the moment. It has the ability to counter several of the most common meta threats, such as Mega Charizard Y, Mega Floette, Kingambit, and Glimmora. This unique hyper offense Mega Lucario team features other Pokemon like Meowscarada, Perish Song Politoed, and Mega Drampa for opposing Trick Room teams.
