## Strategy

North American top player Justin Tang has piloted this to strong results all regulation. The concept dates back to earlier regs: **three setup threats** in **Calm Mind Mega Floette**, **Nasty Plot Mega Delphox** and **Swords Dance Life Orb Kingambit**, plus **two Fake Out users** (Incineroar, Sneasler). You have to respect every Fake Out + setup combination. If you mislead and give one of them a free boost, it often sweeps.

**Sinistcha** comes in mid-game to heal with Hospitality and redirect with Rage Powder. It can also lead next to a setup mon for Rage Powder + boost on turn 1. It carries Trick Room instead of Life Dew. That is useful once Kingambit is boosted, or to reverse the opponent's Trick Room. Sneasler's **Quick Guard** blocks opposing priority. Delphox could run Psyshock, but Psychic is more consistent.
