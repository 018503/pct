## Strategy

Mega Falinks is one of the least used new Mega Evolutions in Regulation MB, but it can still work when built around properly! After featuring all other 15 Megas that were introduced in Regulation MB, it's finally time to wrap things up with Mega Falinks! This team pairs it with a unique Grimmsnarl with Fake Out and Sucker Punch, which can help Falinks set up No Retreat more easily & deal the chip damange necessary to help secure KOes!
