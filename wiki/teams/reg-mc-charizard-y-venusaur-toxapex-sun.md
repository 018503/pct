## Strategy

This was one of the most popular Charizard Y teams at the end of Reg MB, with a top 8 at Worlds. Juan Salerno won a Reg MC regional with the archetype. This is all-time great Paul Chua's version. It is less common now that Charizard has come back down, but it is still worth knowing. **Life Orb Venusaur** (over the Sash variants) brings burst damage next to Charizard. **Toxapex** is the glue. It traps with Infestation, poisons with Toxic, and can Wide Guard Rock Slide / Earthquake for Charizard. The hard part is reading when to click Wide Guard, because good opponents go single-target.

**Sylveon** adds Yawn. Combined with Infestation, it traps a target and then puts it to sleep. Quick Attack finishes things off. Charizard again runs **Ancient Power** and near-max speed for the mirror. Choice Scarf Garchomp and Incineroar are standard.
