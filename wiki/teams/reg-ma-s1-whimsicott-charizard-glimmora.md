## Strategy

Piloted by several top Japanese players in Season 1 — this exact build finished 46th, and the same six were used by Sweeka to take first place, who credited the original builder for the idea. Glimmora slots in well against much of the current metagame: Power Gem punishes Aerodactyl and Charizard, while Sludge Bomb hits Fairy types like Floette and Sylveon, so Glimmora has been climbing in popularity relative to Mega Aerodactyl. Whimsicott partners it as a bulky Tailwind setter that can also sustain through the mid-game, giving the team an explosive lead into most common compositions.

The rest of the core is built from format staples — Charizard, Kingambit, and Garchomp — each with small tweaks. Garchomp runs a Sitrus Berry set for extra survivability. Kingambit is the classic Chople Berry build without Iron Head, carrying just enough Speed investment to take advantage of Tailwind. Basculegion swaps its usual Focus Sash for Mystic Water, since Glimmora already wants the Sash — Mystic Water instead boosts Wave Crash damage, often converting two-hit knockouts into ohkos.

Charizard here is built quite physically defensive, tanky enough to survive hits like Sneasler's Rock Tomb, while still threatening with Heat Wave, Weather Ball, and Solar Beam. Overall this is one of the most common teams in the format right now, and with several Japanese players finding success with their own variants, it's expected to remain prevalent.
