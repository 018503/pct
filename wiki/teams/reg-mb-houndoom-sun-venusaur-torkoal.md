## Strategy

Houndoom has Flash Fire pre-mega (wall for Charizard and Delphox fire attacks) and becomes Solar Power Mega Houndoom, which takes advantage of the Charizard Y sun that is common in the format. It loses Flash Fire on evolving, and Solar Power damage plus frailty mean this team invests purely in speed and power.

Torkoal (Drought, Eruption, Helping Hand) and a backup Sunny Day on Focus Sash Aerodactyl (Rock Slide, Tailwind) set the sun, making the team less reliant on Torkoal. Life Orb Chlorophyll Venusaur with Helping Hand is the second sun sweeper, Choice Scarf Adamant Annihilape (Defiant, Ice Punch for Garchomp, U-turn to bring in Torkoal) scares off Incineroar, and a bulky Sitrus Berry Garchomp provides Ground/Rock coverage and a better Garchomp mirror.

Leads James favours: Aerodactyl + Venusaur (Rock Slide threatens Charizard so Venusaur can attack), Annihilape U-turn into Torkoal, or Torkoal + Venusaur/Houndoom with Helping Hand. It beat the Minimize/Stockpile-style test opponent with Helping Hand Heat Wave.
