## Strategy

A dual-mega hybrid team that can pivot between two very different game plans depending on matchup. In Rain mode, Swampert pairs with Sableye (Rain Dance into Wave Crash) rather than relying on a dedicated weather team to enable it, and Coaching Sneasler can boost either Swampert or Mawile before the team commits to a plan. In Trick Room mode, the team instead leads Incineroar or Farigiraf alongside Mawile, with Swampert or Sneasler held in the back — Swampert even carries Wide Guard, so it can stay in its regular (non-mega) form purely as support while Mawile takes the mega slot instead.

Mega Mawile is one of the best Trick Room sweepers in the format: it's naturally slow, and Play Rough plus Iron Head give it excellent super-effective coverage, backed by one of the strongest Sucker Punches available to any Pokemon this generation. Because the team wants Trick Room up for Mawile to threaten sweeps, min-Speed natures are used across the board on supporting Pokemon like Incineroar and Mawile itself, while Swampert and Sneasler stay Adamant/Jolly with max Attack and Speed for the Rain plan instead.

Farigiraf (heard in places as "Feraligatr" in commentary, but the actual roster member per the paste) runs Colbur Berry with heavy bulk and its own Trick Room, giving the team a second setter. With two Intimidate users on the team — Incineroar and Mega Mawile — care is needed not to hand a free Attack boost to an opposing Kingambit via Defiant.
