## Strategy

Where Mega Staraptor leans on Contrary self-boosts, Mega Malamar (also Contrary) gets there through Superpower, which raises its own Attack and Defense every time it's used, on top of a strong neutral coverage pair in Knock Off and Psycho Cut. Grimmsnarl supports it with Parting Shot, Scary Face, and Fake Tears, making Malamar difficult to pin down and pressure out before it can start stacking boosts.

The core of the team is Grimmsnarl, Malamar, and Sylveon, rounded out with Sneasler or Basculegion. Sylveon specifically runs Yawn to force out whatever the opponent is using to wall Malamar — once that Pokemon is forced to switch, Malamar gets a free turn (or several) of Superpower to snowball out of control.

Spreads are mostly straightforward, but Sneasler and Sylveon are both built extremely defensively, matching the standard Reg MA spreads for each: Sneasler tanks physical hits like Garchomp's Earthquake, and Sylveon survives even strong physical attacks that would otherwise be super effective, like Dire Claw or Iron Head. That bulk buys time for Malamar, which is meant to be the team's primary win condition.
