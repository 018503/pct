## Strategy

2022 World Champion Eduardo Cunha built an incredibly strong team, combining Perish Song Mega Gengar with Rain. The team has a ton of flexibility due to its ability to either KO opponents through Perish Song OR through strong damage from Pokemon like Mega Swampert and Archaludon. It also utilizes Sash Vivillion, which is very powerful into a significant portion of the metagame right now!
