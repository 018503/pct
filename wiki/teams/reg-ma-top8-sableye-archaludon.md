## Strategy

This is another take on the disruptive Sableye / Archaludon duo, finishing top 8. Instead of the more common Light Screen and Reflect, this version runs Encore plus Disable on Sableye, with Will-O-Wisp for extra disruption on top. Sableye is one of the most flexible supportive options in the format with a huge movepool, and Encore plus Disable can completely render an opposing Pokemon useless. The plan is still to set up Rain Dance for Archaludon, which heals back with Leftovers, backed by a rain core of Pelipper and Basculegion.

Mega Venusaur is the team's mega and can be a serious problem for opponents — Floette-based teams normally want to bring Floette in to pressure Archaludon quickly, but Floette actually has a poor matchup into Mega Venusaur, which can't be hurt much and retaliates with super-effective Sludge Bomb. Kingambit is also unusual here, running Focus Sash instead of the classic Chople Berry — Focus Sash still survives super-effective Fighting-type attacks while adding coverage against Fire-type attacks like an opposing Charizard's Heat Wave, and it frees Kingambit up to invest in Speed instead. Combined with Tailwind from Pelipper, that lets Kingambit catch opponents off guard and outpace Pokemon it normally couldn't.

With this team you can lead Sableye plus Archaludon, or Basculegion plus Archaludon, and Mega Venusaur is a genuinely difficult Pokemon to deal with alongside Archaludon — at almost any point you have two bulky Pokemon that can also deal serious damage. Mega Venusaur in particular matches up well into Floette, Garchomp, Basculegion, Incineroar, and Sneasler, and becomes even harder to handle when Sableye is burning things and disabling/encoring the opponent at the same time.
