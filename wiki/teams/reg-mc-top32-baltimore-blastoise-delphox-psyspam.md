## Strategy

Mega Blastoise and Mega Delphox were a well-known balance pairing in Reg MA and MB. Blastoise usage fell in Reg MC because Rillaboom is back. Tanner Mask's update keeps the Incineroar + Sinistcha support core. Fake Out, Intimidate, Parting Shot and Rage Powder buy turns for Blastoise to Shell Smash or Delphox to Nasty Plot, and Sinistcha's Trick Room can reverse opposing Trick Room. Two MC additions make the team more aggressive. Psychic Surge Indeedee-F brings Follow Me, Helping Hand and Psychic Terrain. Pawmot's Revival Blessing revives a fallen Mega, so you can play the set-up mode more aggressively and use your Mega more than once.

The key techs work off the terrain. Unmega'd Delphox is grounded, so in Psychic Terrain it fires a spread, boosted Expanding Force. That is base 114 SpA and base 104 Spe before mega evolving. Mega Delphox's Levitate turns off the terrain boost, so you often hold the mega until you need the SpA/SpD jump. Terrain Pulse on Blastoise takes the terrain's type and doubles in power, and Mega Launcher boosts it again. At +2 in Psychic Terrain it OHKOs a lot of things, including SpD Rillaboom, Sinistcha and Mega Salamence. In Grassy Terrain it becomes a Grass move, which James used to hit Primarina. Water Spout at +2 in rain one-shots Raichu and nearly KOs H-Arcanine.

Spreads are simple. Both Megas run max SpA / max Spe so that at +2 they outrun Choice Scarf users and the new Z megas. Incineroar's speed is set to outrun Focus Sash Kingambit, and Pawmot's Close Combat and Double Shock also cover Kingambit and Basculegion. The berries split the team's weaknesses: Colbur on Indeedee for Kingambit's dark moves, Kasib on Sinistcha for ghost moves like Gholdengo Shadow Ball. James went 4-1 in the video. Leading with Sinistcha lets you switch out of a bad matchup before Blastoise commits. His takeaways: Expanding Force can miss KOs at +2, he wanted a bit more bulk on Delphox, and "it sold me on Terrain Pulse".

| SpD | 125 | 0 | 145 |
| Spe | 134 | 32 | 204 |

**Moves:** Heat Wave · Expanding Force · Nasty Plot · Protect

**Role:** Nasty Plot spread sweeper (Expanding Force pre-mega)

Before mega evolving, Delphox is grounded, so in Indeedee's Psychic Terrain it gets the boosted spread Expanding Force. +2 Nasty Plot plus Helping Hand OHKOed both Rillaboom and Sylveon in the first game. Being grounded also makes it immune to H-Arcanine's Extreme Speed in Psychic Terrain, and it still outspeeds H-Arcanine without the mega. Mega evolve when you need the SpD for bulky special attackers (e.g. Calm Mind Mega Floatzel) or the extra Speed, since Levitate turns off the terrain boost. It is the safer pick into Gengar and Perish teams and into most balance teams.

| SpD | 115 | 0 | 135 |
| Spe | 78 | 32 | 130 |

**Moves:** Protect · Shell Smash · Water Spout · Terrain Pulse

**Role:** Shell Smash win-con

After one Shell Smash behind Follow Me or Rage Powder, Mega Blastoise outspeeds every Garchomp form and the Choice Scarf users, then cleans up with Water Spout and Mega Launcher Terrain Pulse. Terrain Pulse in Psychic Terrain OHKOs Rillaboom, Pelipper and Mega Salamence at +2. In the opponent's Grassy Terrain it becomes a Grass move that hits waters like Primarina. Water Spout does big damage to both foes, even turn one before setting up (it chunked Mega Gengar and Mamoswine). It lives Hurricane at -1 SpD and Extreme Speed at -1 Def. Bring it into rain, Perish/Gengar and Rillaboom-heavy teams, with Indeedee to keep the terrain up.

**Moves:** Throat Chop · Flare Blitz · Parting Shot · Fake Out

**Role:** Fake Out / Parting Shot pivot

A bulky Incineroar with enough Speed to outrun Focus Sash Kingambit, so it can double into Kingambit with Flare Blitz before it moves (unless the opponent has Tailwind). Fake Out plus Parting Shot cuts the opponent's damage and gets Delphox's Nasty Plot or Blastoise's Shell Smash up safely. James often kept it in the back and switched it in turn one to keep the Fake Out pressure. It was left home into rain, where only Intimidate on Basculegion mattered.

**Moves:** Matcha Gotcha · Rage Powder · Trick Room · Protect

**Role:** Rage Powder redirector + Trick Room backup

Specially defensive for Eternatus, psychic spam and Gholdengo. With Kasib Berry it survived Helping Hand Shadow Ball and Sludge Bomb from Mega Gengar. Hospitality heals a set-up Blastoise when it switches in, and Rage Powder protects the Shell Smash turn. Trick Room reverses the opponent's room, or sets your own so Water Spout can sweep. It is a strong lead because it can switch out early. Pelipper's Hurricane is its biggest threat.

In the rain game, Water Spout plus Close Combat removed H-Arcanine. Focus Sash is standard because Pawmot is frail. It usually comes from the back to close games.

**Moves:** Terrain Pulse · Follow Me · Helping Hand · Protect

**Role:** Psychic Terrain + Follow Me enabler

Indeedee is the engine for both Megas. Psychic Terrain powers Expanding Force and Terrain Pulse and blocks priority such as Fake Out, Grassy Glide and Extreme Speed. Follow Me covers the set-up turn, and Helping Hand Expanding Force gets KOs even without Nasty Plot. Its defensive investment and Colbur Berry let it survive Kingambit's dark moves and Wood Hammer. A free switch into Indeedee is often the key turn, since a Rillaboom switch-in overrides the terrain. Life Orb Basculegion's Wave Crash still OHKOs it.
