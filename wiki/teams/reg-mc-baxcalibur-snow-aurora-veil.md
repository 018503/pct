## Strategy

James Baek's updated version of a Regulation H Baxcalibur team. Mega Baxcalibur's huge stats, Thermal Exchange (burn immunity and +1 Attack from fire hits) and Glaive Rush/Ice Shard coverage become far harder to stop once Alolan Ninetales (Light Clay Aurora Veil lasts 8 turns, Snow also gives Ice types +50% Defence) is on the field. The team's goal is to set up either Baxcalibur (Swords Dance) or Grassy Seed Ceruledge (Bulk Up) and sweep.

Rillaboom provides Grassy Terrain (recovery, priority Grassy Glide, Ceruledge's Grassy Seed boost) and Fake Out, which is necessary because Indeedee's Psychic Terrain otherwise blocks Ceruledge's Shadow Sneak. Empoleon (Yawn, Competitive) scares off Intimidate users and slows opposing setup mons, Ninetales' Howl boosts the ally and offsets Intimidate, and Pawmot's Revival Blessing returns a fainted setup mon with Aurora Veil and Snow still active.

In the games the Veil + Snow Baxcalibur survived repeated Close Combat/Wave Crash and won Rain, Charizard/Garchomp, and Sneasler matchups. The hard matchup is Charizard Y + Gholdengo: James lost one game to Charizard/Gholdengo with a surprise Mega Garchomp Z, and a later game against Gholdengo/Frosslass/Kingambit was close.
