## Strategy

Finished 2nd in the ranked season and built around Hisuian Typhlosion, an uncommon pick in the current format. Combining Typhlosion with Sunny Day from Whimsicott and Helping Hand from Farigiraf lets the team blow up opposing squads quickly — Charcoal boosts damage output further, and Burn Up gives Typhlosion a strong secondary attack outside of Eruption. Whimsicott / Typhlosion / Farigiraf forms the core combo, and leading Farigiraf makes the team immune to Fake Out; Whimsicott can also lead alongside Farigiraf to set Tailwind safely without fear of Fake Out disruption.

Floette runs a maximum offense spread — max Special Attack, max Speed — built purely to hit as hard as possible, with Light of Ruin capable of scoring explosive knockouts. Kingambit trades the usual Chople Berry for Focus Sash and runs max Attack / max Speed instead of the slow variant, since this team clicks Tailwind almost every turn and Kingambit can capitalize on the extra Speed. Aerodactyl mega evolves but skips Tailwind in favor of Ice Fang, giving it coverage into Dragon types like Dragonite and Kommo-o that would otherwise wall it, and into Garchomp, which normally checks Aerodactyl outright.

Farigiraf rounds out the team without Trick Room, instead leaning on Armor Tail for anti-priority utility and Helping Hand to turn two-hit knockouts into ohkos. Overall this is a fast, explosive, offense-heavy team that showcases an unusual Hisuian Typhlosion set.
