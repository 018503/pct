## Strategy

Mega Garchomp is not a very common Mega Evolution in Pokemon Champions, but it recently reached Rank #1 in the world on ladder. Yanagi, a top player from Japan, built a unique team centered around SPECIAL Mega Garchomp. In a format filled with Intimidate and physical bulk, special Garchomp threatens huge one-hit KOes onto common meta threats like Mega Charizard, Archaludon, and opposing Garchomp. The team also has some unique picks like Swords Dance Scizor, which covers for Garchomp's Fairy weakness, and Corviknight, while is great into opposing Mega Staraptor!
