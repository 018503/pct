## Strategy

This team revolves around the Beat Up Maushold + Justified Gallade combo. Maushold goes for Beat Up into Gallade before it mega evolves, stacking Gallade to +4 Attack via Justified. Gallade then mega evolves, gaining boosted stats (notably 110 base Speed) and sweeps with Drain Punch, Rock Slide, and Shadow Sneak. At +4, Mega Gallade OHKOs most common threats: Shadow Sneak kills Basculegion, Rock Slide kills Charizard, and Drain Punch handles Kingambit. The key question every game is whether you can find one safe turn to land the Beat Up — if you can, Gallade dominates.

The team has multiple game plans beyond the Gallade sweep. Mega Raichu-Y provides special offense and Lightning Rod support for Milotic, and can be kept in base form to absorb Electric attacks. Milotic runs Coil + Hypnosis + Recover with Leftovers — a defensive wall that takes time to set up but becomes nearly unkillable. The Maushold + Milotic lead is surprisingly effective: Friend Guard + Protect Maushold, free Coil on Milotic, then Super Fang + Hypnosis to neutralize Gallade's biggest threat before switching Gallade in for a safe Beat Up later. Gholdengo with Nasty Plot + Life Orb provides coverage against fairy types that wall Gallade, and Good as Gold blocks status.

Speed benchmarks matter: Mega Gallade at 178 speed ties max speed Mega Staraptor. Maushold at 179 is faster than almost everything to ensure Beat Up goes off. Gholdengo and Milotic both exceed the 112 speed benchmark (outspeeding Mega Aerodactyl under Tailwind). Weavile runs Wide Lens for reliable Triple Axel and has Low Kick for surprise coverage into Kingambit and Incineroar. Raichu has enough HP/Def to survive Jolly Close Combat from Sneasler in 15/16 rolls, and Gholdengo survives Black Glasses Sucker Punch from Kingambit in 15/16 rolls.
