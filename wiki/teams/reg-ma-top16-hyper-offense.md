## Strategy

If hyper offensive teams appeal to you, this top 16 team fits the bill. It was initially built by Figgy Monster and recreated for this video by Justin Tang, so the spreads may not be exact but should be close. The idea is simply overwhelming offense and type coverage across the board, with some strong combinations layered in. Whimsicott can set up Sunny Day immediately, boosting Typhlosion-Hisui's Eruption and Overheat/Heat Wave damage. Tsareena is excellent here thanks to Queenly Majesty blocking opposing priority — it protects against turn-one Fake Out and things like Sucker Punch or Aqua Jet aimed at Typhlosion. Tsareena also carries Helping Hand, so the combination of priority-blocking plus Helping Hand lets you lead it with Typhlosion or Floette and go for Helping Hand into Light of Ruin for a one-hit knockout on anything that doesn't resist it.

Whimsicott is disruptive in its own right, using Encore to render a slot useless, and the team has some fun pieces in Dragapult and Tauros. This Colbur Berry Dragapult survives Dark-type attacks like Kingambit's, and runs Psychic Fangs for incredible coverage against opposing Sneasler, plus Dragon Darts for super-effective damage into common Dragon-types like Dragonite, Garchomp, and Kommo-o. Sucker Punch adds priority and super-effective damage into common attackers. Finally, Tauros-Paldea-Aqua with Mystic Water makes Wave Crash into Aqua Jet extremely powerful, and Intimidate is still one of the best abilities in the game — this Tauros is built to threaten damage rather than run Incineroar's more common support role.

Teams like this aim to win as fast as possible rather than grind out a long game. There isn't much bulk to work with — mainly a little on Tauros and Tsareena — so the whole plan is to hit as hard and as quickly as possible.
