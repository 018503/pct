## Strategy

Two-time Japan National champion Yuma Kinugawa built this snow team on stream and took it into the top 20 of the ladder. The video title says Top 30. Mega Abomasnow has Snow Warning both before and after it mega evolves, which helps it win weather wars. Its coverage fits the MC meta: Blizzard for Rillaboom and the dragons, Leaf Storm for Basculegion and Milotic, Earth Power for Archaludon, Sneasler and Incineroar. Armarouge and Abomasnow have strong type synergy. Flash Fire covers Abomasnow's Fire weakness, and Abomasnow handles the Water/Ground hits Armarouge fears.

Modes: the **Trick Room mode** is Armarouge, Abomasnow, Indeedee and Sirfetch'd. Use Indeedee Follow Me + Armarouge Trick Room, or attack with Expanding Force first and set Trick Room later. You can also lead Armarouge + Indeedee with the **snow mode** (Abomasnow + Beartic) and Garchomp behind. **Abomasnow lead** into rain, because keeping the snow up means Golisopod loses rain's protection from Armarouge's Fire attacks. **Abomasnow + Beartic** puts on immediate Encore / Rock Slide / Icicle Crash flinch pressure. Garchomp fits most leads for fast damage. It replaced the original Choice Scarf Basculegion, and the team started winning more after the swap.

James's verdict: the team was "really fun". Beartic did more work than he expected, and Sirfetch'd "was able to do things". He admitted one game misplay: Feint does work into Froslass. The paste lists Heat Wave on Armarouge, while James described Armor Cannon.
