## Strategy

The 2nd ranked season of Pokemon Champions is here! I wanted to make an early climb to Champion tier with a new Mega Evolution, Lopunny! Mega Lopunny is one of the fastest Pokemon in the format and can be incredibly disruptive, thanks to the combination of a powerful Fake Out and strong attacks like Giga Impact and Close Combat. It's a perfect fit on a hyper-offense team!
