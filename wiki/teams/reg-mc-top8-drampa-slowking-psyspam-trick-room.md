## Strategy

This team made top 8 at a grassroots tournament in Japan and also reached Champion tier on the ladder. It is psyspam Trick Room built for a weather-heavy format. Drampa's pre-mega ability, Cloud Nine, removes the effects of weather. Sun Fire moves, rain Water moves, Electro Shot, Solar Beam and Weather Ball all lose their boost while it is on the field. That makes Trick Room much easier to set against Charizard and Pelipper teams. Once it mega evolves, Berserk gives +1 SpA below half HP on top of base 165 SpA.

The main lead is **Slowking + Indeedee**: Follow Me + Trick Room, or attack straight away with Expanding Force if the opponent doesn't threaten Indeedee. Drampa can lead next to either setter when the opponent's weather would otherwise stop Trick Room. **Kingambit + Metagross** is the fast double-Steel mode, with Sucker Punch and Bullet Punch priority. Metagross + Indeedee is another psyspam pairing. Metagross's Steel Roller removes opposing terrain (Rillaboom's Grassy Terrain), or Indeedee's own if you want to reset it. Grapploct leads are niche but can surprise with Mach Punch / Sucker Punch priority.

James's verdict: "I really like this team". Drampa did "a ton of work". Cloud Nine was so valuable that he sometimes chose not to mega evolve. Slowking's Regenerator and Expanding Force were impressive, and it won the final game as the last Pokemon.
