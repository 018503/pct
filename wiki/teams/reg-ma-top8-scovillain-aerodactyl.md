## Strategy

This dual mega team pairing Aerodactyl and Scovillain was the only team that beat the tournament winner throughout the whole event — the two played 20 sets and this was the only loss the champion took. Mega Aerodactyl and Mega Scovillain is a genuinely unique pairing: Scovillain matches up well into the majority of the format but isn't amazing into Mega Charizard, so Aerodactyl comes in to threaten Charizard (and doesn't even need to mega evolve to get value there). Mega Scovillain otherwise burns opponents, which is huge impact in a format so full of physical attackers like Sneasler, Kingambit, and Basculegion.

The bulkier sets stand out here too. This Basculegion carries Sitrus Berry with heavy HP and Defense investment — normally opponents expect to just Sucker Punch and KO Basculegion with an opposing Kingambit, but combined with burn from Scovillain and this Defense investment, it can hang on for far longer than expected. It won't outpace and secure an immediate Last Respects knockout, but it survives longer and can clean up games later. The team also runs Clanging Scales / Clangorous Soul Kommo-o — lead Sneasler plus Kommo-o and go for Fake Out into Clangorous Soul to set up immediately, or lead Mega Scovillain plus Kommo-o and go for Rage Powder while boosting Kommo-o up.

Aerodactyl and Kommo-o together also make life hard for Charizard, since both resist its main Fire-type attacks. Elsewhere there's a ton of defensive bulk on Sneasler — the same super-defensive Sneasler trend seen across many top teams from this tournament, built to survive Dual Wingbeat from Aerodactyl and Earthquake from Garchomp. If a more supportive mega in Scovillain (rather than a pure attacker) appeals to you, this is worth trying.
