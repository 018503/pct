## Strategy

Mega Absol Z has huge Speed (151) and Attack (154) but poor bulk, so James tests a support set: Jolly Will-O-Wisp / Shadow Claw / Close Combat / Detect. It outspeeds Scarf Basculegion and Scarf-range threats, burns the major physical attackers (Kingambit, Sneasler, Basculegion in Swift Swim) and, being Dark/Ghost, is immune to Close Combat and Expanding Force, resists Dire Claw and ignores Normal moves.

The rest of the team piggybacks on burns: bulky Milotic (Competitive scares off Intimidate, Life Dew recovery) and Sylveon (Calm Mind, Hyper Beam) take physical hits easily when the opponent is burned, Grassy Seed Sneasler sets up Swords Dance with Unburden speed, Rillaboom provides Fake Out and Grassy Terrain recovery and Focus Sash Arcanine-Hisui gives Extreme Speed and answers Golisopod and Rillaboom.

James does not think Absol Z is a real sweeper, but the speed and burns give it an actual niche into Intimidate and Gholdengo-like, Sneasler, and Incineroar teams while the terrain-recovery bulk lets setup mons (Sylveon, Sneasler) get going.
