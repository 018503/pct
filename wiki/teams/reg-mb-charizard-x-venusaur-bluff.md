## Strategy

This team exploits one of the biggest team preview bluffs in the format: opponents see Charizard + Venusaur and almost always expect Mega Charizard Y with Focus Sash Venusaur — the exact six that won a major tournament earlier in the regulation. Instead, they face Mega Charizard X with Belly Drum and Mega Venusaur, catching opponents completely off guard in their Pokemon selection and counterplay. The surprise factor alone generates massive advantages before the first move is even clicked.

Grimmsnarl + Charizard X is the primary setup lead. Grimmsnarl provides dual screens (Reflect + Light Screen) to make the bulky Charizard X even harder to KO, creating windows for Belly Drum. Critically, Belly Drum is not always the play — Charizard X can operate effectively without it by using Breaking Swipe to reduce opposing attack stats and Flame Charge to boost its speed. At +1 speed (149), Charizard X outspeeds max-speed Mega Aerodactyl and everything slower. The team can also pivot to Grimmsnarl + Venusaur as a lead against matchups like rain, where Mega Venusaur's Thick Fat + Giga Drain healing creates a near-unkillable wall under screens. Pelipper + Archaludon or Basculegion serves as an entirely non-mega lead mode against Mega Staraptor teams where neither mega matches up well.

Key weaknesses: Charizard X has low base Attack investment (only 2 points) so even at +6 it can fail to OHKO very bulky targets with Breaking Swipe or Flame Charge. No healing means Belly Drum's HP cost is permanent. Pelipper's own rain reduces Charizard X's fire damage. Mega Staraptor is particularly problematic since neither mega matches up well against it — the team relies on Pelipper/Archaludon/Basculegion to handle it.

**Moves:** Breaking Swipe · Belly Drum · Flame Charge · Protect

**Role:** Setup sweeper with team preview bluff

Extremely bulky for a Charizard at 185 HP / 139 Def / 113 SpD — designed to survive hits while setting up behind screens rather than to OHKO targets. 149 speed means +1 Flame Charge outspeeds the entire format including max-speed Mega Aerodactyl. Breaking Swipe hits both opponents and lowers their Attack, functioning as both offense and a pseudo-Intimidate. Only 2 Atk points means even at +6 you won't always OHKO bulky targets — the trade-off for maximum survivability. Belly Drum should be saved for mid-to-late game when threats that outspeed or tank it have been removed; don't force it early. The team preview bluff is the biggest advantage — opponents prepare for Charizard Y's special attacks and are blindsided by a physical Dragon-type.
