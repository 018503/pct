## Strategy

Regional champion Drew Bliss went 8-3 at Brisbane Regionals with this team and finished just outside top cut (Top 32). Mega Starmie has base 60 HP and barely makes the top 100 in usage, but in MC it works as a mixed attacker. Huge Power Liquidation is boosted by rain and Helping Hand. Base 130 SpA Expanding Force gets the Psychic Terrain boost and becomes a spread move. Ice Beam covers Garchomp and Mega Salamence. Few players expect Expanding Force from Starmie. Cybertron brought Starmie in 70%+ of games. Gengar is the more matchup-specific mega.

Modes: **Indeedee + Starmie** into teams without terrain control. Avoid it if you see Rillaboom or Raichu X. Use Helping Hand Expanding Force for fast KOs, with any two of Pelipper, Archaludon and Sneasler behind. **Starmie + Sneasler** as a scouting lead (good into Whimsicott). Switching Indeedee in on turn 1 sets terrain, blocks Fake Out and Grassy Glide, and triggers Sneasler's Psychic Seed. **Pelipper + Starmie or Archaludon** for rain pressure into teams without weather. **Gengar + Sneasler** for pure speed. Icy Wind cripples Garchomp and Salamence, and Shadow Tag traps a mega-evolved Charizard before Pelipper takes the weather back. The key choice each game is whether to bring Archaludon, since it is much weaker without Pelipper. When the opponent brings their own Indeedee or rain, you can often leave yours at home.

Weaknesses: Dark types, especially next to redirection (Sinistcha, Rage Powder Volcarona). Both Raichu megas (terrain control, Fake Out, and Y outspeeds Starmie). Mega Swampert Earthquake, since there is no Wide Guard or Helping Hand to stop it. Mega Tyranitar's sand breaking Pelipper's Sash. Choice Scarf users that outspeed Gengar. Starmie and Gengar both want OHKOs, so double-targeting into Protect is costly.

**Moves:** Sludge Bomb · Shadow Ball · Icy Wind · Protect

**Role:** Max-offense Shadow Tag mega

This is not the bulky Perish Gengar. It runs max SpA / max Spe Timid, with Sludge Bomb, Shadow Ball, Icy Wind and Protect. Icy Wind is the tech: it slows Garchomp and Salamence so Archaludon can move right after. After mega evolving, Shadow Tag can trap a mega Charizard before Pelipper reclaims the weather. With no bulk, Choice Scarf Basculegion or Garchomp can OHKO it.

**Moves:** Liquidation · Expanding Force · Ice Beam · Protect

**Role:** Mixed Huge Power mega — team centrepiece

It is Hasty, so it has -Def. The 18 Spe points (173) outpace neutral max base 120s like Sneasler and Mega Froslass, and the rest is split between Atk and SpA. Liquidation is boosted by Huge Power, rain and Helping Hand, and Expanding Force is boosted by Psychic Terrain. In the right position it can win the game in one turn. Be careful around physical attackers and Dark types, and avoid attacks that won't OHKO.
