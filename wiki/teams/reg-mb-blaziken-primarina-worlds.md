## Strategy

This team pairs Mega Blaziken with Trick Room support from Farigiraf and a Life Orb Primarina that functions as a pseudo-second mega evolution. The default game plan is to lead Blaziken and Farigiraf together — Blaziken threatens massive early damage while Farigiraf sets up Trick Room. As Blaziken faints (or is switched out), the slow Trick Room sweepers Primarina, Torkoal, and Kingambit come in to clean up. The team is unusually flexible for a Trick Room composition: Torkoal plus Venusaur is a strong secondary lead that pressures with Sleep Powder and sun-boosted Eruptions, while max-speed Adamant Kingambit can threaten knockouts outside of Trick Room entirely.

Primarina is the hidden star of the team — its Quiet nature preserves its Attack stat for a meaningful Life Orb Aqua Jet that catches Aerodactyl and Excadrill off guard at low HP. With 24 HP / 28 Def investment it tanks physical priority like Sneasler's Dire Claw comfortably while dishing out enormous Hyper Voice and Moonblast damage under Trick Room. The team's defensive backbone comes from matching leads to matchups: Venusaur plus Kingambit into rain (Leaf Storm threatens Swampert while Torkoal can switch in to flip weather), Farigiraf plus Kingambit into setup teams like Blastoise/Delphox (Armor Tail blocks Fake Out while Kingambit pressures immediately).

Key weaknesses include Mega Gengar Perish Rain (no ghost types or pivoting moves to escape Shadow Tag), Mega Dragonite (only Primarina handles it, so losing Primarina early is catastrophic), and Taunt users like Sneasler shutting down Farigiraf's Trick Room. Filip Wozniak finished 15th at the 2026 World Championships with this team, winning $5,000 and giving Mega Blaziken one of its best competitive finishes ever.

**Moves:** Flare Blitz · Close Combat · Rock Slide · Detect

**Role:** Aggressive lead / glass cannon opener

Blaziken's 150 Speed on mega evolution outspeeds max speed Gholdengo before Speed Boost, and after one boost it outspeeds the entire unboosted format including Mega Aerodactyl. The 12 HP / 4 SpD investment lets it survive Dual Wingbeat from regular Aerodactyl and Life Orb Earth Power from Venusaur. The team's primary pattern is to lead Blaziken with Farigiraf, double Protect turn one for a free Speed Boost, then threaten Flare Blitz or Close Combat while Farigiraf sets Trick Room. Blaziken often trades itself for 1-2 KOs to give a free switch into Primarina or Torkoal under Trick Room. Rock Slide breaks Dragonite's Multiscale and Detect over Protect avoids Imprison from opposing Trick Room setters.

**Moves:** Psychic · Thunderbolt · Protect · Trick Room

**Role:** Trick Room setter / priority denial

This is an extremely physically defensive Farigiraf — 219 HP / 133 Def gives favorable rolls against even Black Glasses or Life Orb Kingambit's Kowtow Cleave, which was crucial heading into Worlds. Armor Tail blocks Fake Out, allowing safe Trick Room setup alongside aggressive Blaziken plays. Farigiraf is the default partner for Blaziken leads and also pairs well with Kingambit against setup-oriented teams.

Max HP / max SpA Torkoal with Charcoal for consistent sun-boosted Eruptions and Weather Balls. Under Trick Room, Torkoal's 36 Speed makes it one of the slowest attackers in the format.

Defiant punishes Intimidate switches and Icy Wind debuffs. Kowtow Cleave's perfect accuracy is valuable for a team that wants consistent early damage.

**Moves:** Protect · Hyper Voice · Moonblast · Aqua Jet

**Role:** Trick Room sweeper / pseudo-mega / Dragonite counter

The team's hidden star. With 179 HP / 122 Def and natural 136 SpD, Primarina is deceptively bulky — surviving Sneasler's Dire Claw and tanking physical priority comfortably. Life Orb pushes its 176 SpA to devastating levels: Hyper Voice (Water-type via Liquid Voice) and Moonblast threaten OHKOs on most of the format under Trick Room. The Quiet nature preserves the Attack stat (no -Atk from Modest), giving Aqua Jet meaningful damage — enough to KO damaged Aerodactyl at ~40% HP, which consistently surprises opponents. Primarina completely walls both regular and Mega Dragonite, making it the team's primary answer to the World Championship-winning archetype. Losing Primarina early against Dragonite teams is essentially a loss condition.

Venusaur is heavily invested in HP and SpD rather than pure offense — 156 HP / 139 SpD lets it survive sun-boosted Heat Wave from Mega Charizard AND Weather Ball from Mega Charizard in sun, as well as Moonblast from Whimsicott. Paired with Torkoal, Venusaur gains Chlorophyll speed to outpace the format and put key threats to sleep. Also a critical lead option against rain teams alongside Kingambit, where Leaf Storm threatens Swampert and Basculegion.
