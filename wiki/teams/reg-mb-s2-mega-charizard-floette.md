## Strategy

One of the most popular compositions in both MA and MB — the default team many top Japanese players used. Still exceptionally powerful. This version stands out through its surprise spreads designed to exploit opponents' internal damage calcs.

Kingambit is near max HP / max SpD (137 SpD) rather than max Atk — survives special attacks like Light of Ruin and Hyper Beam that people expect to OHKO. Charizard is Bold with near max Def (141) — survives physical attacks. Floette is Timid max SpA / max Spe rather than Modest for speed advantage in mirror matchups. Garchomp runs Adamant with Sitrus Berry for sustained bulk.

No one right way to train this team — variants exist with Life Orb on Basculegion, Choice Scarf on various members, Modest Floette, faster Charizards. But these surprise spreads catch opponents off-guard and are what elevated this version to one of the highest placing.
