## Strategy

This finished 2nd at Gdansk Regionals. Like the Spy Anya build, it is triple Fake Out (Incineroar, Rillaboom, Sneasler) around **Calm Mind Mega Floette**. Instead of Salamence and Basculegion, though, it runs **regular Mega Garchomp** (not Z) and **Life Orb Kingambit**. Cybertron covered an MB version with Sinistcha in Rillaboom's slot. Garchomp is useful even un-mega'd with **Scale Shot**, Rock Slide and Stomping Tantrum. One Scale Shot speed boost takes Mega Garchomp to exactly 190, which outpaces max-speed Sneasler, so it needs little speed investment.

Modes: **protect the Floette**, with triple Fake Out + Floette. **Kingambit + any Fake Out user** goes for Swords Dance and treats Kingambit as a second mega. **Sneasler + Garchomp** opens with Coaching into Scale Shot. Coaching also gives Floette +1 Def against physical hits. Helping Hand Incineroar adds damage to Floette and Garchomp. This Floette runs Dazzling Gleam, not Moonblast, for spread damage.
