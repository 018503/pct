## Strategy

Built by the 2026 Japanese National Champion, this Mega Raichu-Y team went 9-1 in Master Ball tier by rejecting the usual glass-cannon Raichu-Y build. Instead of dumping everything into Special Attack, this spread is near-max HP and Defense with a Timid nature for a bit of Speed and some Special Defense — trading a guaranteed one-hit knockout for real staying power. Even without Special Attack investment, Zap Cannon and Focus Blast still hit hard enough to two-shot most things (Focus Blast is super effective on Incineroar and Arcanine), and Substitute lets Raichu absorb a turn behind cover while a partner cleans up.

Raichu-Y's clear weakness is Ground-type attackers, especially Garchomp, so the rest of the team stacks answers to it: Choice Scarf Vanilluxe is one of the best answers to Garchomp in the format, Basculegion and Dragonite give extra ways to threaten it, and Incineroar's Intimidate softens physical Ground attackers generally. Poison Touch + Poison Jab Sneasler — a set that started rising at the end of Regulation MA — reliably poisons opponents, which is especially punishing against stat-boosting setup teams that this format is full of.

Incineroar itself is unusually offensive here, running an Adamant nature with 26 Attack points rather than a purely defensive Intimidate spread, giving the team a second real damage threat alongside the bulky Raichu-Y.
