## Strategy

Mega Garchomp Z has base 151 Speed, becomes a pure Dragon type and a Special attacker, and gets Levitate. On this sand team Levitate makes it immune to the Earthquakes from Sand Rush Excadrill, giving easy doubles with Draco Meteor, Power Gem and Flamethrower coverage. It outspeeds Scarf Basculegion and almost every Mega.

The plan is straight-up speed and damage. Tyranitar sets sand (Mega is secondary) for Excadrill's Sand Rush and breaks Sashes; Choice Scarf Indeedee-M sets Psychic Terrain (blocks priority, powers up spread Expanding Force) and provides Trick; Psychic Seed Unburden Sneasler becomes very fast immediately; Tkumi-style Life Orb Basculegion (123 Speed) closes out games with Last Respects.

Matchups are best against weather and terrain teams that rely on slower setup: the team can open with Garchomp + Excadrill or Garchomp + Sneasler for immediate KOs and keep Tyranitar or Indeedee in the back depending on opposing weather/terrain.
