## Strategy

Mega Barbaracle hasn't gotten much attention, but this build reached 1800+ Elo on day one of the format. Tough Claws Barbaracle runs Close Combat, Liquidation, and Rock Slide — a coverage spread that threatens huge portions of the metagame for one-hit knockouts, with Rock Slide in particular devastating Mega Charizard and both Fighting- and Water-type STAB moves boosted by the ability. Tailwind support (from Whimsicott) covers Barbaracle's fairly average base Speed so it can actually get those knockouts off before being hit back.

The team runs a second mega in Froslass, kept very bulky here, and a Life Orb Garchomp — now a legal item combination this regulation, and one of the strongest options available on it. Kingambit and Basculegion round out the back line with fairly standard sets.

Spreads are mostly straightforward: Garchomp carries a touch of HP and Defense to help against opposing Dragon Claw users, and Basculegion is trained to survive Sucker Punch from a max-Attack, non-Black-Glasses Kingambit — a common defensive benchmark this format.
