## Strategy

One of the best current ways to run rain-boosted Archaludon: Calm Mind plus Baton Pass Espathra passes boosts straight into it. It's a dual-mega team — Mega Scovillain mainly plays defense and protects Archaludon, while Mega Dragonite adds the offense the Scovillain/Archaludon core otherwise lacks, since opposing Mega Charizard and Mega Venusaur can both be awkward for that core to deal with on its own.

Politoed here is built far more aggressively than usual — essentially a max-offense special attacker with Mystic Water and Modest nature rather than the standard Sitrus Berry support set. Sneasler adds Fake Out plus real damage output, pairing especially well with Espathra. The main gameplan is to get a Speed Boost (or ideally a Calm Mind or two as well) on Espathra and Baton Pass it into Archaludon — even a single speed boost is enough to let Archaludon outrun Garchomp and opposing Mega Charizard. Espathra can also just Baton Pass into Politoed instead, depending on what needs the boost more.

Espathra isn't purely a passing bot, though — it already carries heavy speed investment on its own, outracing most of the format even before a boost, and Lumina Crash hits hard enough to threaten a surprise plus-one OHKO the turn after dropping a target's Special Defense. Mega Scovillain's HP/SpD spread is built to redirect two or three attacks away from a partner, land burns, and protect — and Archaludon itself carries more speed investment than typical Archaludon sets, both to beat opposing Arcanine and to make full use of that Espathra speed pass.
