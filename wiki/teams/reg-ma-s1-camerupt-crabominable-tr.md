## Strategy

A deliberately non-meta Hard Trick Room build featuring two uncommon mega evolutions — Camerupt and Crabominable — alongside Azumarill and Gallade. Farigiraf pairs with Azumarill, Gallade, and Camerupt as leads, threatening immediate offense while also carrying the option to flip into Trick Room. Once Trick Room is up, Mega Camerupt and Mega Crabominable both have strong matchups into much of the current format and can sweep from there.

Azumarill matches up well into Garchomp and Floette, and its Liquidation + Aqua Jet combination can even KO Charizard under sun. Gallade brings Sharpness-boosted Sacred Sword for strong damage into Archaludon, Leaf Blade for Water types like Basculegion, and Psycho Cut for Poison types like Mega Gengar — giving the team answers to several common threats. This is a distinctly different structure from most top teams in the format, and the pilot climbed all the way to 54th place and Champions tier with it, including a win over the video creator near the end of the ranked season.

As expected from a Trick Room team, investment is maximized on offense and HP across the board. The two standout spread choices are Gallade, which carries Speed investment so it can outpace Pokemon around its own Speed tier outside of Trick Room, and a bulky physical wall built to tank hits like Kowtow Cleave from an opposing Kingambit.
