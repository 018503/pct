## Strategy

Dual weather appeared at the end of Reg MB and won the Seniors World Championship. Australian top player Kieran won a regional with this version, pairing it with **Mega Garchomp Z**. The last slot is the flex spot. It has held Basculegion, Mega Swampert (the Worlds winner) and Choice Scarf Garchomp. **Charizard enables Venusaur, Pelipper enables Archaludon**, and **Light Clay Grimmsnarl** screens make everything hard to KO quickly. Garchomp brings fast offence and still works un-mega'd next to mega Charizard: Modest Draco Meteor, Earth Power, and Power Gem into Charizard.

Charizard runs **Hurricane** to use the rain. It doesn't need Ancient Power, because rain Weather Ball and Garchomp's Power Gem handle Charizard. It doesn't need Solar Beam either, because Venusaur's Leaf Storm covers Water types. Leads can be Grimmsnarl + Charizard / Archaludon / Garchomp for screens, or straight weather (Charizard, Venusaur, Pelipper, Archaludon). Kieran's team report stresses the **Timid speed on Charizard**. It outspeeds Hisuian Arcanine, Choice Band Floette and opposing Charizards, so Pelipper can switch in, set rain and Weather Ball before they act.
