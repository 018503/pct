## Strategy

Inspired by 2024 VGC World Championships player Motoki, who paired Gravity Sableye with Sing Mega Altaria. Gravity raises Sing's accuracy from 55% to 91%, letting Altaria put any Pokemon to sleep reliably. Combined with Pixilate Hyper Voice for consistent spread STAB damage, this gives Altaria both disruption and offense.

Mega Altaria gains Dragon/Fairy typing, making it immune to Dragon moves and losing Altaria's 4x Ice weakness. Pixilate turns Normal moves into Fairy-type, so Hyper Voice becomes a powerful Fairy-type spread move. Sableye provides Prankster support with priority Fake Out, Gravity, and Encore to lock opponents into bad moves.

Volcarona serves as a bulky redirector with Rage Powder, drawing attacks away from Altaria while weakening special attackers with Struggle Bug. Flame Body can punish physical contact moves. Scarf Basculegion with Adaptability provides a powerful late-game cleaner — Last Respects scales with each fainted Pokemon, and Choice Scarf at 130 speed outspeeds most of the format.
