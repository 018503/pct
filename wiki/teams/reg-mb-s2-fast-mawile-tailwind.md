## Strategy

Unlike typical Trick Room Mega Mawile builds, this team runs a fast Mawile with 15 speed points — enough to outspeed max speed Jolly Garchomp and everything slower under Tailwind. The dual-mega structure pairs Mawile with Mega Blastoise, which operates well under both Tailwind and Trick Room thanks to its middling speed tier. Mawile is brought in roughly 70% of games and Blastoise in the remaining 30%, with Blastoise better into matchups featuring Gholdengo or heavy fire coverage.

Offensive Farigiraf with Energy Ball provides surprise coverage against bulky Water types like Mega Swampert, which otherwise wall the entire team. The psychic also deals significant damage since opponents rarely respect Farigiraf's offensive potential. Max-speed Modest Sylveon outspeeds common threats even without speed control active and hits incredibly hard with Pixilate Hyper Beam for guaranteed one-hit knockouts. Choice Scarf Garchomp is the fastest Pokemon in the format and provides immediate offensive presence, while Focus Sash Aerodactyl sets up Tailwind and provides Rock Slide spread damage and flinch pressure.

A key strength of this team is its flexibility — Mawile operates under both Tailwind and Trick Room, so opponents can't simply reverse your speed control. The biggest weaknesses are bulky Water types (especially Coil Milotic, which requires Energy Ball from Farigiraf to handle) and fire types that threaten Mawile. Aerodactyl and Garchomp cover the Charizard matchup, but Mawile itself has no coverage into fire types due to lacking Rock Slide.

The star of the team with a unique fast spread. 15 speed points put Mawile at 85 speed — under Tailwind, this outspeeds max speed Jolly Garchomp and everything slower. Best led with Aerodactyl for immediate Tailwind, or with Garchomp for double offensive pressure with Sucker Punch + Scarf attack double-ups.
