## Strategy

A fresh take on sand that placed 2nd in a Victory Road online tournament. It pairs the familiar **Whimsicott + Mega Staraptor** duo with rare picks: **Hippowdon**, **Glimmora** and **Houndstone**. Lead **Hippowdon + Glimmora** and it is hard to avoid Toxic Debris. One layer of Toxic Spikes plus sand chip takes a big bite out of everything in a couple of turns. Then the attackers pick up KOs. Late game, **Life Orb Last Respects Houndstone** in sand KOs one Pokemon after another, and **Substitute + Ally Switch** make it a nightmare to remove.

Either mega can be brought un-mega'd. Glimmora can spread poison before evolving, or you can mega Glimmora and use Staraptor for Intimidate / one big Brave Bird. This Staraptor has **Roost** and no Tailwind, so Whimsicott (Charm, no Protect) is the Tailwind setter. Other modes include Whimsicott + Staraptor straight away, or Kingambit led with any attacker.
