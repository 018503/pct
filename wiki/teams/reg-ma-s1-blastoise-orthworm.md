## Strategy

One of the most unique teams in the video, built around Shell Smash Mega Blastoise, Meowstic, and Orthworm. The team creator liked using Shell Smash Blastoise but often struggled to actually set it up for free — the solution here is Orthworm's Shed Tail: pair Meowstic + Orthworm or Sneasler + Orthworm with Fake Out, have Orthworm Shed Tail out, bring Mega Blastoise in behind the resulting Substitute, set up Shell Smash safely, then sweep. Meowstic is the same Rain Dance-carrying set seen elsewhere this season, and Rain Dance plus healing from Sinistcha's Life Dew and Hospitality provide additional support for Blastoise.

Scizor rounds out the team with unexpected coverage — Metal Coat-boosted Bullet Punch is strong into Fairy types like Floette and Sylveon that are everywhere in the format, and Close Combat is a surprise burst option into Steel types like Incineroar and Archaludon that opponents typically don't expect from a non-mega, non-Swords Dance Scizor. Sneasler crucially carries Feint, letting it punch through Wide Guard or Protect so Water Spout can be unleashed freely even against Aerodactyl or Pelipper's Wide Guard.

The team indexes heavily on Mega Blastoise as the win condition — Meowstic, Orthworm, and Sinistcha are all purely supportive, and nearly everything is built around enabling Blastoise. Blastoise itself runs max offense, relying on Meowstic's Yawn and Reflect, Sinistcha's redirection, and Orthworm's Shed Tail to protect it long enough to get moving. Sneasler carries meaningful HP and Defense investment while Scizor runs the classic max HP / max Attack spread.
