## Strategy

Mega Raichu X has been overshadowed by Mega Raichu Y in Pokemon Champions so far... so when I saw that a really unique team with Mega Raichu X made it to Champion tier, I knew I had to try it out. This team takes advantage of the Electric Terrain by using Rising Voltage on both Mega Raichu X and a unique partner in Jolteon! It also features fun picks like Palafin and Charcoal Typhlosion.
