## Strategy

This dual mega team pairs Mega Scizor with Mega Tyranitar, backed by supportive options in Incineroar and Sinistcha. Scizor and Tyranitar cover for each other's weaknesses, and even though both are megas, you can bring both into a game and simply choose which one to actually mega evolve. Scizor's main issue is strong Fire-types like Charizard, so the rest of the team is built to cover that: Rotom-Wash hits Fire-types for super-effective damage with Hydro Pump, Incineroar and Kommo-o both resist Fire-type damage, and Tyranitar is one of the most annoying Pokemon in the format for Fire-types to deal with.

Sinistcha heals up teammates like Kommo-o and Scizor with Hospitality and Life Dew, and the Incineroar/Sinistcha/setup combination is strong in the current format — Kommo-o in particular starts to feel like a mega evolution itself once it gets a Clangorous Soul boost, and becomes very difficult to deal with once combined with Sinistcha's healing and Incineroar's disruption.

The Choice Scarf Rotom-Wash also brings speed control via Electroweb — both Kommo-o and Tyranitar carry Speed investment, so you can sequence Electroweb into Clanging Scales, or Electroweb into Rock Slide from Mega Tyranitar, to pick up knockouts that wouldn't otherwise be there. The overall strength of the team is having two mega evolutions that function fine without actually mega evolving, giving a lot of flexibility about which threat to commit to in a given game.
