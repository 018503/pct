## Strategy

Mega Gengar had one of its best performances yet in Pokemon Champions, finishing top 4 with this Perish Song set. Mega Gengar is paired with Alolan Ninetales — one of the top duos to come out of this tournament — with the plan being that Mega Gengar traps a target once it mega evolves (Shadow Tag) while Alolan Ninetales disrupts the rest of the field with Encore and Disable. Misposition against this duo and it can steal games very quickly thanks to Perish Song plus Ninetales rendering a Pokemon useless.

Sneasler, Kingambit, and Incineroar round out the team, and crucially Incineroar runs Protect instead of an offensive Dark-type attack so it can help stall out the Perish Song count. Alolan Ninetales carries Never-Melt Ice rather than Focus Sash, with the Sash instead going on Sneasler — this Adamant, max Attack Focus Sash Sneasler is powerful enough to have roughly a 50% chance to knock out even max HP Chople Berry Kingambit in one hit. Dragonite adds extra offense with a set similar to the 2nd place team's, but running Protect instead of Tailwind since every Pokemon on this team carries Protect to complement the Perish Song game plan.

Gengar itself doesn't need Special Attack investment — its base Special Attack plus a Modest nature is already strong enough for two-hit knockouts on super-effective hits, while still being extremely disruptive. Position Gengar and Ninetales well together and a single opponent misstep can hand the game away.
