## Strategy

One of the most creative teams to come out of the Japanese National Championships, packed with sets that can each single-handedly win the game on their own — Calm Mind Sylveon, Psych Up Politoed, Acid Spray plus Heal Pulse plus Trick Room on Slowking, Ally Switch Farigiraf, and Encore plus Healing Wish on Mega Lopunny. The general idea is to use Slowking to catch opponents off guard while every other Pokemon carries its own surprise.

Heal Pulse is the standout support move — paired with Mega Blastoise, it fully restores HP while simultaneously boosting Water Spout's damage output, since that move scales with the user's current HP. Politoed's Psych Up copies whatever boost Blastoise's Shell Smash or Sylveon's Calm Mind has already banked, instantly making Politoed a serious offensive threat too. Politoed also runs real speed investment specifically to make that Psych Up timing work.

There are so many possible lines with this team that opponents have to respect every single Pokemon — Farigiraf can lead directly into Blastoise, Ally Switch adds variance, and Slowking's Acid Spray dropping Special Defense sets up Blastoise or Sylveon for knockouts even against resistant targets. Blastoise itself works both in and out of Trick Room thanks to Shell Smash, and the Mystic Water / Muddy Water / Rain Dance package lets the team surprise opposing weather setters too. It plays functionally very differently from most other teams in the format — and on the ladder in particular, that element of surprise is a real weapon.
