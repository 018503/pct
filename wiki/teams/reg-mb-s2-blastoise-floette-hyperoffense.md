## Strategy

Hyperoffensive team designed to move first and deal maximum damage. No Shell Smash on Blastoise — just Water Spout, Dark Pulse, Aura Sphere, and Protect. The philosophy is "I don't care about setting up, I'm just going to deal more damage before you can deal damage to me."

Talonflame provides Gale Wings Tailwind. Friend Guard Maushold with Super Fang creates a threatening duo with Blastoise — opponents never know if Maushold will Follow Me (protecting Blastoise for Shell Smash that doesn't exist) or Super Fang to halve HP for Blastoise to finish.

Probably one of the more beginner-friendly teams in the video — lots of damage, Maushold redirection + Friend Guard for breathing room, Tailwind for speed control, and all powerful Pokemon.
