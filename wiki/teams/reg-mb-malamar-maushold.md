## Strategy

I've been on a mission to feature every brand new Mega Evolution in Pokemon Champions Regulation MB and I'm super close to finishing it... when I stumbled upon a team with two of the four Megas I had remaining, I knew I had to try it out. This team features a unique mega duo of Mega Malamar as well as Mega Mawile and instantly became one of my favorite teams to play. Malamar can be used as a Trick Room setter for Mawile or can be used to sweep through opposing teams, especially when supported by Whimsicott. With a Wide Lens Population Bomb Maushold and Life Orb Incineroar, you can shock your opponents with quick KOes!
