## Strategy

Spanish player Javier Varela turned his first ever day 2 into a Gdansk Regionals win. It was also Mega Staraptor's first regional title. The team is the Reg MB sand archetype that three top players took to top 32 at the 2026 Worlds. Players mostly forgot it in early Reg MC, until Simone Sanvito (SanvyVGC) placed 3rd at Nice with it. The six Pokemon are unchanged from MB. The new tricks are **Dragon Dance on Tyranitar** in place of High Horsepower / Low Kick, Ice Beam + Icy Wind + Scald Milotic (Haze is gone), and **Rocky Helmet Strength Sap Sinistcha** in place of Shadow Ball. Strength Sap on your own Staraptor triggers Contrary for +1 Atk and also heals Sinistcha.

Main leads: **Staraptor + Gholdengo** is the most flexible. Staraptor covers the Dark types that wall Gholdengo (Incineroar, Kingambit), and either one can start boosting straight away with Close Combat or Nasty Plot. Staraptor can also stay un-mega'd and just set Tailwind. **Sinistcha + Gholdengo** is Rage Powder into Nasty Plot for passive teams, but avoid it into Heat Wave. **Tyranitar + Excadrill** puts sand up immediately into rain, Charizard Y or Froslass, because the slower mega wins the weather war. **Milotic + Excadrill** punishes Intimidate leads (Incineroar, Salamence) as long as the opponent has no strong Grass damage. You will often bring both Staraptor and Tyranitar and decide which to mega a few turns in.

Weaknesses: a well-played Kingambit is the scariest threat, because it checks Gholdengo and Dragon Dance Tyranitar has no super-effective hit on it. Staraptor can't safely come in on opposing Milotic (Coil / Hypnosis sets are extra annoying). Ghosts can switch in on Close Combat and deny the boosts. Excadrill is weak to priority through its Sash and to opposing weather.

**Moves:** Rock Slide · Knock Off · Dragon Dance · Protect

**Role:** Dragon Dance sand setter / flex mega

Jolly 32 Spe with 18 HP. As a mega it survives Focus Blast in sand from uninvested Mega Raichu Y, and un-mega'd it survives Life Orb Garchomp Earthquake / Stomping Tantrum. Dragon Dance makes it a sweeper, but the coverage is thin: Fighting types, Ground types and Kingambit (especially Low Kick sets) match up well into it. Lead it with Excadrill into weather teams. Its slower mega evolution means sand overrides Charizard / Pelipper / Froslass weather.

**Moves:** Shadow Ball · Make It Rain · Nasty Plot · Protect

**Role:** Bulky Nasty Plot win-con

The best-optimised spread on the team. 27 HP / 12 Def survives Life Orb Earthquake / Stomping Tantrum from 32 Jolly Garchomp in 15/16 rolls. 17 SpD always survives Timid max SpA Mega Gengar Shadow Ball and often survives Modest. 10 Spe (114) speed creeps the common 112–113 Tailwind benchmark. Cybertron calls it the "third hidden mega": it gets Nasty Plot up under Tailwind or Sinistcha heals and then takes over. Its biggest fear is Kingambit.
