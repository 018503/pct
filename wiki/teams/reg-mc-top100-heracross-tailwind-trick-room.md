## Strategy

Mega Heracross is one of the least-used megas, but this team finished top 100 in Champion tier last season. **Skill Link** guarantees five hits from Bullet Seed and Rock Blast. That breaks Focus Sash on Basculegion and Pelipper, Rock Blast handles Hisuian Arcanine, Mega Salamence, Volcarona and Charizard, and Close Combat threatens Incineroar, Kingambit and Archaludon. There is no Bug STAB, so it is dead weight into Gholdengo and other ghosts, and it struggles into fairies and Psychic types. Heracross can also stay un-mega'd to fish for a **Moxie** boost first, then mega evolve with +1 Atk.

The team has two speed modes. **Whimsicott Tailwind** pairs with Glimmora, Incineroar or Heracross itself. Lead Heracross when the opponent has no clean answer to it. Otherwise lead Glimmora / Incineroar to soften targets, and Heracross comes in for free after the first KO. **Farigiraf Trick Room** usually comes from an Incineroar Fake Out lead, with Drampa as the slow Expert Belt sweeper. **Incineroar + Heracross with Drampa and Whimsicott / Farigiraf in back** is the recommended rain mode. Intimidate goes up, Heracross Rock Blasts Pelipper, Bullet Seeds Basculegion or Close Combats Archaludon, and Incineroar switches into Drampa so Cloud Nine turns off the rain. The creator's free team report lists eight combinations.

Weaknesses: the **Mega Staraptor / Mega Tyranitar sand** team (Gdansk winner) is the worst matchup. Staraptor outspeeds Heracross and Brave Birds it even un-mega'd, and Milotic stops you leading Incineroar. Redirection (bulky Indeedee, Sinistcha) also hurts, because Heracross can't OHKO the redirector. Whimsicott has no Sash, so losing it turn 1 lets the opponent stall Tailwind. Farigiraf has no Protect, so Life Orb Kingambit is a problem.
