## Strategy

Mega Alakazam has about 1% usage, partly because of base 55 HP / 65 Def. Cybertron met this team on ladder, and its creator then posted a Reddit report showing it reached Champion tier. Take Alakazam away and it is a hard Trick Room team: Armarouge, Indeedee-F, Kingambit, Drampa, Mudsdale. Alakazam adds a fast mode. **Speed Swap** turns Drampa or Mudsdale into fast sweepers outside Trick Room. **Future Sight** guarantees late-game damage against obvious Protects or a final Pokemon with no Dark type. Expanding Force is the main attack. A niche trick: un-mega'd Alakazam (Inner Focus, so no Fake Out flinch) Speed Swaps turn 1, then mega evolving resets its own speed back to fast.

Leads: **Alakazam + Indeedee** spams Expanding Force into teams without Wide Guard, Dark types or Rillaboom, then sets Trick Room as Alakazam falls. Cybertron calls this the beginner-level mode. **Alakazam + Mudsdale** (Indeedee in back) Speed Swaps onto Mudsdale so it KOs before the opponent moves. Heavy Slam threatens fairies like Mega Floette. Into fast or Alakazam-proof teams, drop Alakazam and lead **Indeedee + Armarouge**: Follow Me / Wide Guard into Trick Room, closing with Kingambit, Drampa (into weather) or Mudsdale. Drampa, Kingambit and Armarouge have no Protect, so a Speed Swapped partner can still be doubled and KO'd.

Weaknesses: **Wide Guard** (Pelipper, Armarouge, Gallade, Aerodactyl) blanks Expanding Force, Hyper Voice and Rock Slide. A **Tyranitar + Excadrill** lead outspeeds and threatens the Alakazam / Indeedee / Armarouge leads. Dark types wall Alakazam completely, and Choice Scarf physical attackers outspeed and OHKO it.
