## Strategy

Mega Sceptile is a little overlooked, but it's especially powerful paired with a Discharge user, since Sceptile's Lightning Rod ability turns any Electric attack aimed near it into a free Special Attack boost. This team pairs it with Choice Scarf Rotom-Heat for exactly that purpose — Discharge both boosts Sceptile and chips the opponent, after which Sceptile can clean up with Grass Knot (strong into Water-types like Basculegion) or Earth Power (into Mega Raichu-Y).

Mega Floette-Eternal is the team's secondary mega option (swap for Sylveon if Floette isn't available), and gives Sceptile multiple ways to deal with Mega Staraptor, which otherwise causes it real trouble. Life Orb Garchomp and Focus Sash Aerodactyl round out the team; Aerodactyl in particular helps against Mega Charizard, since even a +1 boosted Sceptile can't reliably one-shot it. Note this Aerodactyl doesn't run Protect, so it's more vulnerable than usual to Fake Out.

Spreads are almost entirely max/max across the team. The one exception is Basculegion, which invests 32 points into Defense (paired with Sitrus Berry) specifically so it can tank hits from Pokemon like Garchomp and Kingambit that opponents might not expect it to survive.
