## Strategy

This is the rain team Louis Fontvieille and Francesco Pio Pero took to 1st and top 4 at Nice Regionals. This paste is DaniVGC's top 64 Gdansk version. The archetype pairs **Mega Raichu X** with **Archaludon** and **Mega Golisopod**, and it is still the premier rain team. Raichu enables both Sneasler and Archaludon. **Raichu + Sneasler** leads are Fake Out + Swords Dance, with the Electric Seed Unburden boost from Raichu's terrain, then **Feint** into Close Combat on turn 2. Alternatively, Raichu self-Volt Switches or Feints its own Archaludon for an instant **Stamina** boost, and the terrain powers Electro Shot.

Archaludon runs the now-meta **Snarl**, which is strong into Archaludon mirrors, psyspam and Gholdengo. Pelipper runs Helping Hand + Wide Guard, with no Tailwind or Protect. Golisopod has no Swords Dance and just attacks. Sneasler uses **Poison Jab over Dire Claw**: Electric Terrain blocks Dire Claw's sleep, and paralysis barely helps, so poison odds matter more. Basculegion-F rounds it out with super-effective coverage.
