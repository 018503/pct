## Strategy

Solo Mega Staraptor team built around raw aggression. Staraptor's main weakness is fairy types, so the team stacks multiple fairy answers: Metal Coat Scizor with Bullet Punch priority, Life Orb Sneasler with Poison Jab (Poison Touch can KO through Focus Sash), and Torkoal resisting fairy damage.

Two distinct offensive modes: Sneasler + Staraptor for fast-paced early game, and Farigiraf + Torkoal for Trick Room. Basculegion and Scizor are flexible — high damage with priority, fitting either mode. The same creator (Yanagi) also reached Rank #1 during the season with Special Mega Garchomp Sand.

Key speed benchmark: both Sneasler (170) and Staraptor (170) are trained to outspeed max speed Jolly Garchomp (156) — but neither is max speed. Staraptor gives up 7 speed points for HP bulk, and Sneasler is Adamant over Jolly to maximize damage (Life Orb Adamant Dire Claw OHKOs bulky fairies that train to survive Jolly variants).
