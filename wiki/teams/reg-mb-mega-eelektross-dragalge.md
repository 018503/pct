## Strategy

Primarily a Mega Eelektross team (Mega Dragalge is a secondary option that can take over as the main attacker if preferred), built around making Eelektross extremely hard to force out. Grimmsnarl provides Light Clay screens and Parting Shot, Incineroar brings Intimidate plus its own Parting Shot, and Sinistcha offers Hospitality plus Life Dew healing — together forming a defensive core that lets Eelektross set up and swing games. Eelektross racks up Special Attack boosts for every knockout it lands, including ones scored by Discharge against its own low-HP teammates — for example, if a Focus Sash Hydreigon has already been brought down to 1 HP, finishing it off with Discharge deliberately triggers a boost on the way out.

Most of the team's offense comes from Eelektross and Focus Sash Hydreigon: Draco Meteor threatens Garchomp, Earth Power hits Incineroar and Sneasler, and Snarl pressures common special attackers. If Dragalge is run instead, it adds real special damage of its own, but the surrounding core stays defensively oriented either way — Sinistcha, Grimmsnarl, and Incineroar are all built to soak hits rather than attack.

Bulk is the theme across the whole team: Sinistcha runs close to max HP and Special Defense, Grimmsnarl splits its points between Defense and Special Defense, and Incineroar mixes bulk with a little Speed specifically to win the race against opposing Incineroar for Parting Shot — one of the most disruptive ways to shut Eelektross down.
