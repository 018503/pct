## Strategy

This is James Baek's recreation of Mitchio Kelch's dual-mega team, which won the first Victory Road online tournament of Regulation MC (the tournament winner's original build is credited via the linked post; Rental Code is N/A). Raichu Y and Staraptor both mega evolve, but usually only one is brought as the mega, and often both are brought: Raichu's Lightning Rod shields Staraptor from Electric moves (e.g. Archaludon), and Staraptor's Intimidate can still be used before Raichu mega evolves.

Speed control comes from three sources: Staraptor's Tailwind, Raichu's Zap Cannon paralysis, and Fake Out from both Raichu and Rillaboom. That speed control feeds Gholdengo, which can set up Nasty Plot behind double Fake Out and then fire boosted Make It Rain or Shadow Ball, and it lets Hisuian Arcanine, Sylveon and Gholdengo outspeed faster threats such as Mega Salamence. Benchmarks called out: Raichu outspeeds base-120s (Mega Salamence, Mega Froslass); Gholdengo's max Speed outspeeds Basculegion and Modest Charizard and speed-ties the mirror; Staraptor's max Speed outspeeds Palafin, Timid Sneasler and Jolly Garchomp; Sylveon outspeeds base-120s after Tailwind or paralysis.

Suggested leads are one Fake Out user plus Gholdengo for immediate damage or a Nasty Plot, or Staraptor for Tailwind. In the featured game vs a King Gambit / Mega Salamence / Rillaboom / Arcanine / Sneasler / Gholdengo team, James leads Raichu and Hisuian Arcanine and double-swaps to Staraptor and Gholdengo for Tailwind plus a free Nasty Plot. The game plan is to preserve the Focus Sash Arcanine and Raichu for the late game. Steel-heavy teams and Hisuian Arcanine are the threats he respects most; Sylveon is often the weakest pick in matchups where Gholdengo and Arcanine are the main threats.

| SpD | 80 | 0 | 100 |
| Spe | 130 | 23 | 190 |

**Moves:** Zap Cannon · Focus Blast · Fake Out · Protect

**Role:** Fake Out + paralysis speed control

Raichu Y's No Guard means Zap Cannon never misses and always paralyzes, giving speed control that lets Arcanine, Sylveon and Gholdengo outspeed faster opponents. Lightning Rod before mega evolving draws Electric moves and boosts SpA. The spread takes enough Speed to outspeed base-120s (Mega Salamence, Mega Froslass), HP and Def to take a Knock Off-style hit like Kowtow Cleave from boosted King Gambit, and 2 SpA points guarantee a KO on max-HP Charizard and some Gambits holding Chople Berry. Focus Blast covers Steel types, which the team otherwise struggles with.

**Moves:** Wood Hammer · Grassy Glide · High Horsepower · Fake Out

**Role:** Grassy Terrain + Fake Out support

Rillaboom provides the second Fake Out, which enables Gholdengo's Nasty Plot, and Grassy Terrain boosts Wood Hammer and Grassy Glide. High Horsepower hits Gholdengo, Hisuian Arcanine, Incineroar and King Gambit. The bulky spread (max HP, 19 SpD) survives strong Fairy attacks such as Moonblast or Hyper Voice, which matters in the Psychic Terrain matchup, and 10 Speed lets it speed-tie or outspeed some megas under Tailwind or paralysis. James notes it does little other than Fake Out support in some matchups, so it is often left in the back.

**Moves:** Head Smash · Flare Blitz · Extreme Speed · Protect

**Role:** Focus Sash offensive threat

Max Attack, max Speed, Rock Head (no recoil, so the Sash stays intact) and Focus Sash make this a very dangerous, hard-to-remove attacker. Head Smash and Flare Blitz threaten Gholdengo, Rillaboom, Incineroar and Charizard, and Extreme Speed gives priority. James calls it a generally good lead against most teams because of the fast Fake Out pressure alongside it, and one of the two Pokemon he most wants to preserve for the late game.

**Moves:** Hyper Voice · Hyper Beam · Quick Attack · Detect

**Role:** Tailwind / paralysis spread damage

Pixilate turns Hyper Voice into a Fairy-type spread move and Hyper Beam into a huge single hit, boosted by Fairy Feather. The spread outspeeds base-120s after Tailwind or Zap Cannon paralysis, has 20 SpA for damage and 23 Def to survive Dire Claw from some Sneasler. Its job is to deal big damage under Tailwind and to handle Garchomp, which is otherwise hard to hit. James thinks it is not a good pick against teams full of Gholdengo, Hisuian Arcanine and King Gambit.

**Moves:** Make It Rain · Shadow Ball · Nasty Plot · Protect

**Role:** Nasty Plot win-con

Good as Gold blocks status moves. Double Fake Out gives a free Nasty Plot, after which +2 Make It Rain (a spread Steel move despite the SpA drop) or Shadow Ball with Life Orb deals huge damage. James also protects it or switches to Arcanine when a speed tie could go wrong.

| SpD | 90 | 4 | 114 |

**Moves:** Brave Bird · Close Combat · Tailwind · Protect

**Role:** Tailwind + Intimidate lead

Staraptor is the second mega: Intimidate before mega evolving, then Contrary, so Close Combat raises Defense and Special Defense instead of lowering them. Max Speed outspeeds Palafin, Timid Sneasler and Jolly Garchomp; 29 HP compensates for having no recovery. Brave Bird is its main damage, and Tailwind is often the first move. It pairs with Raichu so that Lightning Rod protects it from Electric attacks (Archaludon), and it can Tailwind first or set up defensive boosts with Close Combat.
