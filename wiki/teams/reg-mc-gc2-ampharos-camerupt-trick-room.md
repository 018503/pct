## Strategy

Mega Ampharos almost never sees play, but this hard Trick Room team did well in Global Challenge II. Two slow megas sit behind a single setter, Farigiraf. **Mega Ampharos** gains Dragon typing and **Mold Breaker**, so Thunderbolt and Volt Switch ignore Lightning Rod on the popular Raichu. **Mega Camerupt** gets **Sheer Force** Heat Wave / Earth Power / Ancient Power, which blows through Kingambit, Rillaboom and non-specially-defensive Charizard Y. Bring whichever mega the matchup needs, or both, plus Life Orb Primarina for Water / Fairy coverage.

Farigiraf is usually the lead, next to a Fake Out user (Incineroar or Rillaboom) or a big attacker used as bait. Protect Farigiraf when the opponent will double it, and take a KO with the partner. Eject Button Rillaboom gives a key line: Protect Farigiraf, attack, then bring Rillaboom back so the Eject Button pulls Farigiraf in again on the next hit. Keep the Fake Out users alive. A final-turn Fake Out or Volt Switch pivot sets up a second Trick Room.
