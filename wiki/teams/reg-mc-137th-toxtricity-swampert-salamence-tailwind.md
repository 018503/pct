## Strategy

Placed 137th with a 31-8 record in the Global Challenge. Tailwind from Mega Salamence and Talonflame supports a very offensive core of Low-Key Toxtricity (Punk Rock Overdrive and Snarl), Kommo-o and a Choice Scarf Sturdy Archaludon. Regular Swampert is the odd defensive pick, with Damp, Earthquake, Ice Beam, Wide Guard and Roar.

Toxtricity's Electric/Poison typing resists Raichu and avoids paralysis; Punk Rock boosts its sound moves (Overdrive, Snarl) by 30%. Swampert's Wide Guard blocks Hyper Voice and Expanding Force, Ice Beam threatens Garchomp and Salamence, and Roar can disrupt Trick Room or pull out a setup threat. Talonflame's Quick Guard and big Defense live Sucker Punch and let it Flare Blitz back at Gholdengo and Mega Golisopod.

Kommo-o with Life Orb Vacuum Wave handles Kingambit, and Archaludon is the Choice Scarf nuke that outspeeds Z Megas and Mega Aerodactyl. The team does not like Trick Room, which Swampert's Roar helps with.
