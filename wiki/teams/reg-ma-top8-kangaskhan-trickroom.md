## Strategy

This top 8 team is a unique spin on Trick Room, featuring Kangaskhan alongside Charizard as dual megas. This Kangaskhan is unusual in that it carries no Speed investment and a Brave nature paired with Hammer Arm — already slower than normal, and Hammer Arm's Speed drop pushes it even further under the Trick Room threshold. Rather than moves like Low Kick, Drain Punch, or Dark-type coverage like Crunch/Sucker Punch, this set runs Ice Punch and Hammer Arm. Kangaskhan becomes an effective lead alongside the team's primary Trick Room setter, Farigiraf: lead Kangaskhan and Farigiraf, Fake Out into Trick Room, and play from there.

Charizard gives the team a sun mode and can also function under Trick Room, or entirely outside of it — lead Sneasler and Charizard, pressure immediately with Fake Out into mega evolution and Heat Wave. Primarina with Substitute is difficult for opponents to deal with, spamming Hyper Voice under Trick Room to great effect. That gives the team a Trick Room core (Kangaskhan, Farigiraf, Torkoal, Primarina) and a faster mode (leading Sneasler plus Charizard) — the Sneasler here runs U-turn for immediate pivoting, and can also break Focus Sash targets for super-effective damage.

Kangaskhan has been nerfed significantly since its introduction to competitive VGC, but it's still in a solid spot in the current metagame — not usually run solo, but an excellent option on dual mega teams where it can soak up damage and retaliate with knockouts. If minimum-Speed Kangaskhan and Primarina under Trick Room appeal to you, this team is worth trying.
