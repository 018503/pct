## Strategy

The Floette + Salamence duo was hyped at the start of Reg MC. Salamence is in a good spot again, and Federico Camporesi just won an online grassroots tournament with this exact team. It was popularised by former Japanese national champion **Spy Anya**. The plan is **triple Fake Out** (Incineroar, Rillaboom, and a Fake Out White Herb Sneasler instead of Throat Chop / Rock Slide) to buy **Calm Mind** turns for **Mega Floette**. **Draining Kiss** plus Grassy Terrain heals back everything it took. Helping Hand Incineroar adds even more damage. This Floette runs Moonblast + Draining Kiss, with no Dazzling Gleam.

**Salamence** is the secondary mega. It can also come along un-mega'd next to Floette, just for Intimidate, Tailwind and Draco Meteor. Under Salamence's Tailwind, the 8-Spe Rillaboom outspeeds everything without its own speed control. Focus Sash Basculegion rounds out the team. Keeping Floette's HP up is the whole game.
