## Strategy

Washy's dual mega Froslass/Dragonite has been one of the most consistent high-elo teams across multiple seasons — finishing 4th in the most recent season, 5th the season before, and 13th in Reg MA Season 2. Despite these results, it flew under the radar until this dedicated video.

The core pairing covers each other's weaknesses: Froslass-Mega handles rain teams with Aurora Veil and Blizzard, while Dragonite-Mega is nearly impossible to OHKO thanks to Multiscale (active both before and after mega evolving). Dragon Pulse and Heat Wave on Dragonite cover dragons and steels, while Fairy-type threats are handled by Sneasler (Dire Claw), Garchomp (Poison Jab), and Kingambit (Iron Head).

Feint on Sneasler is a standout best-of-one tech — it breaks Protect, enabling Dragonite to OHKO Whimsicott with Heat Wave on turn one for an immediate 4-3 advantage before Tailwind ever goes up. Unburden + Focus Sash means Sneasler hits 378 speed after sash breaks. The team's deceptive survivability (Multiscale, Sash, Chople, Roseli) makes it incredibly hard to trade into favorably.
