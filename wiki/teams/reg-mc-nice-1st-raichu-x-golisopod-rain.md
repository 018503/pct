## Strategy

Louis Fontvieille won the Nice Regional Championships with this rain team, and Francesco Pio Perro made top 4 with the same six. The concept came from a group of Japanese players at the first Global Challenge, who ran Mega Swampert and a different Sneasler set. The Raichu X adaptation first appeared at Louisville. Cybertron's paste is a recreation; the official rental is on Francesco's Patreon. Rillaboom, Indeedee and the terrain seeds make MC Mega Raichu X's best format so far. Raichu X sets Electric Terrain for Electric Seed Sneasler and boosts Archaludon's Electro Shot.

The modes are: **Raichu X + Sneasler**, which goes Fake Out + Swords Dance on turn 1 and then uses Feint to break Protects for +2 Close Combat/Poison Jab KOs, then closes with rain. Avoid it into Gholdengo or teams with clear Fighting/Poison resists. **Raichu + Archaludon** uses Volt Switch into Archaludon for a Stamina boost, pivots to Pelipper and fires Electro Shot. The **full rain mode** is Pelipper, Archaludon, Basculegion and Golisopod into teams with no weather. **Raichu + Basculegion** goes into weather teams. Volt Switch first to bait the opposing mega (Charizard Y, Tyranitar), then bring Pelipper in afterwards so you win the weather war. Into opposing rain you can leave Pelipper at home and bring both Basculegion and Archaludon.

Watch-outs: Charizard Y that holds its mega until after Pelipper comes in. The Baltimore sand team (Mega Tyranitar, Flamethrower Salamence for Golisopod, Excadrill for Raichu). The mirror, where Basculegion speed ties and faster Raichu/Archaludon matter. Ground types, since Raichu has no Protect. Cybertron suggests speed-creeping Archaludon, Raichu, Sneasler and Pelipper now that the mirror is more common.

**Moves:** Muddy Water · Shadow Ball · Ice Beam · Protect

**Role:** Swift Swim special sweeper

The female form gets better special coverage: Muddy Water for consistent Water damage, Ice Beam for Garchomp, and Shadow Ball for opposing Basculegion and Mega Gengar. Once Pelipper sets rain, it can OHKO something like Garchomp before it moves. Winning the Basculegion speed tie decides a lot of mirror games.

It is close to max HP / max Atk, with 2 Spe points to edge out opposing Mega Golisopod. Rain removes its 4x Fire weakness, so it patches the Fairy matchups.

The 11 Spe points (116) let it move first in Archaludon mirrors, so it wins Electro Shot / Dragon Pulse trades at low HP and then heals with Leftovers. Volt Switch and Feint from Raichu help stack Stamina boosts.

It has no Protect: the set is Fake Out, Volt Switch, Volt Tackle and Feint. Its Speed outpaces neutral max base 120s (Sneasler, Froslass). The HP/Def survives Scarf Adamant Garchomp Earthquake or Stomping Tantrum 15/16 times. Feint into a protecting target lets Sneasler land +2 KOs. Pre-mega Lightning Rod can absorb Electric attacks, and it hates Ground types.
