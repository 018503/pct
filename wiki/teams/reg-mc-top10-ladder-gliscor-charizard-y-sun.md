## Strategy

Sahil (shaikhvgc786) reached the top 10 of the Champions ladder late in the season with this psychic spam / sun team. Gliscor is the standout. Its Psychic Seed gives +1 Special Defense in Psychic Terrain to cover its weak special side, and consuming the seed doubles Acrobatics to 110 BP. Hyper Cutter blocks Intimidate and Parting Shot drops. It sets Tailwind and outspeeds Adamant Excadrill.

Charizard Y and Wide Lens Venusaur are the sun core. Venusaur's 143 Speed is James's guess at the reasoning: with Chlorophyll it still outspeeds base 120s like Mega Salamence after one Icy Wind, Electroweb or Rock Tomb drop (e.g. a Rock Tomb Sneasler + Salamence lead). Charizard's HP / Special Defense spread lives Zap Cannon from uninvested Mega Raichu and Rock Slide from Sneasler and Garchomp. Indeedee's Imprison locks out opposing Trick Room and Expanding Force.

Leads: Gliscor + Indeedee (Tailwind + Expanding Force) when the opponent has no Rillaboom; Annihilape + Venusaur (Sleep Powder, with Annihilape scaring Incineroar, Charizard and Salamence); Gliscor + Annihilape; Charizard + Venusaur or Charizard + Gliscor; and Imprison Indeedee into Trick Room. Kingambit usually stays in the back to clean up late game.
