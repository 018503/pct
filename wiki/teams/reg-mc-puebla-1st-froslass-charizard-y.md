## Strategy

Kyle Livinghouse won the second LATAM Regional of the season (Puebla) with this team. It is the first major title for Mega Froslass, which had made top cut many times before without winning. The core of Whimsicott, Charizard, Kingambit and Garchomp is well known from Reg MA and MB, where it ran Basculegion and Mega Floette. The MC version has White Herb Sneasler in Floette's slot and adds Mega Froslass. You normally bring only one of the two megas. Froslass covers the matchups Charizard dislikes, mainly Mega Salamence and Mega Staraptor.

Cybertron calls it one of the most flexible teams in the format. Kyle used about nine different combinations of four on stream. Suggested modes: **Sneasler + Froslass** into Salamence/Staraptor teams, where Sneasler eats Intimidate to trigger White Herb and Unburden while Froslass spams Blizzard and sets Aurora Veil as it goes down. **Garchomp + Charizard or Froslass** for immediate spread pressure (Heat Wave/Blizzard + Earthquake), with Kingambit and one of Whimsicott/Sneasler behind. **Whimsicott + Sneasler** into opposing Tailwind teams, where you match their Tailwind and an Unburden Sneasler is the fastest thing on the field. **Kingambit + Charizard** into Trick Room: Heat Wave plus Kowtow Cleave removes Farigiraf before it can set Trick Room.

Spread benchmarks: Garchomp's HP points mean Choice Scarf Adamant 32 Garchomp's Dragon Claw never OHKOs without a crit. Kingambit's Defense means it always survives Adamant Sneasler Close Combat with 23 Atk points or fewer (about 30% to OHKO from 32). Sneasler outspeeds Jolly max Speed Garchomp. Charizard outspeeds neutral max base 80s like Basculegion and Venusaur (but not Venusaur in sun), and its HP/Def survives Jolly Life Orb Garchomp Rock Slide about two thirds of the time. Watch-outs: Trick Room teams with an end-game Garchomp Z (one of Kyle's two losses), inaccurate moves (Rock Slide, Heat Wave, Blizzard outside snow), Wide Guard against Garchomp, and picking the wrong mega in closed team sheet Bo1 into teams like Golisopod rain.

| SpD | 80 | 0 | 100 |
| Spe | 120 | 30 | 170 |

**Moves:** Close Combat · Dire Claw · Rock Tomb · Protect

**Role:** Fast physical attacker + Rock Tomb speed control

White Herb instead of a seed means Sneasler gets Unburden after eating an Intimidate drop. That makes it a strong lead into Mega Salamence and Incineroar. Rock Tomb hits opposing Charizard Y and Volcarona and lowers Speed so Charizard and Garchomp can move first. Its 30 Spe points outspeed Jolly max Speed Garchomp, and the 20 Atk points hit the 187 Atk jump. It is walled by Ghost types and doesn't like Psychic or Ground damage.

**Moves:** Protect · Encore · Moonblast · Tailwind

**Role:** Prankster Tailwind support

This is a standard max SpA / max Spe Sash set with Encore and Moonblast. Bring it when you need speed control against faster or Choice Scarf teams, or to match an opposing Tailwind. It is weak into Trick Room and Farigiraf, because Armor Tail blocks Encore and Tailwind does little there. Dual Wingbeat is a good way for opponents to break its Sash.

**Moves:** Weather Ball · Protect · Heat Wave · Solar Beam

**Role:** Default mega — spread Heat Wave + Solar Beam

Solar Beam replaces the Ancient Power that was common around Worlds, for better coverage into Water types like Basculegion. It is the most common lead, alongside Garchomp or Kingambit. Its Speed outruns neutral max base 80s, and the rest goes into HP and Def to take physical hits like Garchomp's Rock Slide. Because this is not the HP/SpD-heavy Charizard spread, Mega Raichu Y's Zap Cannon is a real threat.

**Moves:** Blizzard · Aurora Veil · Shadow Ball · Protect

**Role:** Second mega — Blizzard + Aurora Veil

Mega Froslass with max HP and Speed is the answer to Mega Salamence and Mega Staraptor teams. It deals heavy Blizzard damage, then sets Aurora Veil as it is about to faint so Garchomp and Kingambit become hard to KO. It struggles against Ice resists (Incineroar, Kingambit) and against opposing weather that makes Blizzard inaccurate and stops Veil.
