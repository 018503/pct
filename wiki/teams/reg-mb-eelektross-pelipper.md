## Strategy

Mega Eelektross is one of the most interesting new Mega Evolutions because of its ability and wide coverage. I wanted to find a way to start getting boosts for it immediatly and built a team around combinations like Scarf Pelipper with Soak and Discharge with Electric immunes!
