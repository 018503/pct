## Strategy

Carson St. Denis re-established **Whimsicott + Mega Charizard Y** as a top threat and made top 16 at Louisville Regionals with this. In earlier regs the archetype ran Mega Floette in this slot. **Sneasler** is the new piece. It adds burst damage, plus Rock Tomb speed control that also hits Volcarona and Charizard Y. Kingambit has four attacks with Chople Berry: Low Kick for Incineroar / Kingambit / Tyranitar, Iron Head for Sylveon / Floette, and both Dark moves. Charizard is back on **Solar Beam** over Ancient Power, because Charizard mirrors dropped off in early Reg MC and Solar Beam punishes Milotic and Basculegion.

This is the first of three versions in the video. The others are the Puebla-winning Mega Froslass version and Michael Kelsch's Mega Salamence version.
