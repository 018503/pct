## Strategy

James Baek's own take on Mega Salamence built around an under-the-radar buff: Slash went from 70 to 80 base power with a high crit ratio. Dragon Cheer gives +2 crit stages to a Dragon-type ally (+1 to everything else), which stacks with Slash's innate +1 for a guaranteed critical hit. That crit ignores Intimidate drops and defence boosts (Archaludon, Mega Gyarados, Skarmory-style walls) and has no recoil, unlike Double-Edge.

Salazzle (Oblivious blocks Intimidate and Taunt, Focus Sash) and Milotic (Competitive punishes Intimidate) both carry Dragon Cheer and Fake Out/Icy Wind speed control. Gholdengo is the second win condition via Nasty Plot, Rillaboom brings Grassy Terrain and Fake Out, and a Mega-capable Tyranitar covers Charizard, Kingambit and Basculegion with sand and Knock Off/Low Kick. Max-Speed Salamence and Gholdengo are deliberate, and the slower Salazzle and Milotic builds are meant to outspeed the Scarf and Whimsicott targets they care about.

Matchups: the team beats Intimidate spam and Incineroar-style cycling, Basculegion and sand/Tailwind offense; Rain with Sucker Punch Golisopod and Archaludon is the hard matchup, where Gholdengo (+4 Shadow Ball range) and Salazzle Fake Out are the key tools. In the test games the Dragon Cheer + Slash line deleted Kommo-o, Basculegion and Whimsicott lines quickly, and the Tyranitar + Salamence lead handled Whimsicott/Charizard Tailwind.
