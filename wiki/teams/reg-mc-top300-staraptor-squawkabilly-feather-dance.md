## Strategy

A Global Challenge Top 300 team built around Choice Scarf Squawkabilly and Mega Staraptor. Squawkabilly gives double Intimidate with Staraptor, and its Feather Dance combines with Staraptor's mega ability Contrary: lowering Attack by two stages instead raises Attack by two. Parting Shot and Feather Dance set up Staraptor for Close Combat that also raises its defenses.

Weavile (Pickpocket, Ice Spinner to remove Psychic Terrain which scares Staraptor) supplies Fake Out and Taunt, Garchomp Rock Slide covers Charizard, Nasty Plot Gholdengo breaks Floette-Eternal and walls, and Rotom-Wash is an opener with Electroweb and Light Screen for the Staraptor setup (James accidentally ran Protect instead of Light Screen in the video).

The team revolves around getting Staraptor or Gholdengo into safe setup positions; Squawkabilly's Intimidate, Final Gambit for surprise KOs on Raichu, and Foul Play for Gholdengo round it out.
