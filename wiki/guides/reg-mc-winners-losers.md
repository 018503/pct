# Regulation MC Winners and Losers

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Source | [Moxie Boosted — The Winners and Losers of Regulation M-C](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TMR1JauPqzU) |
| Date | 2026-09-16 |
| Regulation | MC |

Moxie Boosted's meta analysis of Pokemon that have been effectively buffed or nerfed by the Regulation MC additions. These aren't Pokemon that became bad — they're still top-tier — but the new arrivals shifted their matchup profiles meaningfully.

## Winners

### Gholdengo

Benefits from multiple MC additions, especially Rillaboom and Mega Salamence.

- **Rillaboom synergy:** Rillaboom can't hit Gholdengo for real damage (unless running High Horsepower, which isn't standard). Gholdengo can Nasty Plot safely next to Rillaboom's Fake Out. Grassy Surge gives passive recovery and halves Earthquake damage — huge for Gholdengo's Garchomp matchup.
- **Salamence matchup:** Mega Salamence needs a coverage move (Flamethrower/Heat Wave) to threaten Gholdengo. Without it, Draco Meteor does negligible damage into Gholdengo, and Aerilate Hyper Voice (Flying-type) hits but weakly.
- **Salamence pairing:** Lead Gholdengo + Salamence lets Salamence set Tailwind while Gholdengo bodyguards by removing Fairy-types, Rock-types (Tyranitar), and other threats.

### Milotic

Competitive ability thrives in a format with more Intimidate users.

- **More Intimidate targets:** Incineroar is more common (needed to handle Mega Golisopod's 4x fire weakness), and Mega Salamence brings another Intimidate. Each Intimidate triggers Competitive for +2 SpA.
- **Key KOs:** +2 Scald/Muddy Water one-shots certain Incineroar builds. +2 Ice Beam one-shots Mega Salamence.
- **Garchomp-Z check:** Strong special bulk lets Milotic tank Draco Meteor from Garchomp-Z and fire back with good damage.
- **Rillaboom coexistence:** While Rillaboom threatens Milotic, Milotic benefits more from its existence than not — Grassy Terrain recovery stacks with Leftovers, and Rillaboom can bodyguard for it.

### Sneasler

Already top-tier, but seed access pushed it further.

- **Seed sets:** With Indeedee and Rillaboom added, Sneasler gets access to Psychic Seed and Grassy Seed. These proc Unburden (instant double speed) while boosting a defense stat, making it bulkier than Focus Sash/White Herb sets.
- **Psychic Seed set:** Protect / Dire Claw / Close Combat / Throat Chop (for Basculegion) or Rock Slide (for Charizard). Can run Adamant since Unburden handles speed.
- **Grassy Seed set:** Next to Rillaboom, can drop Fake Out for Swords Dance. Invest in HP (176) for bulk; becomes genuinely hard to one-shot. Swords Dance + Dire Claw + Close Combat is devastating.
- **Coaching support:** Can click Coaching to boost physical attackers like Mega Golisopod or Mega Salamence.
- **Rillaboom counter:** Effective into opposing Rillaboom, which are everywhere.

### Volcarona

Sleeper pick benefiting from the Grassy Seed + Rillaboom ecosystem.

- **Grassy Seed defense boost:** Next to Rillaboom, gets a free Defense boost, making Quiver Dance setup much safer. Rillaboom also defends Volcarona from Water-types like Basculegion.
- **Flame Body redirector:** Decent Rage Powder user. When redirecting physical moves from Metagross, Sneasler, or Mega Golisopod, Flame Body has a chance to burn them.
- **Post-Quiver Dance threat:** Heat Wave hits hard after a boost. Not enough Rock-type moves in the format to make Volcarona's 4x weakness crippling.
- **Moxie's take:** "Slept on right now... going to pick up over time and probably be a really strong part of the metagame."

### Mega Metagross

Terrain-dependent powerhouse with multiple terrain partners now available.

- **Psychic Terrain (Indeedee):** Can't be Sucker Punched by Kingambit or hit by any priority. Psychic Fangs gets Tough Claws boost (1.3x) AND Psychic Terrain boost — massive damage.
- **Steel Roller tech:** 130 BP move that removes terrain. After Tough Claws: 130 x 1.3 = effectively 169 BP. Nearly one-shots Rillaboom (43% chance to OHKO max HP Rillaboom). Mega Charizard-Y takes 67% if not bulky; KO'd by Psychic Fangs afterward.
- **Grassy Terrain (Rillaboom):** Passive HP recovery, plus Steel Roller can remove the opposing terrain.
- **Terrain uptime:** Terrains are on nearly every team now — Steel Roller almost always has a target to activate on.
- **Speed advantage:** Base speed is significantly faster than Charizard (10 points higher). Outspeeds Modest Charizard-Y when running Adamant, then one-shots non-bulky sets with Psychic Fangs in Psychic Terrain.

### Hisuian Arcanine

Improved matchup spread across the new additions.

- **Coverage:** Head Smash and Rock Slide hit Gholdengo, Volcarona, Charizard, and Mega Salamence. Flare Blitz handles Rillaboom and Mega Golisopod.
- **Rock Head + Focus Sash:** The standard set. Focus Sash makes it an incredible revenge KO tool. Rock Head eliminates Head Smash recoil.
- **Head Smash calcs:** Nothing switches in safely. Even max HP/max Def Milotic gets 2HKO'd by Jolly Head Smash. 80% accuracy is the only downside.
- **Format position:** Covers Golisopod (Fire) and Charizard (Rock) simultaneously — excellent role compression.

## Losers

### Basculegion

Rillaboom is the primary problem.

- **Grassy Glide KO:** Miracle Seed Adamant 32 Atk Rillaboom's Grassy Glide one-shots Choice Scarf Adaptability Basculegion. Even max HP Basculegion is a likely KO roll (heavily in Rillaboom's favor). Grassy Glide has priority in Grassy Terrain, so Scarf speed doesn't help.
- **Still top 10 in usage** — just harder to use freely when Rillaboom is on half the teams. Needs partners like Farigiraf (priority block) or Indeedee (Psychic Terrain blocks Grassy Glide's priority).

### Garchomp

Outclassed and directly nerfed by Grassy Terrain.

- **Earthquake nerfed:** Grassy Terrain halves Earthquake damage. Can't spam it freely with Rillaboom everywhere.
- **Outclassed by other dragons:** Mega Salamence, Garchomp-Z, and even base Dragonite compete for the dragon slot. Ladder is mostly Garchomp-Z (partly new-toy syndrome, but also genuinely strong).
- **Hostile winners:** Milotic (Ice Beam), Volcarona (bulk + burn threat), and the general rise of bulky Water-types all pressure Garchomp.
- **Charizard teams shifting:** Standard Charizard teams may start phasing out Garchomp for an alternative dragon, possibly dual mega with Salamence (though sand matchup gets awkward).

### Farigiraf

Competition from Indeedee and a hard new counter in Mega Golisopod.

- **Mega Golisopod matchup:** Golisopod's Leech Life (no boosting item, no boosts) rolls to one-shot even max bulk Farigiraf. At 32/32 defenses with Relaxed nature, it's still a KO roll. Farigiraf is forced to run Psychic Noise (75 BP, blocks healing for 2 turns) just to stop Golisopod from healing through it — a suboptimal move slot.
- **Indeedee competition:** Indeedee also blocks priority (Psychic Surge), has Follow Me, Helping Hand, AND Trick Room. Unless the team specifically needs a different terrain, Indeedee may be the better support pick.
- **Still good** when the team wants Armor Tail over terrain, but the floor on Farigiraf dropped.

### Kingambit

Multiple new threats that it can't trade favorably with.

- **Sneasler matchup:** Gets one-shot by Close Combat. Sneasler is trending toward top 3-5 usage.
- **Mega Golisopod matchup:** Life Orb Kingambit's Kowtow Cleave does 36-43% to Golisopod. Golisopod's Leech Life does 44-53% back while healing. After Swords Dance, Golisopod rolls to one-shot Kingambit. Kingambit loses the 1v1 convincingly.
- **Volcarona matchup:** +1 Defense (Grassy Seed) Volcarona takes Kowtow Cleave as a 3HKO at best thanks to Grassy Terrain recovery, while threatening a Flame Body burn on every contact and a Quiver Dance-boosted Heat Wave one-shot.
- **Forced bulky:** Many Kingambit now run max HP just to survive the Golisopod exchange, sacrificing offensive investment.

### Tyranitar

Similar problems to Kingambit, plus terrain hostility.

- **Milotic everywhere:** Bulky Competitive Milotic scalding everything makes Tyranitar's life difficult.
- **Rillaboom everywhere:** Wood Hammer from Rillaboom hits extremely hard.
- **Sneasler duality:** Sand + Indeedee + Sneasler is an archetype that benefits Tyranitar, but Sneasler also threatens it in opposing compositions.
- **Sand still viable** as an archetype, but Tyranitar needs more bodyguarding than before.

### Charizard

Still probably the best mega in the format. Just 1% worse.

- **More answers exist now:**
  - Sneasler + Indeedee: Rock Slide in Psychic Terrain.
  - Mega Salamence: Resists all of Charizard's main moves, threatens Venusaur with Hyper Voice.
  - Incineroar: More common now (Golisopod matchup), can switch in on Charizard moves.
  - Hisuian Arcanine: Focus Sash Rock Slide one-shot button. Very common now.
  - Mega Metagross: Faster, Psychic Fangs one-shots non-bulky sets in Psychic Terrain.
- **Rillaboom benefits it too** — works well as a partner (defends from Water), and Heat Wave/Weather Ball nuke opposing Rillaboom.
- **Net assessment:** More counterplay exists, but Charizard's core strength (massive SpA under sun, wide coverage) keeps it firmly at the top. The nerf is real but small.
